# Stage Asynchronous Peer Feedback Before Live Workshop

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a live peer workshop is spending its limited time collecting first reactions or rediscovering issues participants could identify beforehand
**THEN** gather artifact-anchored peer critique asynchronously, consolidate the discussion, and use it to target the live workshop at deeper decisions

## Do
- Make the work available early enough for every reviewer to respond before the live session.
- Anchor critique to specific passages or regions with notes and highlights so the author can connect each comment to its trigger.
- Ask reviewers to read and engage existing comments as they add their own, turning isolated reactions into a preliminary discussion.
- Keep the cohort's feedback together so the author and facilitator can see repetition, disagreement, and emerging priorities in one place.
- Review that discussion before meeting and foreground the questions that deserve sustained live attention.

## Don't
- Do not treat the asynchronous pass as the completed workshop; it prepares the targeted conversation that follows.
- Do not scatter preliminary comments across channels that prevent the author from seeing the whole response.
- Do not spend the live session repeating every posted comment when the collected discussion already reveals the important clusters.

## Checklist
- Was the artifact available before the live workshop?
- Is each critique attached to a concrete part of the work?
- Can reviewers see and respond to one another's observations?
- Can the author access the complete peer response in one place?
- Does the live agenda address deeper priorities surfaced by the preliminary discussion?

## Notes
Starkey's online writing-workshop example assigns different jobs to the two settings. Notes and highlighting collect a visible first pass before class; the shared thread lets peers react to more than the draft alone; the in-person meeting then begins from that accumulated evidence instead of from zero. `VAR_starkey_stage_online_writing_critique_before_in_person_workshop` retains that writing-specific implementation for courses using a shared annotation space.
