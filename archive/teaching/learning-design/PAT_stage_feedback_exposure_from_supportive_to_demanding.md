# Stage Feedback Exposure From Supportive to Demanding

## Pattern Rule
**IF** learners avoid, defend against, or cannot yet use critique because the exposure or demand exceeds their trust
**THEN** begin with a supportive low-stakes feedback route and increase scrutiny as repeated use builds readiness
**ELSE** use the most demanding feedback setting the learner can process productively.

## Do
- Start with readers likely to respond gently enough that unfinished work can be shared without requiring perfection.
- Make the first response specific about intended goals, successes, and misses so support still produces revision evidence.
- Use repeated contact to turn exposure into trust and to make increasing candor predictable rather than sudden.
- Offer an individual conference when the public venue itself is the obstacle.
- Increase challenge until learners can hear demanding critique and inspect their own work with the same rigor.

## Don't
- Do not confuse supportive feedback with unqualified praise.
- Do not expose a learner to the harshest available critic merely to accelerate toughness.
- Do not keep critique permanently gentle after the learner can use sharper evidence.
- Do not assume reluctance in a public group means resistance to revision in every setting.
- Do not retain a stable cohort when its familiarity protects weak work or makes participation unsafe.

## Checklist
- Is the present barrier the content of the feedback or the exposure required to receive it?
- Does the chosen route provide specific usable evidence without overwhelming the learner?
- Has repeated response increased trust and tolerance for candor?
- Is there a clear next increase in scrutiny or audience?
- Can the learner now become a more demanding reader of their own work?

## Notes
Starkey gives beginning writers a progression from supportive readers toward more demanding critics. `VAR_starkey_move_writers_from_supportive_readers_to_demanding_critics` preserves that writing context and its final move toward self-critique. Two other routes change how readiness is built. `VAR_starkey_build_revision_trust_with_a_stable_peer_group` uses cohort continuity and repeated exposure, while `VAR_starkey_offer_a_private_revision_conference` lowers the social exposure through one-to-one response before returning to wider peer learning.
