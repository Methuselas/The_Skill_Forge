# Architecture Reset — 2026-08-15

The authoring infrastructure that had accumulated around source material was
**removed rather than improved**. This file records what went, what stayed, and
what must not grow back.

Starting commit: `8e4c21a`. Rollback tag: `pre-cleanup-2026-08-15`.

## The model restored

```text
SOURCE MATERIAL → an agent studies it → self-contained cards in ONE domain
                → PASS validates them → the universal library
```

The source is how the knowledge is learned. The finished card is the durable
artifact. Delete every research PDF and the library still validates and builds.

## Removed

**Python (9 tools, ~1,830 lines):** `provenance.py`, `source_provenance.py`,
`quality_attestation.py`, `publish_provenance.py`, `stage_source.py`,
`verify_grounding.py`, `preflight_pdf.py`, `render_pdf.py`,
`cleanup_authoring_cache.py`. Plus `PASS/tests/test_authoring_contracts.py`,
whose only purpose was exercising the ledger contract.

**Validation:** rules 13 (locator names a processed unit), 21 (ledger candidate
accounting), 24 (Teaching-lane receipts and cross-domain routes), and 25 (source
registry agreement). The `--ledger`, `--provenance`, and `--scope-ledger-to-library`
flags. `ProvenanceView` and every read of `SOURCE.md`, `UNITS.md`, and
`REGISTRY.md`.

**Release builds:** grounding-attestation and public-provenance gates, source-id
closure, source-scoped card hashing, and the `--ledger` flag. A build can no
longer fail because a source is missing, a receipt is stale, or another domain
was not inspected.

**Schema:** `reference.source_id`, `.publish_date`, `.media_type`, `.locator`,
`.evidence_type`; variant `source_id`, `source_title`, `locator`; the
`lane_fit: teaching_foundation` and `rel: teaching_foundation_for` values.

**Docs and templates:** `PASS_LEDGER.md`, `PASS_GROUNDING.md`,
`SOURCE_TEMPLATE.md`, `UNITS_TEMPLATE.md`, `UNIT_LEDGER_TEMPLATE.md`.

**Data:** `workspace/provenance/` (37 receipts). `Pillow` and `pypdfium2`
dependencies — PyYAML is now the only one.

`workspace/authoring/sources/` was untracked (18 files). No tool read it, and 15
of its 17 tracked images were byte-identical duplicates of assets already shipped
under `library/art/.../assets/`; the other 2 were referenced by no card. Keeping
it as "a record of where the shipped assets originated" would have been a
provenance record for images the library already owns. The files remain on disk as
local scratch.

**Runtime:** the `teaching` package activation block in `skillforge_runtime.py`.
The `lane` (teach/skill) resolution stays: it is card semantics, orthogonal to
execution mode, and routes nowhere.

## Migrated

All 586 cards were rewritten to drop source identity: 930 `source_id` and 930
`locator` values, 586 each of `publish_date`/`media_type`/`evidence_type`, and 344
variant `source_title` values. Zero lines were added; no skill content changed.

`reference` survives as **optional courtesy attribution** — `source_title` and
`author` only. Nothing reads it.

## Quarantined

`library/teaching/` → `archive/teaching/` (11 cards). Nothing depended on them,
and no release recipe named them; `Dynamic_Figure_Drawing.yaml` had listed the
module and no longer does.

Three dated architecture-state snapshots moved to `archive/authoring-state/`.

`archive/` is excluded from validation and builds. Nothing active may depend on it.

## Kept deliberately

**`metaskills`** remains a shared foundation bundled into every release; 14 art
and software-engineering cards link into it. It is craft-neutral process
knowledge, not a peer domain, so it is exempt from the domain-independence rule.
Severing those links would have deleted real content, which the reset forbids.

**`lane_fit: teach`** (81 non-teaching cards), **`routing_class: teaching`** (21),
and **`rel: teaches`** (117) are card-level semantics describing how a card is
applied inside its own domain. They are not lane machinery and were not touched.

**Source-dependent phrase checking** (rule 20) stayed. Banning "see page" and
"study the figure" in a card body is exactly the source-independence invariant.

**Visual-reference review sidecars** stayed. They record that a *shipped image*
was reviewed — asset quality, not research provenance. The originality comparison
against source renders was dropped: it required source payloads, and all 23
references are first-party, so it never fired.

## Added

One rule, `rule 26`: a card's `cross_links` and `foundation_object_id` must
resolve inside its own package or `metaskills`. This makes domain independence a
validated invariant rather than a convention.

`PASS/tools/build_index.py`. All 43 pre-existing `INDEX.md` files carried
`<!-- GENERATED by tools/build_index.py; do not hand-edit. -->` for a tool that
had never existed in this repo's history, so they were unmaintainable by their own
stated route and had drifted. `library/metaskills/INDEX.md` listed 1 of 3 topics
and 1 of 4 cards while being step 1 of the load order in all three skill wrappers
— an agent following the documented order saw a quarter of the metaskills.

The generator derives every index from card frontmatter (`name`, `object_type`,
`stage_binding`, `variants`, `prerequisite_for`), so an index cannot drift from
what the library holds. A directory gets one when it holds cards or has a
descendant that does; display names come from an optional `display_name` in
`MODULE.yaml` (used once, for `software-engineering/core` → "Foundations").

Regenerating produced 79 indexes: 45 written, of which 30 are new Art indexes —
Art had none at all — plus `writing/`, `software-engineering/` roots, and the two
missing metaskills topics. Two tests now guard it: `--check` must pass, and every
card must appear in the index beside it.

## Left alone on disk

Per the user's decision, no local research state was deleted. Any
`workspace/authoring/ledger/`, `renders/`, or `handoffs/` directory is now inert —
nothing reads it — and can be deleted whenever convenient.

## Verified

- 575 cards validate with no sources, no ledger, no workspace, no provenance.
- Each of `art`, `writing`, `software-engineering` validates independently.
- All three release recipes build and pass `check`; a C++ release pulls in no art,
  writing, or teaching.
- Validation output is byte-identical in the full repo and in a stripped checkout.
- 30 tests pass across `tests/` and `PASS/tests/`.

## Do not rebuild

No replacement ledger, provenance graph, authoring database, source manifest,
sidecar format, synchronization protocol, universal meta-domain, Teaching bridge,
migration framework, or mandatory session-state object.

Prefer deletion over abstraction. Prefer plain files over state machinery. Prefer
self-contained cards over reconstructed provenance.
