## Blind execution protocol

For each Drill:

1. Locate the card by `object_id`; do not rely on a remembered path.
2. Read only its frontmatter plus `Practice Task`, `Target Skill`, `Setup`, and `Instructions`.
3. Keep `Success Check`, `Common Failures`, and `Notes` closed until the generation prompt, artifact, and pre-grade observations are frozen.
4. Resolve `foundation_object_id` and any Pattern/AP owners necessary to execute the Drill. The test includes its canonical prerequisite closure.
5. Query Art Memory only after canonical routing, using three to six short action/subject cues. Record the IDs actually returned. Pay particular attention when the query returns evidence concerning hand topology, limb-chain mechanics, multi-pass leakage, locomotion/timing, or fixed-comparison control, but do not force those entries into unrelated work.
6. Immediately before generation, load `AP_prepare_artifact_only_image_generation_handoff` and compile only the current artifact contract. Do not expose hidden grading language to the generator.
7. Generate exactly one attempt. No repair, reroll, selective crop, or second candidate is allowed.
8. Inspect the original-resolution artifact and freeze the prompt plus pre-grade observations: what is successful, weak, missing, or indeterminate.
9. Reveal `Success Check`, `Common Failures`, and `Notes`. Grade every required output and every Success Check bullet from visible evidence.
10. Record separately:
    - `attempt_validity`: valid or invalid;
    - `artifact_quality`: strong, adequate, weak, or failed;
    - `process_validity`: strong, adequate, weak, or failed;
    - `skill_attribution`: strong, adequate, weak, failed, or unproven;
    - component-level results;
    - overall attempt result;
    - card result.
11. Preserve the image for teacher inspection through the host preview. Do not copy generated images into the repository, any card, Memory, or a release.

A required component or process constraint that fails makes the strict attempt FAIL. Later panels cannot retroactively validate an earlier pass whose information ceiling was exceeded. Visual polish cannot excuse missing construction, invariants, or comparative control.

An INVALID attempt means the capability was never fairly exercised—for example, the generator refused, a required input was inaccessible, the execution prompt was underspecified, or the chosen interface could not perform an indispensable physical/temporal operation. INVALID does not count for or against craft capability.

## Anatomy, topology, and comparison inspection

For every materially visible articulated limb or humanlike hand, use the visual-art skill's original-resolution inspection contract.

- Inventory every visible instance, not one representative example.
- Trace limbs from the correct parent origin through ordered joints to the expected endpoint.
- Human arms attach only to the torso/shoulder girdle; human legs attach only to the pelvis/hips.
- Treat the hips as the leg-chain anchor. Hip, torso, and limb rotations may differ only within plausible anatomical limits; do not accept contradictory turns merely because each silhouette reads alone.
- Record expected and observed endpoint type and joint-range class.
- Where full hand topology is visually legal, inspect enlarged local views and trace every visible digit to a unique palm root.
- Mark small, occluded, or unresolved anatomy `indeterminate`; uncertainty is not a pass.
- Judge topology, mechanics, contact, action, expression/identity, construction ceiling, and finish separately.

For every multi-panel comparison, explicitly list the fixed invariants and the intended variable before grading. Near-identical panels pass only when invariance is required and the target variable visibly changes. When a pass is meant to isolate one cue, unrelated value, color, contour, texture, or detail must not carry the result.

## Result interpretation

- **Attempt PASS:** every required artifact, process constraint, and Success Check condition passes.
- **Attempt FAIL:** execution was valid, but one or more required conditions fail.
- **Attempt INVALID:** the target capability was not fairly exercised.
- **Card PASS:** the card is executable and its contract correctly accepts or rejects the frozen attempt.
- **Card REPAIR CANDIDATE:** instructions omit an artifact later graded, the check accepts a complete but clearly bad near miss, or the card conflicts with itself or an owner.

Do not infer a canonical defect from an execution failure. Attribute the failure first to knowledge, orchestration, retrieval, application, continuity, reference, tool, or interface. Practice history remains Memory, never canon. Art changes requiring practitioner judgment must be proposed separately and left unimplemented until the user approves them.
