## Anatomy and perspective findings

- Human arm evidence: the repeated early image did not establish the required
  shoulder → elbow → wrist chain. The elbow must act as the hinge; a plausible
  contour is not enough.
- Hindleg evidence: the animal joint construction itself was strong in Drill 6,
  but missing view/process requirements still made the attempt fail.
- Perspective evidence: Drill 11 failed on eye-level and vanishing-point
  relationships; Drill 13 produced a plausible scene without a validated shared
  perspective field. Perspective is a composition gate for rendered work whenever
  depth, scale, or viewpoint matter.
