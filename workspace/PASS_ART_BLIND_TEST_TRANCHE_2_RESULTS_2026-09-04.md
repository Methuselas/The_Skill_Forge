## Articulated-limb and hand evidence

No full-resolution human hand topology was inferred from figures too small to support it. Small, occluded, or schematic hands were kept `indeterminate` rather than promoted to passes.

### Human motion and notation

- Drill 1: Step, Throw, Rise, and Catch retained believable parent-to-endpoint limb organization at sheet scale. Change Direction failed the mechanics audit: the leg/hip/torso orientations contradicted one another beyond a plausible limited twist. Turn retained typed limbs but had weak arm organization. No endpoint substitution was visible in the other poses; small hand topology was indeterminate.
- Drill 2: limb endpoints were broadly human-typed, but the static sheet did not establish phase-specific joint propagation. Any overlapped small hand or foot detail remained indeterminate. The failure is temporal/action orchestration, not a proven extra-limb topology event.
- Drill 3: ordinary limb chains were broadly readable in individual poses, but contact ownership and opposite-side continuity did not form pure walk cycles. Small hands were below topology-verification scale.
- Drill 5: schematic figure limbs remained readable enough for action notation. Terminal marks were too small for independent hand topology or detailed joint-range classification.
- Drill 6 hand inventory: front hand expected/observed `4 fingers + 1 thumb` and passed; side expected `4+1` but only `2+1` were resolved; three-quarter expected/observed `4+1` and passed; up-view expected `4+1` but observed `4+0`; down-view expected/observed `4+1` and passed. Feet generally retained foot endpoints but some toe articulation was excessive. No required ambiguous-pair rebuild occurred.
- Drill 7: several human chains failed typed origin/endpoint identity. Shoulder-origin chains terminated as feet/boots, violating torso-to-arm and hip-to-leg ownership. No-hidden-route panels overconstructed the hidden path; full-chain panels failed to preserve emerging fragments; build/check geometry drifted. These are impossible identity substitutions, not merely awkward ranges.
- Drill 8: the same foreshortened figure geometry was preserved across treatments. Interconnection, contour, and tone passed; the combination underused tone. Materially visible digits were not large enough for a reliable per-hand `4+1` root audit and remain indeterminate.

### Quadrupeds

- Drill 11: greyhound, goat, and bear retained species-specific skeleton proportions and broadly plausible shoulder/hip attachment. Visible near-side forelimbs and hindlimbs generally terminated as paws or hooves with ordinary ranges. Far-side chains were not independently traceable where exactly overlapped. The lower bear's hind chain omitted the femur between pelvis and lower-leg sequence, so that chain failed even though the overall bear construction remained recognizable.
- Drill 12: all four cougar poses retained feline endpoint types and substantially changed spine/limb directions. Standing and crouching ranges were ordinary; the leap used extreme-but-plausible extension. Some overlapped chains in the turn were visually ambiguous rather than independently proven. The decisive failure was process: contour and mass appeared in every panel, preventing a clean typed-scaffold audit before volume.
