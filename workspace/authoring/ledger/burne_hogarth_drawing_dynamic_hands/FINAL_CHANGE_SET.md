## Consolidation decisions

- Keep finger-chain rhythm distinct from coupled flexion: one solves form integration; the other solves movement sequencing.
- Keep thumb opposition distinct from general hand construction: rotation/facing is a hand-specific mechanical decision with its own failure mode.
- Keep surface-stress distinct from age design: surface stress is pose/load conditional; aging changes the baseline structural and tissue character.
- Keep function/contact/load distinct from communicative gesture: one is governed by physical task/contact; the other by intended message/attitude.
- Keep hand-invention as a Drill rather than a persistent Pattern because projection/reversal is primarily a practice method for building internal visualization.
- Do not create separate cards for named anatomy, fixed movement angles, specific symbolic signs, individual occupations, or literal age tables.
- Do not promote the stronger pre-source claim that the long fingers possess a universal fixed ranking of isolated flexion independence. The book directly supports coupled sequential fist closure and thumb opposition, not that broader hierarchy.
