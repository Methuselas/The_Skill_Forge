## Reconciliation with held project hypotheses

- **Supported/refined:** digit action is not equal or simultaneous during fist closure; Hogarth explicitly stages closure from the little-finger side toward the index, with the thumb last, as a graduated spiral.
- **Supported/refined:** the thumb is mechanically exceptional and reaches opposition through a changing spatial orientation; tracking the thumbnail plane makes that rotation drawable.
- **Not established by this chapter:** the stronger claim that the index is uniquely capable of full independent flexion while each successive long finger has a fixed recruitment hierarchy in arbitrary isolated flexion. The chapter's evidence is a fist-closure/opening sequence, not a general experimental ranking of independent finger flexion. Preserve the narrower source-supported claim.
- **Supported only in the chapter's model:** finger joints are presented with strong hinge-like closure limits. Exact degree values remain internal reference rather than runtime law.
