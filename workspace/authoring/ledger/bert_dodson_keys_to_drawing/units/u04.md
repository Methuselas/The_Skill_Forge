## Guided architecture clarification

MaDin's teaching broadens the ownership boundary without being falsely attributed to Dodson: lighting can be designed through value **or color**, including changes in ambient color, to shape scene mood and narrative emphasis. The architecture therefore separates the concerns:

- **Composition** owns why/where lighting is designed for subject, mood, focal hierarchy, and narrative intent.
- **Rendering** owns how chosen illumination changes visible value/color, edges, cast shadows, reflected light, and material response.

Dodson u04 grounds value/light-shadow design. A full colored-light rendering theory remains pending a later source that actually teaches it.
