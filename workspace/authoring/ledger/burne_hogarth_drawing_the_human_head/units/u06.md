## Delta map

- NEW DRILL — **compare master heads for invariant structure**: analyze unlike master works through one shared structural checklist, separate transferable mass/support/plane decisions from style-specific surface language, then transfer one head into a different mark-making method without losing its construction.
- VARIANT — **verify observed expression asymmetry against its support mass**: when a feature looks out of drawing, check whether hard support and neighboring tissue deformation actually explain the asymmetry before normalizing it.
- VARIANT — **confirm extreme head attitude with multi-region clues**: difficult turns and wrench-tilts are cross-checked through several small, mutually consistent cues across head, neck, and visible shoulder rather than one centerline alone.
- VARIANT — **exaggerate head character without abandoning form order**: caricature, roughness, and stylization may push far from naturalism while the head's internal support relationships remain coherent.
- VARIANT — **concentrate accents at structurally decisive points**: keep minor passages loose or atmospheric and spend the strongest marks only where they carry orientation, identity, or a critical form turn.
- REJECTED/REINFORCEMENT — exact Greek feature alignments, eyelid formulas, wrinkle routes, cephalic labels, fixed focal-point choices, age examples, hatching-as-carving, overlap/tone demonstrations, and broad value recession already have stronger owners elsewhere.
