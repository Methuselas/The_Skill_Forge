## Delta map

- VARIANT — **age the head by coordinating growth, tissue change, and carriage**: use Hogarth's lifespan sequence as a multi-system construction scaffold rather than a calendar of mandatory age symbols.
- VARIANT — **rebuild the late-life lower face from support change before wrinkles**: when dental/gum support loss is actually present, change the jaw-mouth-chin construction first; never assume that condition from age alone.
- NEW DRILL — **age one head through a lifespan strip**: preserve one identity across infant, child, teen, adult, middle-age, and older stages, then strip away hair/fine wrinkles to test whether the structural age progression still reads.
- REJECTED — exact year ratios, mandatory hair loss/graying, fixed emotional fatigue, universal stoop, and universal tooth-loss morphology remain source-specific examples rather than anatomy doctrine.
- REINFORCES — Loomis infant/child/teen age ladder, Loomis structural aging before wrinkles, Hogarth u03 sag/shrinkage and cause-first wrinkles, and Bammes whole-age-type observation.
