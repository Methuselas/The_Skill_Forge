## Delta map

- VARIANT — **map wrinkles as regional flow families**: use Hogarth's frontal/oblique/lateral routes as a pre-detail directional scaffold, while observation decides which routes actually exist.
- VARIANT — **classify wrinkles by tension, pressure, sag, and shrinkage**: choose crease marks from a drawing cause rather than from a wrinkle symbol; the categories are heuristic, not medical diagnosis.
- VARIANT — **layer interacting wrinkle causes**: allow several supported tissue reactions to coexist or oppose each other on one continuous face instead of compartmentalizing them.
- NEW DRILL — **map facial wrinkles by flow and cause**: study several real/reference faces, map large route families, identify useful causes, simplify the crease set, then redraw the weakest case from memory.
- REJECTED — exact local route diagrams, the ear-as-island metaphor, and the claim that sag/shrinkage routes are simply random are retained as source reference rather than universal surface law.
- REINFORCES — existing expression-over-fixed-skull, age-before-wrinkles, active/passive soft-tissue deformation, and expression consistency cards.
