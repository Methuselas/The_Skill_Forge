## Delta map

- VARIANT — **five-part cranial helmet** after the two-mass block: forehead shell, crown/vault, occipital bulge, concave temple wall, and brow visor give the cranial ball useful directional structure without requiring literal skull-suture drawing.
- VARIANT — **dominant jaw plus secondary facial masses**: use the mandible as primary facial support, then organize brow/nose/socket/cheek/mouth/chin/jaw-angle/ear forms as spatial construction rather than feature symbols.
- VARIANT — **support mass to secondary forms for complex features**: globe/socket → lids, nose wedge → component masses/underplane, dental barrel → lips, ear shell/bowl → rims/projections. Local average ratios remain non-binding.
- VARIANT — **rank head anatomy by rigidity and visible impact**: distinguish bone/cartilage/muscle, then prioritize only the groups that materially affect surface form and action instead of drawing a complete ecorche.
- NEW DRILL — **rebuild complex facial features from support to cover** across multiple views, transfer one study back to a head, then redraw the weakest feature from memory and correct the earliest structural miss.
- REINFORCES — the existing Hogarth two-mass head foundation, Hampton's stage gate and feature-perspective inheritance, Bammes/Zarins skull-to-surface construction, Loomis's linked eye spheres, and the broader rule that average proportions yield to observed/design-specific anatomy.
- NON-PROMOTED — fixed front/side ratios, eye-width and ear-third recipes, exact jaw/ear angles, letter-shape lip mnemonics as law, exhaustive facial-muscle naming, and other local anatomy facts that do not change a reusable decision beyond the retained construction variants.
