## Delta map

- VARIANT — **balance facial turn with rear-cranial reveal**: use the shifted curved facial centerline as bookkeeping for how much back-of-skull width must be recovered during a front-to-profile turn. Hogarth's equal measure remains a heuristic, not a perspective theorem.
- VARIANT — **track the side plane from the shifted brow point**: move the brow-side landmark with the centerline and let the visible side plane expand as the head turns. The nominal equal shift and fifteen-degree drop remain optional construction shorthand.
- VARIANT — **propagate tilt through curved feature tracks**: use the brow as master wrapped axis, carry related curvature through crown/feature/jaw bands, and place the ear between brow and nose-base tracks so up/down views read as one solid.
- VARIANT — **carry the facial centerline into the anterior neck**: in extreme angles, continue the face axis toward the throat before adding the neck column from the skull base; this is an orientation check, not an exact larynx locator.
- DRILL VARIANT — **measured rotation strip from a front template**: adapt the existing head-rotation Drill into a progressive front → three-quarter → profile strip using Hogarth's rear-cranium and side-plane bookkeeping, then reverse the turn.
- REJECTED — the claim that the jaw outline stays exactly fixed through rotation, exact equal-third proof ratios, the fifteen- and forty-five-degree local angle rules, fixed feature ratios, and other diagram measurements are source heuristics/reference rather than universal anatomy or perspective law.
- REINFORCES — the existing curved head centerline, cranial/facial mass rotation, support-to-cover feature construction, and general ellipse/perspective cards.
