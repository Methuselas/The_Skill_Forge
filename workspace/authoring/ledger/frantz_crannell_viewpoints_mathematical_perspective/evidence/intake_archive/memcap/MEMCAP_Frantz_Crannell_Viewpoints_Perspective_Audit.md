# MEMCAP — Frantz & Crannell Viewpoints Perspective Deep PASS

## Why this source was read
The five artist-facing Perspective books were already complete. *Viewpoints* was added as a dedicated mathematical audit so internal decision logic could be rigorous without forcing math pedagogy into normal drawing runtime.

## What survived
- **Replacement:** `PAT_control_perspective_distortion_with_viewpoint_and_projection_choice` supersedes the D'Amelio VP-spacing-only distortion Pattern.
- **New specialization:** `PAT_validate_three_point_viewpoint_geometry` — acute VP triangle, orthocenter viewing target, exact viewing-distance geometry.
- **Finalize exact-camera variant:** eye/station point + Picture Plane + viewing target + viewing distance; Chapter 2 perspective formula stays internal.
- **Finalize coupled-VP visual-ray variant:** a direction VP is the Picture-Plane intersection of a sight ray from the eye parallel to that world direction.
- **Extend reference recovery:** exact station-point/viewing-distance recovery when known orthogonal/proportional geometry exists.

## Holds resolved
- Fixed 50/60-degree COV numbers are **not** promoted as universal mathematics; exact station/display geometry is the durable rule.
- Skyscraper close-up paradox is resolved: flat rectilinear perspective is not wrong, but practical support/viewing distance can become absurd; spherical/curvilinear projection is a deliberate compact alternative.
- Tilted/three-point views use exact VP-triangle geometry rather than forcing the ordinary horizontal-horizon mnemonic.
- Ellipse minor-axis theorem is **not addressed by Viewpoints** and therefore remains unpromoted; Robertson heuristic + D'Amelio exact fallback remain.

## What did not become core
Anamorphosis, forced perspective, Hitchcock zoom, and spherical-painting art history remain reference/special-effect material except where they support the projection-choice Pattern.
