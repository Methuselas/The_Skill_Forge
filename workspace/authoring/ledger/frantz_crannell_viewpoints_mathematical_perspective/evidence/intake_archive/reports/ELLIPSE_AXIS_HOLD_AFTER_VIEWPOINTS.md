# Ellipse Axis Hold — Final Status After Viewpoints

## Question carried into the audit
Does the practical claim used by Norling/Robertson—that the minor axis/axle of a perspective ellipse tracks the projected normal/axis of the source circle—survive as a universal projective theorem?

## Source result
The perspective half of Frantz & Crannell's *Viewpoints* does not teach projected-circle/ellipse axis geometry. Its uses of ellipses occur in anamorphic examples and do not prove the needed theorem.

## PASS disposition
**UNRESOLVED AS A UNIVERSAL THEOREM; NO PROMOTION.**

This is no longer a blocker for the Perspective curriculum:
- Robertson's minor-axis/axle rule remains an operational drawing heuristic for ellipse placement and rotation.
- D'Amelio's point-constructed circle-in-perspective method remains the exact fallback when correctness matters.
- No future source is required unless the project specifically wants the formal conic/projective proof.

The important thing is that the runtime library must not silently convert the heuristic into stronger mathematics than the sources support.
