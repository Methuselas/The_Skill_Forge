The full Combining Spheres and Boxes chapter was read and visually checked. Its distinctive lesson is not merely mixing primitives; it is learning to **feel one continuous surface over different underlying structures**. Vilppu has the student imagine material stretched over a sphere and box, then bend, twist, pinch, and stretch that combined form while keeping the underlying volumes legible.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — interconnection by wrapping one continuous surface over dissimilar underlying masses.
- REINFORCES — pinch/stretch, overlap, controlled proportion variation, and reference-free form invention.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another proportion or primitive-combination Drill; existing Loomis/Hampton material already covers those practice goals.
