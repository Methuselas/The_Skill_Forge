The complete Indirect Lighting and Modeling Tone chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu explicitly separates this from literal direct lighting: it is an **analytic modeling convention** for understanding form, with the viewer effectively functioning as the light source. Surfaces facing the viewer stay light and darken as they turn away; edge contrast can also be reduced as forms recede. The atmospheric material introduced near the end is held for u12.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — resolved-form tone with a viewer-centered analytic modeling mode that can test volume without literal scene lighting.
- REINFORCES — value control, edge softening, line suppression, and form-first analysis.
- HOLDS FORWARD — distance-based contrast/detail reduction and receding-edge treatment to u12 Atmospheric Perspective.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another generic value-scale or no-line simple-form Drill.
