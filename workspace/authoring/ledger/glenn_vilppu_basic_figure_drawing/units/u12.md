The full Atmospheric Perspective chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu starts from the physical fact of reduced contrast/detail with distance, then deliberately abstracts it into a **figurative design tool**. He uses local atmosphere and edge/value suppression to separate overlapping forms, make some masses emerge or recede, and strengthen the action path.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — resolved-form tone with local atmospheric contrast/detail control for figure depth and action.
- RESOLVES u10 FORWARDS — distance-based contrast/detail reduction is retained here; the specific darker-leading-edge device remains a narrow implementation rather than a separate rule.
- REINFORCES — shadow-value compression, edge loss, and conceptual tone as subordinate to underlying form.
