The full first anatomy chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu uses anatomy selectively as a set of **clarifying landmarks** rather than as a catalog. His distinctive operational move is to let visible bony point relationships act like cross-axes or terminal planes that reveal the direction of the limb or girdle in space.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — joint transitions by using bony landmarks as directional cross-axes/end planes.
- REINFORCES — torso symmetry, contrapposto compression/stretch, shoulder-girdle orientation, and radius/ulna mechanics.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — separate torso-landmark and pronation cards because Hampton/Bridgman already cover those more deeply.
