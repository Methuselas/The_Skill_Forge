The full second anatomy chapter was read and visually checked. Most of its anatomy mechanics are already represented more deeply by Hampton, Bridgman, Hogarth, and Dynamic Hands. The genuinely distinct practical contribution is Vilppu's **surface-path exercise**: literal stripes and the “ant crawling” visualization train cross-contour perception before those guides become implicit in normal drawing.

## Delta map

- NEW DRILL — trace literal surface paths over wrapped/crumpled forms, then internalize them as imagined cross-contours.
- REINFORCES — anatomy subordinate to parent volumes, T-overlap, shoulder/hip connections, active/passive stretch/compression, and simple-mass-first anatomy.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — additional regional anatomy cards.
