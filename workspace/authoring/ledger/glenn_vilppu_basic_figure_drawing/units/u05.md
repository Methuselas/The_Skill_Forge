The complete Ellipses and Cylinders chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu's distinctive operational emphasis is to solve the cylinder's **axis and endpoints before the ellipses**, keeping those ellipses perpendicular to the same centerline. His broader pedagogy is that the construction should become an underlying visual logic rather than a visible end product.
