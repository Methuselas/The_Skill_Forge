The full 2D/3D observation chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu's distinctive contribution is the **deliberate alternation between two valid readings of the same figure**: three-dimensional form construction and two-dimensional placement/shape survey. Either mode alone can fail—3-D analysis can drift away from the observed pose, while 2-D copying can flatten the figure. Their disagreement becomes a diagnostic.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — Loomis's live-figure visual survey with an explicit 2D ↔ 3D cross-check loop.
- REINFORCES — overall placement before detail, relative measurement, diagonals/arcs, and proportion.
- ADDS EMPHASIS — negative shape and silhouette function as flat checks on otherwise volumetric construction.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another general proportion card; Loomis already covers adjustable relative units.
