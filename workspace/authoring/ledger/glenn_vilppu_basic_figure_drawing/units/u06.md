The full Basic Procedure chapter was read and visually checked. Vilppu explicitly presents a layered procedural approach that moves from the total gesture into clarified masses and later specifics, while repeatedly warning that this is a flexible plan rather than a rule. The pedagogical value is problem separation: one pass can solve one class of uncertainty instead of asking every line to solve everything at once.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — the staged workflow with a general-to-specific, one-problem-at-a-time procedural emphasis.
- REINFORCES — Stage 1 gesture first, Stage 2 mass clarification, construction as temporary scaffold, and direct drawing as a valid alternate mode.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — medium-layering techniques and line-style examples; existing architecture already carries their general lessons.
