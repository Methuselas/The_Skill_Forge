## Explicit non-promotions

- sphere, box, cylinder, or anatomy construction as a mandatory universal mannequin;
- “there are no rules, just tools” as a standalone runtime card;
- duplicate anatomy catalogs already covered more deeply by Hogarth, Bridgman, Hampton, and Dynamic Hands;
- generic value-scale, box-rotation, ellipse, or cylinder drills already represented by broader practice objects;
- indirect/modeling tone as literal physical scene lighting;
- full lighting taxonomy already covered by Gill and existing rendering architecture.

## Basic-figure curriculum result

Vilppu closes the basic-figure book campaign by strongly validating the current staged architecture rather than overturning it. His most distinctive additions are practical drills for short visual-memory gesture and surface-path perception, plus flexible Stage 1/2 transitions, procedural problem separation, 2D↔3D observational cross-checking, and compact tone diagnostics.

## Reserved practical follow-up

After the basic figure curriculum is closed, practical drawing drills may use approved references and memcap records to stress-test the retained Skills. One planned comic-panel drill is specifically:
`comic panel -> Stage 1 skeleton extraction -> critique/correction -> approved Stage 1 memcap`.
The purpose is structural pose analysis, not style copying.
