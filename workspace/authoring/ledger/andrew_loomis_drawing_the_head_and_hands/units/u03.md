## Delta map

- VARIANT — **single-light planar head-value study** on `PAT_consolidate_resolved_form_with_tone`: construct the head first, use one strong light, simplify into broad light/halftone/shadow/cast-shadow masses, preserve evidence of the planes as edges soften, and postpone wrinkles until the plane/value read works.
- VARIANT — **turn planes with stroke direction before increasing value contrast** on the same tone foundation. Loomis's hatching/mark direction becomes a form cue rather than decoration, useful when a line-dominant drawing should stay tonally restrained.
- NEW DRILL — **reverse-engineer one head reference through structural stages**. Make separate proportion, anatomy/construction, plane, and finished studies from the same reference; compare the stack and repair the earliest stage that fails instead of hiding the problem with finish.
- REINFORCES — form-shadow versus cast-shadow cause, edge softness following plane rate, individualized likeness from whole structure, structural aging before fine wrinkles, and muscle-driven expression all already have stronger committed ownership.
