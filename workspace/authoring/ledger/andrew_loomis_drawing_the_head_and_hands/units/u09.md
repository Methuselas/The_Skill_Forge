## Delta map

- NEW DRILL — **Make Many Observational Studies of Your Own Hand**: use the always-available live hand across varied views/actions, compare palm and digit relationships directly, include foreshortening and object contact, and immediately repeat the weakest structural view.
- VARIANT — **Pre-Shape the Hand to the Object Before Contact** on `PAT_configure_hand_around_function_contact_and_load`: treat the approaching grasp as an anticipatory contour adjustment rather than waiting until contact to fit the fingers.
- VARIANT — **Reduce the Hand to Three Dorsal Planes and Three Palmar Blocks** on `PAT_construct_hand_from_palm_wedge_and_digit_chain`: Loomis's block-head-like simplification gives the palm/back a fast grasp-capable solid before finer anatomy.
- VARIANT — **Align the Nail Centerline to the Finger Column** on `PAT_unify_finger_chains_with_knuckle_shank_rhythm`: use the nail as a terminal-axis/facing check so it cannot slide around the digit independently.
- VARIANT — **Age a Young Hand by Palm-to-Finger Mass Ratio** on `PAT_design_hand_age_by_structural_and_surface_cues`: establish baby/child age structurally through the fuller palm/thumb-heel versus smaller fingers before surface age cues.
- FILTERED — hand-proportion ratios, generic palm hollow/flexion, tendon anatomy, general foreshortening/action/knuckle material, sex-based hand averages, tapered-finger beauty framing, old-hand wrinkle advice already owned by stronger age structure, and broad learning-logistics/farewell doctrine remain reference or reinforcement rather than additional cards.
