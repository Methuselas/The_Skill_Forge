## Delta map

- VARIANT — **keep planar structure under a soft head design** on `PAT_construct_head_from_cranial_ball_and_facial_wedge`. Loomis explicitly keeps the block/plane logic under the women's-head examples while reducing how strongly the planes and jaw/cheek anatomy are stated at the surface. The historical male/female contrast is not promoted as a universal sex classifier.
- VARIANT — **block hair as a large mass before strands** on the same head foundation. Loomis repeatedly treats hairstyle as a silhouette/plane mass around the constructed skull, with strand/curl detail delayed until the large form reads.
- REINFORCES — divided-ball construction, accurate feature placement, proportion-before-detail likeness, hidden anatomy beneath visible surface, and structural aging before fine wrinkles all already have stronger committed ownership.
- REJECTS — commercial-market claims, fixed beauty/fashion prescriptions, and categorical male/female shape rules are period-specific context rather than durable PASS runtime doctrine.
