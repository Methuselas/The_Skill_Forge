## Delta map

- NEW PATTERN — **elicit natural child expression through rapport**: when a child becomes guarded under adult direction, reduce pose authority and build rapport so the observed expression is natural rather than commanded.
- VARIANT — **transition from small child to school age by raising the eye band and maturing the jaw** on `PAT_construct_head_from_cranial_ball_and_facial_wedge`: preserve the still-young upper-cranium relationship while the eye band, dentition, jaw, mouth/nose region, and rear jaw mature toward but not yet into adult structure.
- VARIANT — **check head age against the body context** on `PAT_set_figure_proportions_with_adjustable_head_units`: a child head can look older in a crop because head growth and body growth are not synchronized, so use the whole figure when developmental age matters.
- VARIANT — **gate tone on age and expression reading in outline** on `PAT_consolidate_resolved_form_with_tone`: do not use shading to rescue a line-led child head whose age/expression read is structurally wrong.
- FILTERED — period-specific advertising mood rules, categorical schoolgirl appearance claims, exact quarter/diagonal formulas, and decorative age symbols remain source context or supporting evidence rather than runtime micro-cards.
