## Delta map

- VARIANT — **standard-head unit grid** as an optional proportional alternative to the divided-ball route. Loomis uses an arbitrary repeatable unit to stabilize an average adult male head in front and side views, then fits the ball-and-plane construction into that grid. The retained boundary is explicit: averages are a starting scale, not fixed anatomy.
- VARIANT — **rotate the facial muscle map with the head construction**. After learning attachment/action, Loomis asks the learner to place the muscle groups into tipped and turned heads around the same middle line rather than retaining a front-view anatomy chart.
- VARIANT — **paired eyes as linked spheres**. The eyes are treated as two spherical forms working together with lids wrapped over them, which gives a compact way to prevent flat or duplicated eye symbols in non-frontal views.
- NEW DRILL — **hold one head type across an expression set**. Build one type, then draw that same character smiling, frowning, pouting, laughing, worrying, and so on; use a mirror to observe actual muscle/form action and compare the set for identity drift.
- VARIANT — **age the face from structural forms before wrinkles**. Keep the same base head construction, use large bony/soft-tissue changes and major creases to carry age, and subordinate the fine wrinkle network.
- REINFORCES — whole-head viewpoint before feature detail, lips over the rounded dental mass, lower-jaw motion, feature perspective, anatomy by action/attachment rather than names, and muscle-driven expression over stable skull structure.
- NON-PROMOTED — Loomis's period-specific commercial-art emphasis on smiling faces, his “happy/unhappy” muscle labels, photographic-crutch argument, tooth-by-tooth smile rendering advice, and other local micro-facts that do not change a reusable learner decision.
