## Delta map

- VARIANT — **baby head with short facial plane and four divisions** on `PAT_construct_head_from_cranial_ball_and_facial_wedge`: preserve the dominant cranium, shorten the facial plane, then quarter the compact face below the brow rather than scaling down adult facial spacing.
- VARIANT — **age the young-child head by growing the facial mass under the cranium** on the same foundation: jaw/nose/upper-jaw growth lengthens the face, so the eyes and brows sit progressively higher in the whole head from infancy toward early childhood.
- NEW DRILL — **build a baby-head age ladder from reference**: study still/sleeping/photo references across first-year, second/third-year, and third/fourth-year stages, reconstruct the proportion scaffold from memory, then correct the earliest cranial/facial relationship error.
- REINFORCES — u04 soft-surface planar construction, u00 head-type envelope variation, Bammes global-proportion likeness, and the existing age-type whole-figure drill.
- REJECTS AS MICRO-CARDS — isolated infant feature averages, iris explanation, hair-as-age cue, and local cheek highlighting remain supporting evidence rather than separate runtime objects.
