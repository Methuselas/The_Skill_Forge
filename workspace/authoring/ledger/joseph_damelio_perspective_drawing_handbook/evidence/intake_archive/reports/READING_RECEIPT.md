- The cube plates make the changing direction-family convergence much clearer than prose alone.
- The distortion examples on pp. 58-60 show the practical difference between too-close and too-distant vanishing points.
- The measurement/grid diagrams on pp. 68-76 are essential to understanding center finding and repeated spacing.
- The circle/cylinder plates on pp. 81-86 visually establish the difference between the ellipse's apparent center and the original circle's projected construction center.
- The shadow diagrams on pp. 87-96 distinguish parallel sunlight projection from radial point-source projection.
