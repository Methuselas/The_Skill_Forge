## Key teacher corrections
- Projection transports relationships; it does not replace construction knowledge.
- Relative spatial correspondence survives viewpoint change.
- Once a perspective grid is established, the original figure is only calibration; the grid governs the scene.
- Action drawings are keyframes. Do not polish a weak key; move through the action to find a stronger one.
- Comics may require deliberate exaggeration: wind-up and follow-through must sell the action.
- Construction order and kinetic order are different.
- Hand detail is deferred to *Drawing Dynamic Hands*.
