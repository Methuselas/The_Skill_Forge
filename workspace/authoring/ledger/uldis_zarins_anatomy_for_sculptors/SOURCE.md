## Source-role note

Zarins is a completed PASS source. Its sculptural/3D orientation is treated as a source of drawing-relevant form analysis: simplification, planes, cross-sections, landmarks, mass relationships, and mechanics. Eliot Goldfinger's *Human Anatomy for Artists* may be consulted only to verify anatomical plausibility when needed; it is not a source of Zarins PASS deltas.

The perspective-committed PASS repository is the frozen comparison baseline. In particular, Robertson's committed section/curve machinery is available as canonical spatial infrastructure; Zarins must earn only human-form-specific additions rather than duplicating general section drawing.
