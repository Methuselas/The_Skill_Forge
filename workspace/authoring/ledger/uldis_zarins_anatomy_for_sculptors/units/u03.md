## Delta map

- REVIEW — Zarins supplies unusually clear multi-view visual proof that shoulder, scapula, chest, and back form change together as the arm changes position, but the existing Hogarth/Bridgman/Hampton shoulder architecture already owns the runtime decisions.
- REVIEW — photo → anatomy → mesh comparisons strongly validate the u01 planar-blockout and u02 layer-causality variants; they do not require a third transfer card.
- REFERENCE — 3D scans are excellent body-type/surface references but are not methods by themselves.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — scapular rotation, multi-view shoulder checking, developmental head-unit averages, and idealized-vs-realistic adult head-unit standards.
- NO NEW OBJECTS OR VARIANTS — this unit primarily validates and visualizes the now-mature torso/shoulder/proportion system.
