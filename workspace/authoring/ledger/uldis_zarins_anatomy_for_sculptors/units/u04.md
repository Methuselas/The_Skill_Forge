## Delta map

- VARIANT — **anatomically anchor head plane breaks**: use persistent skull boundaries such as the temporal turn, cranial widest point, and jaw/chin relationship to decide where the living head stops behaving like a generic sphere.
- VARIANT — **expression deforms soft tissue over a stable skull framework**: keep bony landmarks as the structural reference while muscles pull/squeeze eyelids, lips, cheeks, platysma, and wrinkle fields.
- REVIEW — eye, mouth, nose, ear, neck-muscle, and proportion material where current head/neck construction already owns the learner decision or the imminent specialist-head campaign should perform the deeper extraction.
- REFERENCE — emotion/ethnicity galleries are useful visual breadth but do not earn runtime cards by themselves.
- NO NEW STANDALONE OBJECTS — this unit contributes two focused 3D/planar specializations without preempting the dedicated head books.
