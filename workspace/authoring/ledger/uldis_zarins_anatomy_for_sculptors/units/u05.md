## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Goldfinger was used only as a plausibility check on the forearm rotation model already established by prior committed sources: the radius rotates relative to the ulna and the ulna remains a strong little-finger-side skeletal/surface reference. No Goldfinger-only claim is promoted in this unit.

## Delta map

- VARIANT — **track pronation through changing cross-section and a persistent ulnar rail**. Radius-around-ulna mechanics must alter the visible forearm volume and flexor/extensor orientation; use the subcutaneous ulna as a stabilizing surface reference while the other masses turn around it.
- REINFORCES — Bridgman radius/ulna mechanics, Bammes's twistable quadrangular forearm and cross-sectional arm architecture, Hampton arm sequencing, Dynamic Hands palm/finger/thumb/wrist systems.
- REVIEW — Zarins's front/back arm-root asymmetry, carrying angle, hand proportions, wrist-range charts, and hidden finger-root/crease diagrams are useful references but do not beat the current runtime baseline.
- QUESTIONABLE AS GENERAL RULE — the source labels radial deviation as an unnatural position better avoided in sculpture; PASS does **not** promote that recommendation as a universal constraint.
- NO NEW STANDALONE OBJECTS — u05 contributes one sculptural rotation specialization without duplicating the mature hand curriculum.
