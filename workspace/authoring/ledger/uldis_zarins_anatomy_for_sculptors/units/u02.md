## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Goldfinger was consulted only to verify anatomical plausibility. He independently supports the layered-surface model: serratus can remain visible beneath the thin latissimus, and trunk surface relief can be created by deeper structures beneath tendinous/superficial layers. Goldfinger also maps male/female fat pads and describes fat/skin overlapping the iliac crest. These checks support Zarins's anatomical plausibility; no Goldfinger-only fact is promoted and all PASS locators remain Zarins.

## Delta map

- VARIANT — **layer causality**: visible torso relief may be generated by deeper anatomy displacing a superficial sheet; diagnose the stack instead of naming the top layer from contour alone.
- VARIANT — **deformable breast mass over pectoral wall**: separate breast tissue from pectoralis/rib cage, keep its attachment, and redistribute its mass with gravity/orientation rather than pasting a sphere.
- VARIANT — **regional subcutaneous-fat layer**: preserve the skeletal/muscular core while selectively expanding, smoothing, or masking it with soft-tissue pads; adiposity is not uniform scaling.
- DEFER — deltoid insertion/surface tracking until u05, where Zarins supplies the complete upper-arm blockout and surface naming system.
- REVIEW — muscle catalog pages, clavicle shape, generic shoulder anatomy, flank sex differences, abdominal naming, and pelvis blockout where the golden baseline already has the governing construction behavior.
