## Delta map

- VARIANT — **connect changing leg cross-sections with anatomical rails**. Use meaningful section stations plus persistent skeletal or long muscular routes to derive asymmetric planes through the whole leg instead of stacking cylinders.
- VARIANT — **treat the posterior knee as a pose-dependent layered surface**. An extended knee need not be hollow at the back; fat and underlying structures can fill it, while flexion deepens the fossa and separates the bordering masses.
- REINFORCES — Bammes rolling-knee mechanics, Bridgman lower-leg bony asymmetry, Hampton calf/leg volume design, Vilppu landmarks, and Zarins's earlier regional-fat/deep-layer causality.
- REVIEW — muscle catalogs, body-type galleries, and 3D scans remain reference rather than new runtime cards.
