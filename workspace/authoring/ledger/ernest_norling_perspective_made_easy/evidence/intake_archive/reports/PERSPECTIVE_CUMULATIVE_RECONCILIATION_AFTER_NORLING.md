# Perspective Cumulative Reconciliation — After Norling

## Frozen input
The D’Amelio PASS candidate package is the read-only perspective baseline for this pass. No D’Amelio candidate file and no canonical library file is changed here.

## What Norling changes
Norling is primarily reinforcement. It does **not** supersede D’Amelio’s eye-level/vanishing structure, scale transfer, plane metrology, inclined planes, projected circles, cylinder/cone center logic, or cast-shadow AP.

It earns three additions:
- reverse-engineering the view field of an existing image;
- level-plane reflections;
- plan/elevation mechanical projection.

It earns five patch proposals that make existing D’Amelio cards easier to diagnose and operate, but these are deliberately stored as proposals until the perspective sequence is reconciled as a whole.

## Authority boundary
Where Norling offers a simplified mnemonic that appears weaker than the already-held D’Amelio construction—most notably the ellipse-axis “T” rule—the stricter cumulative baseline is retained and the mnemonic is held for verification.

## Next source
Gwen White should be read next as the broader/careful construction source, with special attention to ellipses, inclined planes, oblique perspective, shadows, and reflections.
