# Ellipse Axis Verification Hold

## Source claim
Norling's cylinder sequence (printed pp. 124-129) presents a simplified rule that the ellipse's long axis and the cylinder's center/axle make a right-angle “T,” with the short axis following the cylinder axis.

## PASS disposition
**Questionable — verify later; no patch and no new card.**

The cumulative baseline already contains D'Amelio's stricter projected-circle construction: establish the circle on its perspective plane through an enclosing perspective square and distinguish the true projected center from the ellipse's apparent center. Norling's mnemonic may be useful in limited cases, but promoting it now could overgeneralize a screen-space ellipse shortcut into a universal projective rule.

Hold this claim for direct reconciliation against Gwen White, Scott Robertson, and the later *Viewpoints* mathematical audit.
