# Patch Proposal — Reframe the View Instead of Forcing a Panorama

**Target:** `PAT_control_perspective_distortion_with_vanishing_spacing`

**Disposition:** candidate patch proposal only; target card remains byte-unchanged.

## Proposed addition
Add a composition-level rollback: when a view becomes so wide that one fixed two-point construction produces violent edge stretching, treat the turned portion as a new look/view or deliberately choose a panoramic/curvilinear system later. Do not keep dragging the same vanishing pair across an unlimited field.

## Evidence
Ernest Norling, *Perspective Made Easy*, printed pp. 49-56 and 91-93.

## Why patch rather than new card
D'Amelio already covers excessive convergence and vanishing-point spacing. Norling contributes a practical “change the view” response.
