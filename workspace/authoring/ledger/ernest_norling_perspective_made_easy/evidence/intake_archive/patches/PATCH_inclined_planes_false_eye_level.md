# Patch Proposal — Slope Auxiliary / “False Eye-Level” Diagnostic

**Target:** `PAT_construct_inclined_planes_from_base_vanishing_directions`

**Disposition:** candidate patch proposal only; target card remains byte-unchanged.

## Proposed addition
For a road or surface that rises or falls while nearby buildings remain level, keep the true eye level for level-world horizontal directions and establish a separate slope-aligned vanishing line/direction for objects traveling with the incline. Norling calls the slope cue a “false eye-level”; retain that wording only as a teaching analogy, not as a second literal camera eye level.

## Evidence
Ernest Norling, *Perspective Made Easy*, printed pp. 185-189.

## Why patch rather than new card
The D'Amelio inclined-plane Pattern already contains the geometric rule. Norling adds a strong street/hill application and diagnostic vocabulary.
