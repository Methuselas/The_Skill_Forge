# Patch Proposal — Eye-Level “High-Water Mark” Diagnostic

**Target:** `PAT_establish_eye_level_and_vanishing_directions`

**Disposition:** candidate patch proposal only; target card remains byte-unchanged.

## Proposed addition
Add a compact visual diagnostic: treat eye level like a high-water mark across upright forms. Surfaces and landmarks above eye level expose undersides; those below it expose tops; features exactly at eye level flatten toward edge-on. Use this as a quick check after the formal eye-level line is chosen.

## Evidence
Ernest Norling, *Perspective Made Easy*, printed pp. 13-19.

## Why patch rather than new card
D'Amelio already supplies the eye-level foundation. Norling adds a memorable perceptual check, not a new capability.
