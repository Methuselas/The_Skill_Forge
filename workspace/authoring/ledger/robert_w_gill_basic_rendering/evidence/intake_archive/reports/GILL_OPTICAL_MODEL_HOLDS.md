# Gill Optical-Model Holds

These source claims are retained as historical/teaching models but are **not** promoted as universal project truth without later verification.

1. **Fixed 60-degree cone of vision.** Useful as a conservative drafting convention; already queued with White/Robertson for the *Viewpoints* mathematical audit.
2. **“One-look” as literal human-eye behavior.** Keep the useful single-viewpoint construction rule, not the claim that a picture must reproduce what an immobile eye literally sees.
3. **Basic tonal pattern as universal ranking.** Gill often ranks horizontal light plane > vertical light plane > form shade > cast shadow under “normal conditions.” His own later examples admit reflected-light exceptions. Keep it as a controlled exercise model, not a universal lighting law.
4. **White reflects all / black absorbs all.** Gill labels these theoretical endpoints. Do not treat them as practical material physics.
5. **Atmospheric-particle mechanism.** The observed depth effect survives; the simplified explanation of scattered/deflected rays is not imported as a complete physical model.

These holds are intentionally separated from the durable runtime cards so the later patch review can distinguish useful artist heuristics from mathematical/optical claims.
