## Notes

Placement: `PAT_use_domain_specific_cues_not_generic_problem_frames` went to
`foundations/problem-solving` with the u06 material, and the other four to
`foundations/deliberate-practice`. That split follows the chapter's own division —
§10.1 and §10.2 are about what problem solving is, while §10.3 and §10.4 are two
training techniques, which is training rather than solving.

Both variants attach to foundations from earlier units and both sharpen rather
than extend them. The germane-load variant is the more interesting case because it
**contradicts a sentence in the foundation it attaches to**.
`PAT_separate_intrinsic_from_extraneous_load` closes by saying only intrinsic and
extraneous bear on reading code in front of you; chapter 10 shows germane load
bears on it whenever the reading is meant to teach you something. The foundation's
Notes were amended to point at the variant rather than left standing, since
leaving a claim the library elsewhere refutes is worse than the edit.

The second variant is where the chapter connects back to u07. Chapter 7's negative
transfer is about beliefs, repaired by checking what you assumed; chapter 10's is
the same interference in procedural memory, where the belief is already correct and
only repetition helps. The two cannot be one card because the repair differs, and
they cannot be two foundations because the decision is the same.

One deliberate non-object: the chapter's germane-load material could have become a
standalone pattern about protecting capacity for learning. It was absorbed as a
variant instead because the decision it governs — how to account for load — is
already owned, and a second card would have split the load vocabulary across two
places.
