## Notes

Placement: all five new objects went to `foundations/working-practice`, the topic
u11 opened. That is the right home — this chapter is about how work is organised
around people, and four of the five carry `lane_fit: teach` with
`routing_class: teaching`, which is the largest concentration of teaching-lane
objects this source has produced.

**This closes the source.** All 13 units are processed and the reconciliation in
§8 of `PASS_RUN.md` is now due; it is deliberately not done in this unit run,
since source closure is a separate step with its own checks and its own commit.

The variant onto `DRILL_read_code_with_text_comprehension_strategies` is the
cleanest `context` variant in the source. The seven strategies are unchanged and
the code being read is unchanged; what changes is who performs each pass and why.
Three of the seven move to the team entirely, because they require knowledge the
newcomer does not have — you cannot activate prior knowledge you lack, cannot rank
importance in a system you do not know, and cannot infer undocumented domain rules
you have never been told. That is a different enough execution to be worth
recording and not a different enough decision to be a second drill.

One deliberate non-object: the seven strategies could have been rewritten as an
onboarding-specific drill. They were not, because the foundation from u05 is
already the canonical statement of the method and a second copy would drift from
it. The variant points at the same drill from the onboarding context instead.
