## Notes

Placement: the transfer and learning material went to
`foundations/deliberate-practice` alongside the u03 objects, and the misconception
material to `foundations/code-comprehension`. The split follows the chapter's own
two halves — the first is about acquiring a language, the second about faulty
beliefs operating while you read and write code. Nothing went to
`foundations/problem-solving`; that topic is about choosing a representation, and
this chapter is about knowledge carried in from elsewhere.

Both variants land on u06 objects, which is the first time a unit has fed straight
back into its immediate predecessor. That is a property of the source rather than a
coincidence — chapter 7 explicitly reopens the snowman puzzle from chapter 6 and
supplies the inhibition mechanism chapter 6 stated without explaining. Rather than
write a second pattern restating the first, the sharper method was absorbed.

Two collisions were checked and left as `related_to`.
`PAT_beware_assumptions_avoid_or_enforce` (gcbc u09) is about assumptions you bake
into code you write, remedied by removing or enforcing and renaming; the new
verification pattern is about assumptions you hold about code you read, remedied by
externalizing the belief. Different decision, different repair.
`DRILL_elaborate_a_new_concept_against_known_ones` (u03) already covers the
cross-language question at the level of a single concept, so the new comparison
drill was scoped to run once over a whole language before learning starts and to
output a trap map — a different target skill, cross-linked rather than merged.
