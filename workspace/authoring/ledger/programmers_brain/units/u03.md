## Notes

Placement: the five retained objects went to a new `foundations/deliberate-practice`
topic rather than to `foundations/code-comprehension`, where u01 and u02 material
lives. Retrieval on the six existing comprehension patterns showed all of them
govern reading code in the moment — scope, chunking, beacons, tracing, confusion
diagnosis, token verification. Nothing there covers deliberately acquiring the
long-term knowledge that reading depends on, which is a different learner
decision and belongs in its own topic.

The one collision worth recording is with
`PAT_calibrate_code_reading_scope_to_reader_knowledge`, which answers the same
underlying situation — required concepts are not in the reader's LTM — with the
opposite method. That produced the variant row rather than a rejection, per the
decision-versus-method check: same decision, different durable method, real
tradeoff between them.

Rejections here are heavier than the retained count because the chapter carries a
lot of cognitive-science background (encoding, schemata, network storage,
Ebbinghaus's apparatus, Bartlett's folk tale). All of it grounds the retained
objects and none of it changes what a practitioner does on its own.
