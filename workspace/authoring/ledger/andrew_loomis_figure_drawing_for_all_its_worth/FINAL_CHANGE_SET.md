- one-card-per-muscle, feature, fold, or garment-construction fact;

## Reserved follow-up

Loomis's head and hand material in this general figure book is retained only as a bridge pass. Dedicated Loomis head and hand sources should be processed separately and may deepen, replace, or specialize the provisional u11 variants.

## Consolidation result

Loomis materially strengthens the Stage 1 → Stage 2 handoff, observational measurement, locomotion/support design, purpose-driven study density, rhythmic contour continuity, and costume interpretation. His strongest contribution is not a new anatomy system but a set of practical, adjustable construction and observation procedures that integrate well with the existing Hogarth/Bridgman/Hampton foundation.
