## Delta map

Hampton establishes whether a pose's support and center of gravity are believable. Loomis adds a locomotion-specific mechanism: **forward travel repeatedly moves the body mass ahead of the current support, propels from behind, and catches that displacement with the next contact**. This explains why animated limbs can still look stationary when the major body mass has not actually entered the travel.
