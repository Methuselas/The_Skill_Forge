## Delta map

Loomis contributes a particularly clean **single-pose phase-selection diagnostic**. His “sweep” is the felt movement immediately before or after the depicted instant. The artist should understand the entire action before selecting the frame; an extreme often causes the viewer to complete the movement mentally, while some actions legitimately demand a suspended middle instant. This specializes the existing Hogarth action-sequence Drill rather than creating another gesture system.
