## Delta map

Loomis contributes two useful costume-specific refinements. First, not every fold comes from the body: **garment construction itself has intention** through seams, darts, gathers, pleats, bias, and fitted versus loose cutting. Second, drapery rendering should preserve the garment's local value unity rather than overmodeling every plane. Both extend existing Hampton/Gill architecture without requiring a separate costume system.
