## Delta map

Loomis's strongest u08 workflow delta is **purpose-first rendering**: decide what a study is for before deciding how finished, tonal, linear, or detailed it should become. His strongest rhythm delta is **follow-through across the solid**: one contour can be mentally carried through the form and picked up by another, giving separated edges a shared rhythmic plan without requiring a literal continuous outline.
