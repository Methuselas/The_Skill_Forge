## Delta map

Loomis's durable low-pose contribution is **support redistribution**. Once the figure leaves ordinary standing support, feet may become secondary while buttocks, thighs, knees, hands, elbows, or the chair/floor take load. Those new contacts should visibly affect the pose: a bracing arm can raise one shoulder, shift the hips toward the brace, relax the spine toward it, and compress weight-bearing soft tissue. This specializes Hampton's general support Pattern rather than creating a separate seated-figure system.
