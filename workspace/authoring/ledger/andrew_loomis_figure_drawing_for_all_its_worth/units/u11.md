## Delta map

This chapter is deliberately retained as a **bridge pass**, not the definitive Loomis head/hand extraction. Two compact procedures are distinct enough to keep now: the divided cranial-ball layout makes feature placement inherit one turned head structure, and the hand envelope delays digit separation until the whole hand's taper, direction, and proportion are established. Both are expected to be revisited when the dedicated Loomis head and hand sources are processed.
