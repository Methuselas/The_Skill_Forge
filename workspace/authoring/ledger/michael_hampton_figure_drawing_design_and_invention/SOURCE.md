## Unit scheme

The book is process-driven and cumulative. PASS therefore follows Hampton's major instructional chapters rather than splitting every named muscle subsection into its own unit.

1. u00 — Introduction + Gesture Drawing
2. u01 — Landmarks
3. u02 — Forms and Connections
4. u03 — Head Drawing
5. u04 — Anatomy: trunk/neck/shoulder design system
6. u05 — The Arm
7. u06 — Hands
8. u07 — The Leg
9. u08 — The Foot
10. u09 — Drapery + Notes on Light and Shadow + book-level consolidation

The anatomy chapter's repeated Gesture → Shape → Volume treatment will be evaluated as a method family before any regional anatomy is promoted separately.
