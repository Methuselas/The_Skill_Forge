The complete Hand chapter was read against the committed Dynamic Hands and Bridgman hand baseline. Most Hampton construction steps substantially reinforce existing palm, digit, thumb, wrist, proportion, and foreshortening mechanics. The durable delta is his contour-design distinction between firmer dorsal structure and softer palmar tissue, with the nail retained as a perspective-bearing terminal plane.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — finished finger design through hard/soft side opposition while preserving volume.
- REINFORCES — relational proportions, palm dome/wedge, thumb independence, knuckle chains, finger arcs, wrist integration, pinch/stretch, and overlap.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — Hampton's glove, roof-box palm, teardrop muscle, and straight/sphere/cylinder hand recipes are already covered more deeply by Dynamic Hands/Bridgman.
