The complete Head Drawing chapter was read through front, profile, back, and lighting examples. Hampton repeatedly stages the same hierarchy: solve large head placement and perspective first, then introduce proportions and secondary facial forms, and finally allow feature design to ride on the already-established three-dimensional structure.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — head construction with an explicit stage gate: shape/tilt/turn/jaw/perspective before proportions and features.
- SPECIALIZES — facial features inherit one shared head perspective rather than being placed as independent symbols.
- REINFORCES — cranial mass, jaw/facial attachment, wrapping lines, feature asymmetry, overlap, and construction-before-finish.
- REJECTS AS UNIVERSAL LAW — exact average proportion subdivisions and one fixed side-plane recipe remain Hampton method/reference.
- REINFORCES POLICY — detailed construction can be shortened or internalized once the spatial model is learned.
