The complete Foot chapter was read through functional design, arch structure, plantar padding, primitive construction, ankle/tarsus bridge, toes, process, and final line design. Hampton's durable additions are the explicit two-arch support model and a material-sensitive hard-top/soft-bottom contour rule; the remaining construction recipes reinforce the existing Hogarth/Bridgman foot architecture.
