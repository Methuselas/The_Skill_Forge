The complete Arm chapter was read through shoulder-girdle construction, upper-arm anatomy, forearm simplification, pronation/supination, elbow connection, process studies, and sphere/ellipse value exercises. Hampton consistently treats the shoulder as the spatial bridge from rib cage to arm and separates the arm's action, bone direction, volume, and anatomical design into successive but cumulative layers.

## Delta map

- SPECIALIZES — shoulder integration with an independent clavicle/scapula perspective bridge.
- SPECIALIZES — arm construction by separating lyrical gesture, bone-direction line, cylindrical volume, and asymmetrical anatomy.
- REINFORCES — Bridgman radius/ulna pronation-supination model, joint transitions, and Hampton u04 micro-gesture anatomy.
- REJECTS AS GRANULAR — individual arm/forearm muscle ellipses and rotator-cuff mnemonics remain source examples.
- REINFORCES — isolated limb studies still belong to the torso/shoulder system.
