The complete Leg chapter was read through pelvic/hip groups, thigh masses, knee-spanning structures, and lower-leg/calf examples. Hampton repeatedly returns to the same modern design idea: first solve the leg's gesture and cylinder perspective, then place simplified muscle masses asymmetrically around that volume according to activity rather than copying left/right or inner/outer bulges.

## Delta map

- REINFORCES — u04 anatomy micro-gesture process, pelvic/hip attachment, Bridgman thigh/knee/lower-leg structure, and cylinder-based limb perspective.
- REJECTS AS GRANULAR — abductors, adductors, IT tract, sartorius, hamstrings, quadriceps, and calf mnemonics remain source examples.
