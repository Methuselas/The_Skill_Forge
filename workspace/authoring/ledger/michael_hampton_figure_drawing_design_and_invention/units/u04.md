The trunk/neck/shoulder anatomy span was read as one method family. Hampton repeatedly uses the same sequence across sternomastoid, pectoralis, trapezius, deltoid, abdominals, obliques, serratus, erector spinae, and latissimus: identify useful action, reduce the group to a designed shape, place it on established landmarks, alter it through active/passive deformation, and give it volume by wrapping it over the parent perspective.

## Delta map

- NEW PATTERN — anatomy as a nested micro-gesture process: shape, placement, action, and perspective on established parent forms.
- REINFORCES — shoulder-girdle mechanics, neck attachment, compression/stretch, surface overlap, and perspective inheritance.
- REJECTS AS GRANULAR — individual Hampton muscle mnemonics remain source examples, not separate runtime cards.
- FORWARD — anatomy-plane-to-light/value handoff to u09 for final cross-chapter evaluation.
