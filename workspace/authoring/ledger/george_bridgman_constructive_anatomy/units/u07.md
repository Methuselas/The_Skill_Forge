The Thigh/Knee span was read through the front, rear, inner, and outer lower-limb plates. Bridgman describes the thigh as an inward-inclined, multi-plane mass rather than a uniform cylinder, then gives the knee a distinct beveled block with the patella tied to the angle of the thigh and leg and the popliteal hollow organized by posterior tendons. fileciteturn6file1
