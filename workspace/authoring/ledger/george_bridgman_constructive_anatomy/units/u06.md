The pelvis/hip section was read with the preceding cage-into-hips wedging plate and the opening lower-limb mechanics. Bridgman treats the iliac crest as the stable surface landmark while the hip musculature changes markedly, and he locates the femoral shaft laterally from the deep socket through a neck/trochanter relationship before the femur converges toward the knee.
