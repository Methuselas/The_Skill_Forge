## Consolidation decisions

- RETAIN all 19 Bridgman variants. No two variants collapse without losing a distinct artist-facing decision.
- KEEP `VAR_bridgman_mass_plane_line_wedging` as the global construction-language variant. Regional wedge/interlock variants remain because they encode specific mechanics rather than merely restating the doctrine.
- KEEP the two torso variants distinct:
  - three-plane block opposition solves relative orientation of major masses;
  - split-torso wedge solves how deformation is mediated through the mobile waist.
- KEEP the two finger-rhythm variants distinct:
  - thumb-centered arcs organize several digits as a coordinated group;
  - stepped/wedged joints organize articulation inside each digit.
- KEEP thigh and lower-leg variants together on the leg-rhythm Pattern because they operate on different segments and different asymmetries.
- KEEP elbow and knee variants together on the joint-transition Pattern because each is a local specialization of the same general transition problem.
- RESOLVE the u01 wrist forward into u02: no additional wrist-form card was earned; only wrist-flexion/finger-closure coupling survived.
- RESOLVE the u03 forearm/elbow forward into u04: radius/ulna rotation and opposed elbow masses survived.
- RESOLVE the u08 ankle forward into u09: outer-low/inner-high foot-and-ankle asymmetry survived.
- RESOLVE the opening pressure/tension/power hypothesis as explanatory theory only. It does not earn a separate runtime object after regional review.
- REJECT historical racial/evolutionary facial-angle claims from runtime authority.
- REJECT one-card-per-muscle, one-card-per-feature, and one-card-per-landmark expansion throughout the book.
