## Delta map

- **0 NEW STANDALONE OBJECTS.** The current hand foundation is already stronger and more operational after Dynamic Hands, Constructive Anatomy, Loomis, Bammes, and the anatomy passes.
- **2 RETAINED VARIANTS.** One specializes passive contact/load by seating a relaxed hand on the little-finger-side heel before broader pressure spreads; one adds a within-digit construction sequence from knuckle outward without replacing the existing across-finger coupled spiral.
- **CONSTRUCTIVE ANATOMY DUPLICATION FILTER.** Bridgman's action/inaction sides, six construction-line route, thumb-centered finger arcs, wrist/finger coupling, and radius-around-ulna turning have already been evaluated and retained from *Constructive Anatomy*. This book strongly corroborates them but does not earn duplicate variants.
- **DYNAMIC HANDS DUPLICATION FILTER.** Palm arch/scoop, thumb opposition, digit interlacing, surface stress, age, functional grasp, and general hand gesture remain stronger in the dedicated Dynamic Hands stack.
- **DATED TYPOLOGY FILTER.** Claims that infer race, sex, class, occupation, morality, or stable psychological character from categorical hand types remain source-history material only.
- **REFERENCE ONLY.** Named carpal anatomy, muscle origin/insertion lists, exact crease/pad routes, and many finished plates remain useful evidence and visual study material but do not alter runtime construction decisions.
- **SCHEMA HYGIENE.** The touched finger-flexion candidate already carried five tags against the vNext four-tag ceiling; the redundant `movement` tag was removed while adding the variant. This is a schema repair only, not a source-derived rule change.
