## Notes

All seven patterns are new foundations under the existing `avoiding-surprises`
topic, each cross-linked (rel: related_to) to the Ch.1 foundation
PAT_match_caller_mental_model. Per PASS_SCHEMA §1, they stay foundation/general/none
because their rules need no language/tool constraint (null safety, C++ output
parameters, and switch exhaustiveness warnings are illustrations, not constraints
on the rule).

Magic values: the Ch.4 unit ledger (units/u04.md) explicitly rejected/deferred the
magic-value pattern for Ch.6 to own, because the author defers detailed treatment
to this chapter. PAT_avoid_returning_magic_values is that owner, grounded in the
getAge/minValue examples, and cross-links back to Ch.4 PAT_dont_hide_errors and
Ch.2 PAT_prefer_null_safety_or_optionals without duplicating them.

  implementation) folded into one PAT_use_null_object_pattern_only_when_safe; the
  string-as-chars vs string-as-ID distinction is captured in its Do/Don't.

  enum) folded into PAT_handle_enums_exhaustively; the default-case and cross-system
  caveats live in its Do/Don't/Notes.
