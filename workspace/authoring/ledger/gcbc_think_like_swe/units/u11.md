## Notes

All seven patterns and the AP are new foundations under the existing `testing`
topic; each cross-links to a Ch.10 testing pattern or the Ch.1
PAT_design_for_testability foundation. Per PASS_SCHEMA §1 they stay
foundation/general/none because their rules need no language/tool constraint
(NUnit/Truth/Jasmine matchers, BeforeEach/AfterEach blocks, parameterized-test
attributes are illustrations, not constraints on the rule).

These practices specialize the Ch.10 principles: PAT_dont_expose_privates_for_testing
is the testing case of Ch.2 PAT_dont_widen_api_for_reuse_or_testing (which flagged
the "visible for testing" trap) and cross-links back; PAT_split_code_to_make_it_testable
routes to Ch.2 class-sizing; PAT_use_shared_test_setup_carefully relates to Ch.9
global-state; PAT_inject_dependencies_for_testability is the testability motive for
Ch.8 dependency injection. The AP_write_a_unit_test_suite is a capstone sequencing
the two testing chapters' principles and practices into one workflow.
