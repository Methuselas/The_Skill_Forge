## Notes

All seven patterns are new foundations under the existing `modularity` topic,
each cross-linked (rel: related_to) to the Ch.1 foundation PAT_design_modular_interfaces.
Per PASS_SCHEMA §1 they stay foundation/general/none because their rules need no
language/tool constraint (DI frameworks, Kotlin delegation, mixins/traits,
HttpResponse types, checked exceptions are illustrations, not constraints on the
rule).

This chapter is the DI/interfaces deep-dive Ch.2 previewed: PAT_use_dependency_injection
and PAT_depend_on_interfaces_not_concrete_classes cross-link back to the Ch.2
AP_refactor_monolithic_class_into_layers and PAT_use_interfaces_for_swappable_layers.
The two implementation-detail-leak patterns are the return-type/exception
specializations of Ch.2 PAT_expose_clean_api_hide_implementation; the exception one
is the object Ch.4's u04.md forward-referenced (8.6/8.7) and cross-links to
PAT_prefer_explicit_error_signaling_for_recoverable_errors.

Merges / rejections (no separate rows):
- 8.1.3 (design with DI in mind / static cling) folded into PAT_use_dependency_injection's
  Do, since it is the provider-side of the same inject-the-dependency decision.
- 8.3.3 (genuine is-a; fragile base class, diamond, single-inheritance; interfaces
  for hierarchy + composition for reuse; mixins/traits) folded into
  PAT_prefer_composition_over_inheritance's Do/Don't/Notes.
- The DI-framework, delegation, Law-of-Demeter, and data-object sidebars are context,
  captured in the relevant Notes, not standalone objects.
- No AP: the composition-over-inheritance refactor is captured as a drill; the
  Ch.2 AP_refactor_monolithic_class_into_layers already covers the DI/interface
  workflow at the class-decomposition level.

Cross-links resolve against Ch.1 (modularity), Ch.2 (interfaces, clean-api, refactor
AP), Ch.4 (explicit-signaling) and the new objects. Chapter 9 (reusability, global
state, generics) will build further on dependency injection.
