## Notes

All seven patterns and the AP are new foundations under the existing `hard-to-misuse`
topic, each cross-linked (rel: related_to) to the Ch.1 foundation
PAT_make_code_hard_to_misuse. Per PASS_SCHEMA §1 they stay foundation/general/none
because their rules need no language/tool constraint (final/const/readonly keywords,
ImmutableList libraries, java.time/Noda/chrono, Kotlin data classes are illustrations,
not constraints on the rule).

This chapter is the immutability home that Ch.3 (PAT_make_misuse_impossible_by_
removing_invalid_states) and Ch.6 (PAT_avoid_unexpected_side_effects,
PAT_dont_mutate_input_parameters) forward-referenced; PAT_prefer_immutable_objects
cross-links back to those. The dedicated-type pattern is the misuse-focused sibling
of Ch.5's readability-focused PAT_replace_primitives_with_descriptive_types (which
Ch.5 flagged to link here); they cross-link and are kept distinct (readability vs
type-safety/misuse).

Merges / rejections (no separate rows):
- 7.1.2 + 7.1.3 (construction-only, builder, copy-on-write): construction-only is
  the core immutability pattern (PAT_prefer_immutable_objects); builder + copy-on-write
  folded into one PAT_keep_immutable_with_builder_or_copy_on_write (two methods, one
  decision: stay immutable with optional/modified values).
- 7.2.2 + 7.2.3 (defensive copy, immutable structures) folded into
  PAT_make_immutability_deep as two methods with a preference for immutable structures.
- 7.3.2 (pair types) folded into PAT_use_dedicated_types_over_general_ones as the
  intermediate rung that still fails.
- 7.5.2 "when deriving is expensive" (lazy caching) folded into
  PAT_single_source_of_truth_for_data's Do/Notes.
- The poka-yoke / EUHM framing (p. 170-171), data-objects and const-correctness
  sidebars are context, captured in the relevant Notes, not standalone objects.

Cross-links resolve against Ch.1 (hard-to-misuse), Ch.2 (decomposition), Ch.3
(make-misuse-impossible), Ch.5 (descriptive-types), Ch.6 (mutating-inputs) and the
new objects. Chapter 8 (modularity: dependency injection, interfaces) will
specialize further; chapter 8.6/8.7 (leaking implementation details) relate to the
single-source-of-truth and dedicated-type material.
