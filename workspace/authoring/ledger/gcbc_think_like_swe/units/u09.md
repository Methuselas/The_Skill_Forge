## Notes

All five patterns are new foundations under the existing `reusability` topic, each
cross-linked (rel: related_to) to the Ch.1 foundation
PAT_make_code_reusable_and_generalizable. Per PASS_SCHEMA §1 they stay
foundation/general/none because their rules need no language/tool constraint (null
coalescing operator, generics/templates syntax are illustrations, not constraints).

Cross-chapter placement:
- The global-state pattern builds on Ch.8 PAT_use_dependency_injection (dependency-
  inject shared state) and cross-links to it.
- The assumption pattern's enforcement half relates to Ch.3
  PAT_enforce_contracts_at_runtime_with_checks (assertion vs explicit signal by
  recoverability) and cross-links.
- The focused-parameters pattern names the deliberate tension with Ch.8
  PAT_encapsulate_related_data_together (narrow vs pass-the-whole-object) and
  cross-links; the judgment clause reconciles them.
- The default-values pattern is the reusability-angle sibling of the Ch.4/Ch.6
  default/magic-value patterns (low-level defaults harm reuse) — kept distinct;
  captured in its Notes rather than cross-linked to avoid over-linking.
