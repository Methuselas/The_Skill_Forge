## Delta map

- **1 new Drill in u06.** `DRILL_audit_foreshortening_with_nine_depth_checks` converts the chapter's nine-principle organization into a bounded correction exercise for the specific moment when a deep-space figure looks wrong but the artist cannot yet identify which spatial control failed.
- **NO NEW PRINCIPLE CARDS.** Principles 1, 2, 4, 5, and 9 are already stronger in the committed Dynamic Figure Drawing foreshortening/tone stack; Principle 3 reinforces the articulated-chain → mass stage order.
- **CORROBORATED, NOT DUPLICATED.** Principle 6 repeats the Dynamic Figure Drawing Chapter 5 pivot/reach method, while Principles 7-8 repeat that source's Chapter 6 view-projection and figure-derived scene-calibration methods. This source adds corroborating evidence, not a different learner decision.
- **PERSPECTIVE BOUNDARY.** Hogarth's perspective tracks remain a figure-construction aid. The dedicated perspective family controls actual scene-perspective geometry when precise construction is required.
- **APPLICATION PLATES.** The closing anatomy-labeled figures demonstrate that the depth controls can carry through to resolved anatomy, but they do not introduce a tenth principle or a new anatomy method.
