## Delta map

- **1 new Drill in u03.** `DRILL_compare_figure_styles_by_context_and_form` preserves the chapter's durable comparison method: separate individual manner from broader convention, compare the same observable figure attributes across works, use documented context to interpret purposeful stylization, and investigate persistence/combination/transition rather than forcing hard bins.
- **REINFORCED** — dynamic figure movement, proportion as a scaffold rather than a universal, connected masses, contour, tone/edge control, gesture, and deep-space construction all have stronger existing operational cards.
- **FILTERED** — Hogarth's broad historical causal narratives, judgments about cultures/eras, and synesthetic/kinesthetic/cryptesthetic/cerebresthetic taxonomy remain source-specific interpretation rather than runtime doctrine.
- **ROUTED FORWARD** — the closing scan-p. 70 Hogarth torso plate to u05, where anatomy and surface-form relationships are explicitly taught.
