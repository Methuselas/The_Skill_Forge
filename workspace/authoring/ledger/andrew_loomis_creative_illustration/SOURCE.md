## Source boundary

This source is Andrew Loomis's integrated treatment of creative illustration, not merely a composition manual. The book assumes substantial drawing ability and develops the whole pictorial effort through line, tone, color, storytelling, idea generation, professional illustration fields, experiment, and continuing study. It belongs in the Composition curriculum because whole-picture organization and pictorial purpose are central, but useful non-composition material remains eligible when it contributes a genuine Art capability.

The table of contents explicitly marks illustration pages separately in italics. Source-native units therefore follow instructional ownership and actual page transitions rather than creating one unit for every TOC line. A physical-order anomaly is also preserved: the Part Five divider appears on physical p. 184 before several late Part Four instructional pages; that divider is treated as structural front matter for Part Five, not as a reason to reassign the surrounding Part Four content.
