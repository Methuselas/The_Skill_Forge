## Delta map

- **NEW:** an animal-specific Stage 1 action-line Pattern that routes one governing sweep through the neck, trunk, tail, and selected limbs while allowing deliberate accents and counterdirections.
- **VARIANT:** the existing encompassing-contour Pattern gains Hultgren's animal-specific straight/curve, reversal, repetition, and opposition treatment for avoiding monotonous line flow.
- **VARIANT:** the existing center-of-gravity Pattern gains an animal-action support-handoff context that separates reaching limbs from the contact actually receiving body weight.
- **REINFORCES:** takeoff exaggeration is retained as one whole-body extension example rather than split into a micro-card.
- **FILTERED:** heavy-line weight shorthand and the mandatory horizontal/vertical/diagonal maxim are not promoted as universal drawing doctrine.
