## Delta map

- **NEW:** a horse-specific skeletal construction Pattern that extends the general quadruped block into scapular, pelvic, and limb-chain anatomy.
- **NEW:** an inside-out horse anatomy Drill that overlays the skeleton through muscle and surface studies.
- **NEW:** a force-reversal Drill built from Hultgren's forward-load/backward-load exercise.
- **NEW:** a horse-gait Pattern that reads trot/canter sequences by support, push, recovery, and preparation roles without treating the source as a complete biomechanics chart.
- **NEW:** a comparative animal-conformation Pattern using shared boxes and structural landmarks to preserve differences between related types, ages, or breeds.
- **NEW:** an animal-caricature Pattern that exaggerates coherent type-defining proportion axes while preserving articulation.
- **VARIANT:** horse eye placement is added to the existing animal-head construction Pattern through skull sectioning and bone-flow checks.
- **VARIANT:** natural zebra stripes become an animal-specific surface-wrap transfer inside the existing surface-path Drill.
- **FILTERED/REINFORCEMENT:** literal rib-cage elongation, universal juvenile awkwardness, fixed breed/species traits, and the isolated tooth-angle claim are not promoted as doctrine; perspective boxes, action-line roughing, plane blocking, and general deformation already have stronger canonical ownership.
