## Delta map

- **NEW:** one animal-motion observation Pattern that uses successive slow-motion/frame-by-frame reference to expose extreme positions and phase relationships that are hard to see at normal speed.
- **VARIANT:** Hultgren's deer-specific caricature treatment is absorbed into the existing type-defining animal caricature Pattern: taper and clean sweep for adult grace, short-body/long-leg contrast for a fawn, and broader chest/neck plus antler flare for a stag.
- **REINFORCES:** the three-part quadruped block, governing action line, moving-pivot/overlapping-arc analysis, and comparative animal proportion already have stronger canonical ownership.
- **FILTERED/DEFERRED:** the thin lower-leg anatomy note, fixed stag/doe differences, antler age/type claims, and fawn knock-kneed statement are not promoted into anatomy doctrine from this stylized section alone.
