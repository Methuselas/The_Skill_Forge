## Delta map

- **NEW:** one general Stage 0 composition Pattern moves Hultgren's line-of-action idea from a single animal to the whole picture: repeat or oppose directional paths across animals and setting, carry the viewer through the group, and use a deliberate accent or redirection to prevent monotony and focus attention.
- **VARIANT:** the existing Stage 0 thumbnail-search Pattern gains Hultgren's effect-first camera route: decide the desired mass/drama effect, choose the viewpoint that magnifies it, lock the principal pose, then organize the rest of the picture to intensify the same read.
- **REINFORCES:** individual animal action lines, straight-versus-curve opposition, and perspective viewpoint skills remain owned by their existing Patterns; u21 coordinates them at picture level rather than duplicating them.
- **FILTERED/BOUNDED:** foreground grooves, vertical anchors, head turns, and accents are retained as methods inside the new composition Pattern. Hultgren's vertical/horizontal/diagonal statement is not normalized into a universal checklist.
