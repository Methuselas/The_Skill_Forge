## Delta map

- **NEW:** a sequential animal-motion Pattern for arcs around pivots that themselves travel with the body, plus overlapping paths for connected secondary parts.
- **NEW:** an animal-impact Pattern that stages extension/bunching, first-contact reaction, reversal/rebound, and progressive recovery as the event loses energy.
- **REINFORCES:** u03's reverse-direction and whole-body extension ideas, without duplicating them as separate cards.
- **FILTERED:** Hultgren's broad weight/stress vocabulary and the rubber-ball comparison remain explanatory support rather than independent doctrine.
- **BOUNDED:** the horse fall is kept as animation evidence, not normalized into exact gait, trauma, or species-wide biomechanics.
