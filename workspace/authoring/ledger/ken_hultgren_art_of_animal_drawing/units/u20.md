## Delta map

- **NEW:** one bear-specific construction Pattern organizes compact mass with three body units/breaks, a high-withers neck-to-back interruption, and enough straight structural passages to avoid a uniformly rounded block.
- **VARIANTS:** animal gesture gains Hultgren's rear-leg-to-front-leg follow-through route for moving a compact bear mass; animal caricature gains adult-bulk, cute-small-bear, comic-mass, and stylized-polar proportion routes.
- **REINFORCES:** the section's diagonal/curve choices, head studies, rear/front construction views, and quick action sketches reinforce existing line, head, and quadruped ownership.
- **FILTERED/DEFERRED:** broad brown-bear, cub-age, pigeon-toe, polar-bear morphology, and fight/action claims are not promoted into fixed biology or biomechanics from this source alone.
