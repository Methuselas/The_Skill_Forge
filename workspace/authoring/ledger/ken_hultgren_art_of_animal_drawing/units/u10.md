## Delta map

- **REINFORCES:** proportion checking, multi-view construction, generic animal-head blocking, and related-type comparison already have stronger canonical ownership.
- **FILTERED/DEFERRED:** the reaching-ground pose, baby/adult age comparison, and isolated caricature points remain source evidence rather than inferred biomechanics, age doctrine, or micro-cards.
