## Delta map

- **NEW:** an animal-specific quadruped body scaffold rooted in a dorsal axis and three large body regions.
- **NEW:** a projecting mammal-head construction that explicitly separates cranial base, nasal length, and muzzle.
- **NEW:** a deliberate-practice route from skeletal pivots to main forms across different quadruped proportions.
- **SPECIALIZES / VARIANT:** the existing perspective-box pattern gains Hultgren's temporary organic-pose angle-check context.
- **REINFORCES:** hidden-form continuation is already owned by the canonical overlap pattern.
- **FILTERED:** stance generalizations and the literal rib-cage-elongation statement are retained only as source evidence, not doctrine.
