## Delta map

- **NEW:** one feline-head construction Pattern that moves from skull/cranial groundwork through a perspective-aware midline and broad planes before features, fur, or tone.
- **VARIANT:** Hultgren's "weave the forms" route is absorbed into the existing animal-gesture Pattern as a mass-connection method for a feline pose whose action line is clear but whose blocked forms still read as separate pieces.
- **VARIANT:** the animal-caricature Pattern gains a feline route contrasting elongated/tapered panther design with mass-concentrated comic-lion design.
- **REINFORCES:** three-part quadruped blocking, moving-pivot action analysis, comparative proportion, skeleton/surface study, form-following tone, wet-brush pressure control, and natural markings already have stronger ownership.
- **FILTERED/DEFERRED:** the claimed hind-leg spring mechanism, categorical lioness/male difference, upper-lid/nose specifics, lion-jump contact mechanics, and universal kitten proportions are not promoted into biological doctrine from this stylized source alone.
