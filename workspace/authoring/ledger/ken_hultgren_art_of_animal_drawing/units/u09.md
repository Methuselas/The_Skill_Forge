## Delta map

- **NEW:** one bovine construction Pattern organizes cattle from a strong dorsal route into a deep hanging trunk with readable shoulder/pelvic anchors; the source's "weight hangs from the spinal column" idea is explicitly retained as a drawing metaphor, not literal biomechanics.
- **VARIANT:** the animal-caricature Pattern gains Hultgren's cattle route: deep/angular cow bulk or overbuilt bull forequarters opposed by reduced/thin legs, with horns, jowls, muzzle size, and tail tuft kept subordinate to the large structural contrast.
- **REINFORCES:** inside-out skeleton study, generic animal-head construction, governing action line, moving-pivot action analysis, and comparative proportion already have stronger canonical ownership.
- **FILTERED/DEFERRED:** cattle limb/hoof studies, categorical bull conformation, and isolated feature accents remain source evidence rather than separate objects or universal anatomy claims.
