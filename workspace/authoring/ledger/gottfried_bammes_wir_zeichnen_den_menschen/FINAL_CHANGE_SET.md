## Explicit non-promotions

- fixed male/female/child proportional canons as universal laws;
- one geometry system as the mandatory figure mannequin;
- one-card-per-bone, muscle, joint, facial feature, or anatomical label;
- anatomy facts that do not change a drawing decision;
- duplicate gesture, center-of-gravity, cross-section, perspective, overlap, or hand-mechanics cards already represented more deeply elsewhere;
- medium-specific pencil, brush, hatching, or smudging recipes;
- Bammes's specific seated-gesture readings as factual psychological diagnosis of a real person's inner state.

## Goldfinger verification boundary

Eliot Goldfinger's *Human Anatomy for Artists* was used only to verify anatomical plausibility in anatomy-heavy units. Goldfinger supplied no Bammes PASS deltas and is not provenance for any object or variant in this change set.

## Consolidation result

Bammes materially strengthens the mature figure system by repeatedly converting anatomy into **drawable mechanics**: whole-mass proportion before contour, body-type ensembles rather than isolated canons, load-chain contrapposto, joint mechanics, three-point foot support, hidden-skeleton projection, deformable soft walls, twistable limb scaffolds, palm-caused digit orientation, skull-to-living-head transfer, and final whole-pose support/reclining reconciliation.

The final chapter confirms the architecture rather than replacing it. Its distinct additions are position-specific: weight-bearing contacts must visibly affect the whole pose, seated torsos should articulate through a chain of masses rather than one smooth bend, and reclining figures can be stabilized by a longitudinal/cross-axis depth scaffold before local construction.
