## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Goldfinger was consulted only for anatomical verification. He independently confirms that the distal radius rotates around the relatively fixed ulna during pronation/supination, changing the wrist's surface planes, and that metacarpals two through five radiate distally while forming a transverse arch that contributes to the concavity of the palm. These checks support Bammes's mechanical plausibility but do not supply any Bammes candidate.

## Delta map

- VARIANT — turn the understood radius/ulna mechanism into a **drawable twistable quadrangular forearm form** so pronation/supination changes planes through the member.
- VARIANT — preserve Bammes's **cross-sectional arm architecture**: upper-arm flexor/extensor masses stack more in depth; forearm functional masses spread more in width and recede before the wrist.
- VARIANT — let the **palm vault cause the digit fan**: radiating metacarpal structure gives each long finger a different starting spatial orientation before local articulation.
- REINFORCES — Bridgman radius-around-ulna mechanics, Vilppu elbow landmark triangle, Hampton arm sequencing, Dynamic Hands palm dome, thumb opposition, hand measurement, stress landmarks, coupled fingers, gesture, and functional grasp.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another elbow card, wrist-range card, hand-proportion card, thumb card, or hand-gesture Drill.
