## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Eliot Goldfinger's *Human Anatomy for Artists* was consulted only to check the anatomical plausibility of Bammes's mechanisms. Goldfinger independently supports the patella's integration with the quadriceps/patellar-ligament chain and its tracking between the femoral condyles, and describes the longitudinal/transverse foot arches plus the talus between tibia/fibula and the calcaneus. No Bammes candidate is grounded in Goldfinger and no Goldfinger-only fact is promoted here.

## Delta map

- VARIANT — construct the knee from flexion mechanics: distal-femur plane/block logic, condylar rolling/opening, and patella integrated with the extensor chain.
- VARIANT — anchor the foot's arch system over a three-region support plan: heel + great-toe ball + little-toe ball.
- VARIANT — infer anatomical function from joint axis and pull direction, then group named muscles into larger living architectural masses; use cross-sections to verify plane meetings.
- REINFORCES — Bridgman knee block and lower-leg asymmetry, Hampton leg ellipse-tumble/dual arches, Hogarth support wedge, Vilppu construction sequence, and general construction-over-copying doctrine.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another generic cylinder-leg drill, foot-wedge card, arch card, or muscle catalog.
