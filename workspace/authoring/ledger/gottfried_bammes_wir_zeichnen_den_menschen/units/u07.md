## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Eliot Goldfinger's *Human Anatomy for Artists* was consulted only as anatomical verification. Goldfinger independently presents the skull from multiple views with the brow ridge, orbit, nasal bones, zygomatic arch, mastoid, mandible, and other bony landmarks; he also describes the eye as a curved eyeball/lid system whose surface plane depends on the globe, and the sternocleidomastoid as a straplike muscle spiraling down the side of the neck from the mastoid region toward sternum/clavicle. These checks support the plausibility of Bammes's skull-to-surface and neck descriptions. No Goldfinger-only fact is promoted into the Bammes change set.

## Delta map

- NEW DRILL — construct a spatial skull, draw through hidden bilateral reference points, then build the living head over it on a registered layer; the skull must generate the surface rather than be retrofitted afterward.
- VARIANT — establish portrait/head likeness from the global proportional envelope before features, especially profile height/depth and the sequence of protrusions/recessions; normative ratios remain provisional.
- REINFORCES — Hogarth cranial/facial mass construction and neck seating, Bridgman head perspective cage and cervical mechanics, Hampton head-stage gate and feature-perspective inheritance, Loomis divided-ball feature placement.
- HOLDS FOR DEDICATED HEAD STUDY — detailed eye, ear, mouth, local facial anatomy, and portrait-medium material. These are useful but should not preempt the planned specialist head sources.
