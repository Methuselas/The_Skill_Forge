## Delta map

- VARIANT — one-leg unloading is a whole-body causal chain: support reduction → pelvic shift/drop → spinal and shoulder compensation → loaded/free-side form contrast.
- VARIANT — locomotion is mechanically committed when the center of mass leaves the current support and must be caught; use this to distinguish actual travel from Bammes's `Scheinbewegung`.
- REINFORCES — Hampton center-of-gravity design, Loomis forward-travel balance, Vilppu total-action analysis, and tools-not-rules.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another generic gesture/brush drill, movable-manikin exercise, or medium-selection card.

## Translation notes

Bammes's `Standbein` is the load-bearing/support leg and `Spielbein` the unloaded/free leg. `Restunterstützung` refers to the remaining support after one foot is removed from load. `Scheinbewegung` is treated here as apparent/represented movement that can still remain statically supportable, contrasted with locomotion that commits the body beyond its current base.
