## Goldfinger verification — reference only, not PASS provenance

Goldfinger's *Human Anatomy for Artists* was consulted only to verify Bammes's anatomical premises. Goldfinger independently describes the rib cage and pelvis as rigid regions separated by a soft muscular body wall that accommodates their opposition by stretching, contracting, folding, and bulging; he also describes the shoulder apparatus as sitting and gliding on the rib cage. This confirms the anatomical plausibility of Bammes's exercise and deformation logic without contributing PASS deltas.

## Delta map

- NEW DRILL — project hidden rib-cage and pelvic skeleton forms into the living torso, working axes/simple solids → skeletal differentiation → live surface from the inside out.
- VARIANT — make torso soft tissue visibly answer compression, stretch, and torsion: bunch/fold across shortening, thin/reveal structure under stretch, and redirect into diagonal tension under twist.
- REINFORCES — Hampton/Bridgman shoulder-girdle mechanics, rib-cage/pelvis opposition, functional muscle grouping, cross-sections, landmarks, and construction-over-copying.
- REJECTS AS DUPLICATE — another shoulder-yoke card, another torso-mass Pattern, or another generic cross-section Drill.
