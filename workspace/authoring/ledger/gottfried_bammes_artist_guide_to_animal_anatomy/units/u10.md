## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_read_quadruped_locomotion_from_support_swing_and_suspension_phases` generalizes gait analysis beyond the existing horse-specific Hultgren owner: mark actual contacts, distinguish support from swing/recovery, track support geometry, and note suspension before restoring anatomy.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_stage_quadruped_springing_action_through_gather_push_suspension_and_reception` treats leap/jump/bound motion as a whole articulated event from preload through reception, with dog/horse/cat differences preserved rather than flattened into one formula.
- **OWNERSHIP RELINK:** `PAT_read_horse_gait_as_support_push_and_recovery_cycle` now specializes the new general quadruped gait Pattern, which in turn specializes moving-pivot/overlapping-arc analysis.
- **DEFERRED:** the obvious phase-strip/contact-diagram exercise waits for u11 §4.3, where Bammes explicitly enters exercises in repose and motion; strong spinal flexion/extension claims wait for the dedicated trunk unit u23.
- **FILTERED:** fixed hindleg-only propulsion, a fixed forward center-of-gravity model, categorical species ambling assignments, and literalized tension-spring/centrifugal language are not promoted as universal animal mechanics.
