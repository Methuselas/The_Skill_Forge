## Delta map

- **NEW Drill:** exact profile measurement becomes a calibration exercise: establish one modular framework, then repeat by estimation and use exact measurement only when uncertainty remains.
- **STRENGTHENED EXISTING VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_measure_animal_type_with_head_module_and_trunk_rectangle` now carries the full §3.2 sequence—front height, trunk length, rear height, rectangle, belly check, neck length, head direction—and its explicit release into estimation.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** the analogous/simultaneous method and framework-before-detail hierarchy are already owned by the u06 proportional-ensemble foundation.
- **FILTERED:** the named withers/croup/chest/pelvic extrema and profile preference stay as implementation/boundary details rather than micro-cards.
- **DEFERRED:** human/anthropoid proportional examples wait for u22's dedicated primate comparison; no source numeric ratio becomes a universal animal canon.
- **UNIT-MAP CORRECTION:** §3.2 continues into the left column of printed p. 29 before §3.3 begins on the same page. u07 therefore ends at that subsection boundary, while u08 starts from the §3.3 heading on the shared page.
