## Delta map

- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_order_whole_trunk_skeleton_by_support_function_and_optical_weight` extends the existing Stage 1 vertebral-and-limb framework into a more complete trunk-skeleton study. Once the main directions and pivots are correct, Bammes asks the artist to work architecturally: distinguish supporting and supported elements in space, decide the optical weight and relative value of detail, keep comparative impression and gesture alive, and simplify forms until their function and interaction in the working chain are clear.
- **REINFORCED:** u01's inside-out animal-construction foundation remains the broader owner of construction-before-surface and function-aware structure; u02 remains the owner of selective impressional quality; u23 remains the vertebral/backline owner; u24 remains the thorax plastic-core and changing-section owner.
- **BOUNDED:** “optical weight” is treated as a drawing hierarchy, not a biological load calculation. Bammes's cow in fig. 109 is a worked example of simplification, not a universal trunk-skeleton stencil.
- **DEFERRED:** the section's closing statement explicitly makes the complete trunk skeleton preparatory to understanding the disposition and extent of the trunk muscles; that musculature is owned by u26 §7.4 rather than extracted early here.
