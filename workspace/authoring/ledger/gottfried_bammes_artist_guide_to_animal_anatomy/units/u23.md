## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_propagate_vertebral_bend_and_twist_through_trunk` makes the vertebral route causally visible in the Stage 2 trunk: lateral bend compresses the concave thorax side and extends the convex side; torsion progressively turns sections around the axial route rather than skewing only the silhouette.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_trace_animal_vertebral_axis_before_contour` converts Bammes's p. 98 procedure into a fast Stage 1 practice route: judge view/rises/falls, then place one uninterrupted nose-to-tail axis and delay contour until the line carries both movement/expression and three-dimensional body orientation.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_model_animal_trunk_torsion_with_four_sided_block` preserves the fig. 105 progression from a rounded four-sided/Plasticine model through multiple views into organic animal form and a final reconstruction from imagination.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_use_backline_profile_as_type_signature` extends the existing animal-type proportion owner with Bammes's comparative backline check: highest point plus the sequence of withers, thoracolumbar, sacral/croup and tail rises/falls can identify type before local detail.
- **REINFORCED:** the u03 vertebral-framework Pattern remains the Stage 1 owner; u23 adds a dedicated practice route and the Stage 2 deformation consequence instead of replacing it. Hultgren's governing action-line Pattern stays related rather than foundational because Bammes's line is simultaneously expressive and anatomical/spatial.
- **DEFERRED:** detailed thorax shape/cross-section doctrine visible around fig. 101 is held for u24 §7.2, which has first claim on the thorax as a plastic core.
- **BOUNDED:** exact claims about segmental spinal mobility, nuchal-ligament mechanics, and individual species' neutral back profiles remain source-scoped; the retained cards extract drawing decisions rather than fixed zoological laws.
