## Delta map

- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_construct_complete_runner_foreleg_from_architectural_masses_and_sections` completes the full living-form runner-foreleg exercise deferred by u17. It sits after the u16 skeletal Drill and u17 functional-muscle Pattern, turning them into one architectural Stage 2 study with explicit shoulder geometry, cross-sections, dimensional contrasts, tendon hierarchy, and distal hard-form checks.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_shape_ruminant_foreleg_with_angular_masses_and_broad_carpus` extends the u17 runner-foreleg mass Pattern with Bammes's cow comparison: harder/angular prominent forms, a markedly broad carpus, and stronger bend around it when actually observed.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_seed_animal_invention_from_random_marks` extends the u12 improvisation Drill with a distinct chance-first method: broad accidental watercolor marks precede the animal idea, then pen crystallizes a suggested figure. The source explicitly keeps this play subordinate to internalized structural knowledge and repeated form study.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_iterate_imagined_animal_action_until_function_and_expression_agree` extracts the collapsing-horse sequence as a diagnosis-led invention rule: do not polish the wrong read; re-block, vary, exaggerate, and test until the function and expression agree, then let the working study carry form discovery before final emphasis.
- **REINFORCED:** u15's reference-free invention/visualization owner receives direct confirmation that imagination is a comprehension test and that half-assimilated anatomy should be taken back to study rather than rehearsed as a guessed form.
- **FILTERED:** historical master examples support the repertory/visualization argument but do not become style-imitation cards; cow/ruminant cues stay contextual rather than universal anatomy claims.
