## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_read_hindleg_muscle_groups_from_joint_axes_and_lines_of_pull` organizes hindleg musculature from the articulated skeleton outward: keep the joint cross-axes, trace the functional line of pull, account for multi-joint groups, and build grouped front/back masses before individual muscles.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_taper_mammal_limb_mass_from_proximal_muscle_to_distal_tendon` captures Bammes's major plastic hierarchy: muscle bellies concentrate near the trunk and upper pivots while distal regions become progressively more tendinous and skeletal, with the horse treated as an extreme rather than a universal template.
- **FILTERED/BOUNDED:** individual muscle examples remain evidence inside the functional grouping; the source's own simplification warning and species differences prevent a fixed horse atlas map from becoming doctrine.
- **DEFERRED:** fig. 60's living-hindleg assignment is explicitly only the first phase of a practical sequence. u15 §5.3 will decide whether it becomes a full Drill or a component of a larger construction/visualization exercise.
