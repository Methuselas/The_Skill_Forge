## Delta map

- **NEW Drill:** broad-medium proportion studies use the side and swivel of a wide tool to force trunk-to-limb relationships into a few concise overlapping shapes before detail.
- **NEW Drill:** jointed proportion blocks preserve segment size and fixed pivots while the same simplified animal is reposed into several movements.
- **NEW VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_establish_animal_proportion_with_broad_overlapping_shapes` adds a fast, flat-shape alternative to the exact head-module/trunk-rectangle method under `PAT_establish_animal_type_from_proportional_ensemble`.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** fig. 29 repeats the exact simultaneous-measurement procedure already retained in u07; the pivot-hole rule reinforces u03's articulated-framework ownership.
- **FILTERED:** tool-length scaling, keeping the chalk down, the swivel geometry, heavier near-leg printing, and optional printmaking effects remain method details inside the retained exercises rather than micro-cards.
- **BRIDGE FORWARD:** Bammes explicitly uses this unit to move from proportion into repose and locomotion; the next units can therefore deepen movement without re-extracting the proportion foundation.
