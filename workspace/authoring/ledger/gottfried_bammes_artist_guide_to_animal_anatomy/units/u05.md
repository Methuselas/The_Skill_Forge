## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** choose the texture process by how much edge control versus natural spread the intended surface can tolerate; use dry/stable, half-dry/broken, damp/running, or wet-on-wet behavior deliberately rather than fighting the medium.
- **NEW Drill:** compare one animal mass or body-cover patch across stable, broken, and running medium behaviors, then select the route that implies the surface most efficiently.
- **VARIANT:** the general material-rendering foundation gains Bammes's animal body-cover route: coat and bare skin are implied through selected medium-native evidence rather than literally copied as exhaustive hair, cracks, or markings.
- **ABSORBED:** sprayed/damp ink, half-dry opaque paint, wet-on-wet watercolor, wax resist, color modulation, and loaded-brush pressure/trailing/lift are retained as examples within the process decision instead of becoming one card each.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** treating body cover as a possible dominant impressional quality is already owned by u02's single-quality study Pattern.
- **BOUNDED:** Bammes's no-preliminary-sketch warning is kept only for direct texture processes whose freshness and unity would be harmed by a detailed underdrawing in another medium; it is not a universal ban on construction or underdrawing.
