## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_specialized_runner_foreleg_from_mobile_scapula_and_hinge_chain` builds the horse/ungulate runner foreleg from the mobile scapula through a fore-aft shoulder/humerus system into a comparatively constrained distal support chain. Horse-specific fusion, hoof, and load-ratio claims remain bounded examples rather than general mammal doctrine.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_rebuild_animal_limb_joint_form_from_articulation_and_axis` generalizes the repeated Bammes lesson that a joint's visible construction changes with state. u13 supplied hindleg evidence; u16 independently confirms it in scapular, carpal, and digital foreleg mechanics. `PAT_change_hindleg_joint_form_with_flexion_and_axis` is relinked beneath this new general owner while retaining its hindleg-specific cues.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_construct_runner_foreleg_from_directions_to_joint_axes` preserves fig. 68's actual study sequence: assess segment lengths/directions, add scapular and joint construction in three-quarter view, mark joint and spatial axes, isolate a difficult joint if needed, then compare an articulated/flexed state before musculature.
- **DEFERRED:** functional foreleg musculature is reserved for u17 §6.2. Carnivore and primate differences are retained as chapter framing but are not dispositioned into anatomy objects before u19-u22 develop them.
