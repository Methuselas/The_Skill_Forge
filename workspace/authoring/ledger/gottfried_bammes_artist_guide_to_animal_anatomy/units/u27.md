## Delta map

- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_internalize_profile_backline_with_unbroken_contour_and_memory` extends the earlier vertebral-axis Drill with a deliberately two-dimensional profile exercise: head-length calibration, one unbroken muzzle-to-back-foot contour, repeated same-animal studies, and alternating observation-only/memory-only concentration passes. It is explicitly bounded away from strong foreshortening so outer contour is not confused with the hidden center route.
- **NEW:** `PAT_orient_animal_body_in_depth_with_backline_and_transverse_axes` owns §7.5's three-dimensional reference system: continue the longitudinal back/center route through concealment, intersect it with a few structurally grounded transverse axes such as scapular and hip lines, and use those crossings to orient the body before adding masses.
- **NEW:** `DRILL_construct_foreshortened_animal_from_backline_cross_axes_and_core_masses` preserves fig. 115's teaching sequence as deliberate practice: axis map -> ordered core masses -> limbs and organic intersections -> reduced-guide final, with natural markings available only as a secondary wrap check.
- **REINFORCED:** optical-memory practice remains with `DRILL_capture_gesture_from_short_visual_memory`; rapid whole-animal shorthand remains with `PAT_preserve_rapid_motion_with_open_incomplete_sketching`; natural stripes as volume evidence remain with `DRILL_trace_surface_paths_over_wrapped_forms`; selective emphasis/optical center remains with the existing tone/accent hierarchy.
- **BOUNDED:** Bammes's rejection of generic discs, ellipses, and ovoid formulas is retained as a failure mode of the new spatial Pattern, not generalized into a ban on primitives. Primitives remain valid when their orientation is earned by the subject's actual construction.
- **DEFERRED:** head construction begins with u28 §8.1 and is not pre-extracted here despite p. 112's transition sentence about head/expression.
