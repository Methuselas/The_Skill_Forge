## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_mammal_hindleg_from_pelvic_anchor_and_joint_chain` turns the hindleg from a generic zigzag into a pelvic-anchored articulated chain whose profile angles, front/rear support read, and distal foot stance are reconstructed from the animal being drawn.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_change_hindleg_joint_form_with_flexion_and_axis` preserves Bammes's plastic-mechanics lesson: femuro-tibial and tarsal joint forms must change with flexion state and pivot orientation rather than behaving like fixed hinge symbols.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_construct_hindleg_skeleton_from_axes_to_joint_forms` follows Bammes's explicit study sequence from directions/pivots to joint forms/axes, shaft taper/curvature, and the foot/digits, then repeats the same procedure on a contrasting mammal.
- **FILTERED/BOUNDED:** the evolutionary plantigrade-to-unguligrade wording, literal all-mammals identity claim, and horse-specific pelvic/foot details are not promoted as universal biology.
- **DEFERRED:** fig. 59's functional muscle grouping is strong but belongs to u14 §5.2, where Bammes develops the musculature directly.
