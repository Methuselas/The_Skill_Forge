## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** animal type/conformation should be established from a flexible proportional ensemble before descriptive identifiers; proportion is relationships of parts to the whole, not one generic animal formula.
- **VARIANT:** Bammes's figs. 23-24 add a compact side-view measuring route using head length as a temporary module plus a trunk/ground-clearance rectangle.
- **OWNERSHIP RELINK:** `PAT_compare_related_animal_types_with_proportion_boxes` is now a specialization of the new animal-proportion foundation instead of hanging directly from the generic quadruped mass block; its explicit relationship to the quadruped construction scaffold is preserved as a cross-link.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** the ambush-versus-pursuit comparison strengthens the existing u01 Bammes comparative-function variant but does not create a second overlapping comparison card.
- **FILTERED:** fixed horse/cow/lion/dog ratios, the stated leg-neck “law of correlation,” and survival/locomotor causal claims are not promoted as universal biology.
- **DEFERRED:** primate limb proportions and associated mechanics wait for u22's dedicated primate shoulder-girdle treatment; the short thorax cross-section comparison in the §3.1 tail on printed p. 26 waits for u24's dedicated thorax unit.
