## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_primate_arm_from_mobile_shoulder_girdle_and_compound_rotation_chain` treats primate manual reach as a whole-arm construction problem: two-part mobile shoulder girdle, clavicular bracing, multi-axis shoulder, elbow flexion, proximal/distal radio-ulnar turning, wrist deviation, and an opposable hand all contribute distinct degrees of freedom.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_map_primate_arm_mobility_from_girdle_to_hand_axes` converts fig. 99's layered anatomy plate into a repeatable study: skeleton -> named/visible joint axes -> reduced axis map -> changed pronation, with elbow bend, forearm turn and wrist redirection kept separate.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_anchor_gorilla_arm_with_lateral_mobile_scapula_on_square_thorax` adds Bammes's gorilla shoulder calibration to the Hultgren gorilla mass owner: broad mobile scapula, farther around the side of the nearly square thorax, as the long arm's climbing/hanging base.
- **REINFORCED:** the existing human shoulder-girdle and thumb-opposition owners receive comparative support but no duplicate humanoid variants. The u19 carnivore radius-ulna route remains related, not foundational, because the primate clavicle/wrist/hand system materially changes the decision.
- **BOUNDED:** source language about uniqueness/innovation and exact functional biology is kept source-scoped; PASS retains the drawing-relevant mechanics rather than universal evolutionary claims.
