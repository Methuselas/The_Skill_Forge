## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_animal_thorax_as_plastic_core_from_changing_cross_sections` turns the rib cage from a generic barrel into a species- and view-specific enclosed core: dorsal/ventral longitudinal routes first, then selected cross-sections whose width, depth, asymmetry and front-to-back expansion actually change, with curve apexes and major plane convergence used only where they clarify the solid.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_model_and_redraw_animal_thorax_across_views_and_sections` preserves Bammes's explicit physical-model route: build or obtain a simplified thorax core, draw profile/front/cross-section and unfamiliar angles, compare the same object across views, then reconstruct one view from memory before applying the core beneath the living surface.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_use_thorax_section_and_front_rear_expansion_as_type_signature` extends the animal-type proportion owner with thorax width-depth section, shoulder-side compression versus posterior expansion, and major asymmetry/squareness as type cues when a side silhouette is insufficient.
- **REINFORCED:** the u01 inside-out core-mass Pattern remains the required foundation; the u04 animal plane/section block variant and Robertson/Zarins section AP remain related methods rather than mandatory prerequisites. u23's vertebral deformation Pattern remains related because a neutral thorax can be learned without first solving a bend or twist.
- **BOUNDED:** Bammes's dog/lion/horse/cow/human/gorilla section diagrams and the narrow-front/broad-rear comparison are treated as comparative construction evidence, not fixed zoological laws. The fig. 106 caption's primate exception is preserved.
