## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** choose one dominant impressional quality—proportion, function/action, surface, solidity, structure, massiveness, delicacy, or another clearly observed characteristic—and make it the governing statement instead of developing every attractive feature equally.
- **NEW Drill:** repeat that choice across several animals, making each study concentrate on one selected quality and deliberately omitting or quieting competing observations.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** u01 already owns anatomy as an inside-out articulated system with hierarchy; §§1.1–1.2 strengthen that foundation but do not justify a duplicate card or cosmetic variant.
- **DEFERRED:** mental-image reconstruction waits for Bammes's actual visualization exercises; the p. 10 limb-direction/weight-transfer diagrams wait for §1.3, where support mechanics become explicit rather than previewed.
- **BOUNDED:** a selected quality changes emphasis, not structural truth. Bammes's function language remains a drawing-analysis aid rather than automatic biological causation.
