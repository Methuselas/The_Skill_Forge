## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_carnivore_foreleg_from_independent_radius_ulna_and_rotating_paw` establishes the carnivore foreleg as a rotational specialization: the shoulder remains comparatively conservative through the elbow, while the independent radius-ulna relationship carries pronation and paw orientation through the lower limb.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_distinguish_cat_and_dog_foreleg_rotation_and_carpus` preserves Bammes's internal carnivore contrast without splitting the family into duplicate cards: cats receive greater rotational/abductive freedom and a broader, straighter distal arrangement; dogs are more restricted and narrower.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_construct_carnivore_paw_from_fanned_metacarpals_curved_arch_and_digit_support` extracts the distal support architecture as its own reusable subskill: fanned metacarpals, two-directional curvature, articulated digits/claws, high pollex where applicable, and separate pressure-bearing pads.
