## Delta map

- **VARIANT:** Stage 0 camera search gains Bammes's form-revelation criterion: for anatomy/construction studies, choose the open viewpoint by which angle exposes the animal's characteristic masses and planes most clearly, not only by drama or compositional effect.
- **VARIANT:** Stage 2 mass construction gains an animal-specific viewing-plane and cross-section route: simplify large/small masses, expose a few back/side/top/turning planes, and use selected section cuts to prove spatial gradients before anatomy.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** horizon/eye-level setup is already owned by the perspective foundation, including the above/below-eye-level diagnostic; Bammes supplies an animal application rather than a different learner decision.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** hatching that follows plane orientation is already owned by the resolved-form tone Pattern through the Loomis stroke-direction variant.
- **DEFERRED:** the animal-versus-human thorax comparison waits for the dedicated thorax unit u24.
- **BOUNDED:** three-quarter high/low views can reveal more form, but they are not promoted into a universal preferred camera.
