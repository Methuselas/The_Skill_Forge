## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_isolate_animal_study_difficulty_with_medium_and_scope` turns Bammes's practice advice into a deliberate-practice decision: reduce concurrent demands and choose a tool whose normal behavior favors the skill being trained.
- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_capture_animal_motion_with_one_continuous_brush_pass` preserves Bammes's specific watercolor movement exercise: rapid profile-first studies, direct head-to-hindleg continuity, pressure/brush-shape variation, restart rather than patch, and blank-paper articulation for later foreshortened/overlapping studies.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_test_animal_motion_with_visual_memory_and_imagination` is absorbed into `DRILL_capture_gesture_from_short_visual_memory`; Bammes changes the method from retaining a briefly observed human gesture to testing whether an already-studied animal movement can be reconstructed from visual memory/imagination, exposing gaps in understanding.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** broad chalk mass blocking and the movable proportion block were already canonical from u08 and are not cloned.
- **RECOVERY:** the u10 phase-strip/contact-diagram Drill remains unpromoted. §4.3 assigns brush, medium-isolation, and imagination/memory practice but does not explicitly assign a contact-strip drill, so PASS does not manufacture one from u10's explanatory diagrams.
