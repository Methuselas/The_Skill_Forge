## Delta map

- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_improvise_small_animal_sketches_from_internalized_structure` turns Bammes's free-play instructions into repeatable practice: familiar tool, small compact studies, bold searching lines, no fixed final solution, and a required return to structured study when the internal form repertory runs out.
- **NEW Pattern:** `PAT_preserve_rapid_motion_with_open_incomplete_sketching` preserves Bammes's nonfinito decision: when completion starts freezing a rapid action, leave open edges, gaps, and unresolved passages so only the essential movement remains fully stated.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** p. 48's claim that shorthand tests essential understanding strengthens the u11 visual-memory/imagination diagnostic and u02's hierarchy of essential versus subsidiary information; no new variant is needed.
- **FILTERED:** the large-outline/fine-line scale remarks are useful context but too briefly stated and medium-dependent to support independent cards.
