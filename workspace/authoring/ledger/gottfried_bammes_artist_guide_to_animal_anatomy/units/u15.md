## Delta map

- **NEW Drill:** `DRILL_build_animal_form_from_skeletal_landmarks_to_planes` completes the living-hindleg exercise deferred from u14 and generalizes Bammes's strict constructional study: skeleton reference points first, viewing planes second, straight block-like solidity, then selected hollows/hatching/cross-sections, followed by one abbreviated repeat that preserves structure rather than merely speeding up contour copying.
- **VARIANT:** `VAR_bammes_rebuild_animal_action_from_simplified_forms_at_new_viewpoint` on `DRILL_notate_action_figures_without_reference` adapts an existing humanoid invention owner instead of duplicating it for animals. The Bammes route reduces a studied animal action to linked simple forms, solves direction/movement, restores joints and modeling masses, then redraws the same action from an altered view to test internalized structure.
- **REINFORCEMENT:** flat-brush/crayon medium choice stays under the existing study-isolation Pattern; intensive-observation-to-fluent-line progression reinforces the already-canonical improvisational fluency Drill; the existing animal plane/cross-section variant receives stronger source support without another variant ID.
- **BOUNDED:** straight-line construction and avoidance of painterly value are conditions of the strict study, not universal stylistic rules for finished animal drawing.
