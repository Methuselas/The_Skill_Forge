## Delta map

- **NEW:** `PAT_read_animal_trunk_muscles_from_spinal_axes_and_levers` extends Bammes's joint-axis reasoning from the limbs to the vertebral column. The reusable decision is to establish pelvis/sacrum anchor, three spinal axes, and rib/process levers before interpreting dorsal extensor, ventral flexor, lateral-bending, and diagonal torsion groups.
- **NEW:** `DRILL_map_pure_trunk_muscle_actions_from_spinal_axes` preserves figs. 110-111 as a deliberate-practice sequence: cross-section axis map first, then broad dog/horse side-view forms and action prediction from the mechanical arrangement.
- **REINFORCED:** `PAT_propagate_vertebral_bend_and_twist_through_trunk` remains the owner of visible trunk deformation; `PAT_construct_animal_thorax_as_plastic_core_from_changing_cross_sections` remains the owner of thorax volume/sections. u26 explains a muscular mechanism around those structures rather than cloning their construction rules.
- **BOUNDED:** physiological functions listed on p. 106 are source context, not promoted to visible-form rules. Trunk-to-limb and trunk-to-shoulder-girdle muscles are explicitly outside §7.4 and remain outside the new Pattern/Drill.
- **DEFERRED:** graphic aids for turning trunk knowledge into fuller body depiction remain u27 §7.5.
