## Delta map

- **NEW Pattern:** reduce an animal pose to vertebral direction, principal limb directions, and major pivots before adding volume; rebuild those directions when the pose changes rather than bending a stock standing scaffold.
- **NEW Pattern:** distinguish the quadruped forequarter as a thorax suspended through the shoulder/scapular support system from the bone-linked pelvic hindquarter and its more angular push-off geometry.
- **NEW Drill:** redraw the same articulated framework through movement, upended, sitting/crouching, and stretching poses so structural directions—not contour memory—carry the anatomy.
- **ABSORBED:** the hindleg extension/push-off explanation belongs inside the fore/hind architecture Pattern rather than becoming a separate micro-card.
- **DEFERRED:** horse locking/tendon detail waits for the specialized runner foreleg units; vertebral bracing waits for the trunk chapter; the joint-condensation figure waits for later limb-construction sections.
- **FILTERED/BOUNDED:** the bridge analogy remains a construction metaphor, and Bammes's two-thirds/one-third load split is not promoted into a universal animal ratio.
