## Deepest new capability
The major Robertson delta is **X-Y-Z section drawing**. A complex imagined object is planned in a clear draft view, transferred onto perspective planes, sliced with cross-sections, and only then given its tangent outer silhouette. Cuts and design curves are projected through that same section network. This is materially different from merely putting a bounding box around an object.
