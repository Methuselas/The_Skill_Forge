# Ellipse Axis Verification Hold — Robertson Update

## Prior state
Norling's broad ellipse-axis “T” mnemonic was held because D'Amelio's exact projected-circle construction locates the projected center from the supporting perspective plane, and White did not prove Norling's mnemonic.

## Robertson contribution
Robertson gives the minor axis a central operational role. Printed pp. 72-73 state that the minor axis is the important perspective-drawing feature, visually align it with the axis perpendicular to the ellipse's surface, and use it as the practical axle/hinge direction for wheels, doors, rotations, and later constructions. Printed pp. 75-79 repeatedly apply that rule.

## PASS disposition
**Operational support increased; universal theorem still held for the mathematical audit.**

Robertson is a second independent artist-source supporting the minor-axis/axle heuristic, so it is safe to retain as a sketching and rotation control inside Robertson's own workflows. It is still not promoted here as a universal projective theorem that overrides D'Amelio's exact perspective-square/center construction.

The planned *Viewpoints* Deep PASS should settle the mathematical scope: under what projection and circle-plane conditions the ellipse minor axis corresponds to the projected plane normal / cylinder axle, and when exact center/axis constructions need a different treatment.
