# Perspective Cumulative Reconciliation — After Scott Robertson

## Frozen input
The cumulative D’Amelio + Norling + White package is the read-only baseline. No prior candidate, patch, report, or canonical library file is mutated.

## What Robertson changes
Robertson does **not** replace the prior foundation. He adds the strongest construction layer yet for imagined hard-surface form:
- same-unit rotated perspective grids on one ground plane;
- practical local grid construction when vanishing points are off-sheet;
- X-Y-Z section drawing for complex volumes;
- curve/cut projection through sectioned surfaces, including temporary construction planes for severe foreshortening.

Two short craft drills survive because they directly activate those methods without remaining resident.

## What Robertson patches
- White's camera-model patch should become a Station Point / Line of Sight / Picture Plane / COV branch.
- Norling's coupled-VP observation should be represented by Robertson's exact Visual Ray construction while keeping the simpler intuition.
- D'Amelio's distortion Pattern should gain field-of-view and grid/projection choice.
- D'Amelio's shared-scene AP should gain a hybrid underlay branch.

## What remains held
- Numeric COV/lens thresholds are not universalized.
- Linear-versus-curvilinear wide-angle behavior, especially look-up/skyscraper views, remains queued for *Viewpoints*.
- The ellipse minor-axis/surface-normal claim is operationally supported but still mathematically scoped later.

## Diminishing-return check
The run still exhibits healthy diminishing returns: six cards survive from a 200+ page technical book because most of Robertson's perspective fundamentals and later applications are already represented. The cards that survive are not restatements; they change how difficult constructed objects and grids are actually built.
