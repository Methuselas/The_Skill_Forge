# Robertson → *Viewpoints* Mathematical Audit Queue

These are source-supported practical rules that should **not** be promoted as universal mathematics before the dedicated *Viewpoints* Deep PASS.

1. **Cone-of-Vision numbers and lens equivalence.** Robertson offers 50°/60° working guidance and lens analogies; White supplied a fixed 60° / distance rule. Audit what is convention, what depends on image size/viewing distance, and what is genuinely geometric.
2. **Linear versus curvilinear wide-angle projection.** Robertson's pp. 62-64, 118, and 186-187 distinguish efficient linear design grids from photographic/fisheye curvature. Audit exactly when a planar perspective is mathematically correct yet perceptually awkward, especially in skyscraper/look-up compositions.
3. **Ellipse minor-axis / surface-normal claim.** Robertson gives strong operational support; reconcile it with D'Amelio's exact projected-circle center and the prior Norling hold.
4. **Tilted view / vertical convergence.** Robertson's station-point and Line-of-Sight diagrams give a practical camera model. Audit the precise relationship among physical eye level, image horizon, center of vision, and vertical vanishing behavior for extreme upward/downward views.

The purpose of the audit is internal decision logic. It should not turn the runtime curriculum into algebra unless a task explicitly asks for mathematical proof.
