## Notes

Eleven candidates, four kept — three new objects and one variant. The reject rate
is high and the reason is structural rather than a failure of the read: this is
the book's one explicitly optional chapter, and roughly two-thirds of it argues
*about* metaphors rather than teaching with them. The extraction stance from u01
did most of the dispositioning work here, and it did it cleanly — every reject
above fails the same test, that its IF fires during a discussion about software
rather than during the building of it.

Two things worth carrying:

**The TOC is wrong again.** u01's range was right, u02's was long by one page.
That is two of two chapters where the printed range had to be checked against
chapter text, and one of them moved. Verify every remaining range.

**Two open reconciliation items** are recorded in the disposition rows above and
should be settled at source closure rather than now: whether
`AP_grow_a_system_from_a_running_skeleton` becomes a variant of the metaskills
iterative-construction AP once the art lane's edits to that file land, and whether
`PAT_pick_and_choose_testing_philosophies` should point at
`PAT_keep_a_toolbox_instead_of_adopting_one_methodology` as its foundation.
