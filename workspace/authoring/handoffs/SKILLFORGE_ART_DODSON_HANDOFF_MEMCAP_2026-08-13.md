## DODSON — HIGH-VALUE TEACHING / ARCHITECTURE

### Perceptual wrongness comes first as an inspection alarm

A trained eye can register that something is wrong before conscious diagnosis. Treat immediate perceptual wrongness as a reason to scrutinize the work rather than rationalize it away. The eye does not necessarily identify the cause; anatomy, perspective, proportion, FORCE, balance, overlap, measurement, etc. diagnose it.

This should be used when inspecting Art work, including Stages 1–4.

### Search / Control complements the four Stages

Dodson's free-versus-controlled operation was generalized into a cross-domain metaskill.

Macro Art rhythm:
- Stage 1 — SEARCH
- Stage 2 — CONTROL of Stage 1
- Stage 3 — SEARCH
- Stage 4 — CONTROL of Stage 3

The modes can also nest recursively inside a Stage. Search discovers; Control selects, verifies, constrains, organizes, or refines. Do not demand unrestricted exploration and final precision from the same operation at once.

This may transfer to Writing, Coding, Design, Research, etc.

### Post-production verification is a cross-domain metaskill

Separate production from evaluation. After producing, ask whether the result actually satisfied the objective, instructions, constraints, and intended effect — not merely whether the internal process was executed.

### Observation and measurement

Measurement calibrates the eye; it does not replace it.

Useful loop:
eye/search -> perceptual alarm if needed -> measure/control -> restate/correct -> eye judges again.

Shared relational sighting now owns midpoint/divisions, plumb/level, comparative measurement, and increased verification density where tolerance is small.

### Lighting architecture

Composition owns expressive lighting decisions: mood, subject meaning, focal hierarchy, value/shadow design, ambient/color intent, and deliberate manipulation when appropriate.

Rendering owns the visual/physical consequence: local value/color under illumination, form turning, cast shadow, reflected light, edge behavior, and material response.

Artistically designed lighting may deliberately bend literal physical continuity. Example taught by MaDin: in sequential comic panels, two characters can remain in the same physical room/light setup while the accused betrayer is pushed into heavier shadow in the later panel to carry narrative mood. That is intentional design, not the same as an accidental lighting error.

Colored/ambient light can likewise shape mood, but Dodson itself only grounds value/light-shadow material; do not falsely attribute a full colored-light theory to him. Let later color/lighting sources specialize this architecture.

### Memcaps are usable accumulated project experience

Dodson's "personal experience" teaching is useful to AI only when actual durable memories/memcaps exist.

A memcap can preserve:
- prior human teaching
- corrections
- failed interpretations
- successful outcomes
- recurring failure modes
- decisions and precedents

Use that preserved history as source material when relevant. Never invent AI lived experience or an unrecorded memory. If no memcap exists, use current sources/user context instead.

A source book tells SkillForge what an author taught. Canon tells SkillForge which reusable decisions survived PASS. Memcaps tell SkillForge what happened when those decisions were actually used.

### Future drills are for learning, not just scoring

After Art is finished, MaDin intends to run drills specifically to generate experience.

Loop:
CANON -> ATTEMPT -> INSPECT -> FIND SUCCESS/FAILURE -> MADIN TEACHES/CORRECTS -> EXTRACT LESSON -> SAVE MEMORY -> APPLY NEXT ATTEMPT.

Important existing experience example: an extreme falling figure required many iterations. Failures included extra limbs, missing/malformed fingers, bad perspective, and disconnected anatomy. The final result succeeded and was strong enough that MaDin used it as a desktop background.

Reusable lesson: in extreme foreshortened action, validate the perspective scaffold and explicitly account for every limb, hand/digit group, attachment, and major overlap before refinement. Do not let a convincing distal endpoint or rendered detail hide an impossible articulated chain.
