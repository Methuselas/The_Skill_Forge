# Audit the Discoverable Career Footprint Before Promotion

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a job search or public career profile will cause employers to look for the candidate online
**THEN** inspect the material a stranger can discover, classify its professional effect, and assign a realistic correction or response to each material problem
**ELSE** repeat the audit periodically while any public professional presence remains active

## Do
- Search the candidate's name and relevant variants through more than one search route, including general results, images, video, public posts, and known network profiles.
- Review both the content and its prominence; a useful professional item buried beneath unrelated results does not provide the same first impression as one that appears early.
- Record the location, controller, concern, and desired action for every item requiring follow-up.
- Remove or correct material under the candidate's control, and make a polite, specific request to the person or organization controlling third-party material.
- When accurate material cannot be changed, prepare a concise truthful explanation and align future public behavior with the reputation the candidate intends to establish.

## Don't
- Audit only the profile being built while ignoring tagged images, old comments, association pages, forum posts, or other public traces.
- Treat every informal or old item as damaging without considering context and likely professional effect.
- Demand that another person alter material without explaining the request or acknowledging their control over it.
- Invent misleading content merely to bury an accurate result.
- Assume a deletion request has worked until the public result has been checked again.

## Checklist
- Text, image, video, and known social results have all been reviewed from a signed-out or otherwise public perspective.
- Each concerning result has an owner, location, requested action, and follow-up state.
- Benign material has been distinguished from material that creates a real professional conflict.
- Unremovable accurate material has a truthful response plan where explanation may be needed.
- A later search confirms the effect of completed corrections and identifies remaining exposure.

## Notes
Creating a polished profile does not reset the rest of a person's discoverable history. Employers may encounter third-party photographs, comments, and association pages before the candidate's chosen presentation. The audit converts vague anxiety into controllable cases: change what can be changed, request help where another party controls it, and prepare for what cannot responsibly be erased.
