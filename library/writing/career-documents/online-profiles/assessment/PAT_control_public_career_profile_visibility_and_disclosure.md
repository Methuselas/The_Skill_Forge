# Control Public Career-Profile Visibility and Disclosure

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a professional profile must be discoverable without exposing private, confidential, or search-sensitive information
**THEN** decide the audience for each profile element and connection before publishing it, then configure and test the available controls from that audience's view
**ELSE** keep the material off the public service until its disclosure is acceptable without relying on uncertain controls

## Do
- Separate information intended for recruiters and professional contacts from personal posts, private contact details, and connections that need not be publicly mapped.
- Test what an unknown visitor, ordinary connection, recruiter, and current workplace contact can see rather than trusting a settings label.
- Account for whether visible job-search signals, new connections, or resume links could reach a current employer before activating them.
- Clear every work sample for employer confidentiality, commercial sensitivity, shared-project permission, and the privacy of identifiable people.
- Verify unexpected invitations and suspicious links through another contact route before accepting or opening them.

## Don't
- Publish a home address, private telephone number, protected employer information, or another person's identity merely to make the profile look complete.
- Assume a personal account remains private because its intended audience is friends or family.
- Display shared work as solely owned or request a public recommendation from someone who cannot credibly assess the work.
- Treat the ability to delete or hide an item later as permission to disclose it now.
- Depend on a control whose effect has not been inspected from outside the account.

## Checklist
- Every public field has a defined professional purpose and acceptable audience.
- Personal and professional material are separated to the extent the platform and candidate require.
- Current-employer exposure has been assessed for job-search signals and public activity.
- Work samples have passed confidentiality, ownership, permission, and identity checks.
- Visibility tests match the intended result for strangers and connected viewers.

## Notes
Discoverability and privacy are not opposite switches. A profile can expose professional evidence to the people meant to find it while withholding unnecessary contact data, personal conversation, network structure, and protected work. Because settings and connection models change, the dependable check is the externally visible result, not the control's name.
