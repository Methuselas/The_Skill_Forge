# Maintain a Career Profile Through Current Evidence and Useful Participation

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a professional profile remains public after its initial completion
**THEN** keep its facts, proof, network, and visible activity current through a sustainable review and contribution rhythm
**ELSE** deactivate or remove the profile when it cannot be maintained and its stale presence would misrepresent the candidate

## Do
- Schedule periodic reviews for changed roles, contact routes, qualifications, memberships, promotions, work samples, and professional direction.
- Add connections selectively where a real professional, educational, or working relationship makes the network meaningful.
- Share or discuss field-relevant articles, tools, developments, and experience in ways that are useful to the intended community.
- Ask people who know the work well for recommendations, especially after they have already offered specific positive feedback that can be adapted with permission.
- Review available audience or search information to see whether the profile reaches the intended groups, then adjust content or participation rather than chasing raw activity.

## Don't
- Let an old title, abandoned contact route, obsolete sample, or long silence contradict the energetic reputation claimed in the summary.
- Accept every invitation or exchange endorsements merely to increase visible counts.
- Publish reactive arguments, workplace complaints, or strong unrelated opinions through the professional channel.
- Post filler solely to appear active when it supplies no professional value.
- Keep expanding to new services while existing profiles remain inaccurate or neglected.

## Checklist
- Current role, credentials, contact details, memberships, and work links are accurate.
- Recent visible activity serves the target profession or network rather than merely increasing volume.
- Connections and recommendations come from credible relationships.
- Review reminders are frequent enough to catch meaningful career changes.
- Audience response is assessed against intended professional reach, not vanity metrics alone.
- Any profile that cannot be sustained has an explicit deactivation or removal decision.

## Notes
Publishing creates an ongoing claim about professional currency. Stale facts damage trust directly, while purposeless activity can make an accurate profile look performative. Maintenance therefore combines factual updates with selective participation: keep the evidence true, the network credible, and the visible contribution useful to the people the profile is meant to reach.
