# Build and Maintain a Professional Online Profile

## Objective
Establish a discoverable, truthful professional profile whose reputation, evidence, audience, visibility, and continuing activity support a defined career direction without replacing targeted application materials or exposing protected information.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Define the professional result.** Name the target role family, intended viewers, and profile functions such as discovery, networking, opportunity monitoring, industry learning, or work display. Activate `writing_ground_professional_reputation_in_role_relevant_evidence`; if intended qualities lack conduct or evidence, omit them or establish a development plan before writing public claims.
2. **Inspect the existing public baseline.** Activate `writing_audit_discoverable_career_footprint_before_promotion`. Search the candidate as an outside viewer, classify material by professional effect, and assign follow-up. A material identity conflict or unresolved disclosure risk blocks new promotion until it has a correction, request, or truthful response plan.
3. **Select a sustainable channel.** Activate `writing_choose_career_network_platforms_by_audience_and_maintenance_cost`. Verify that the intended audience uses the service and that its controls and upkeep fit the candidate. If no service passes both audience and maintenance gates, stop without creating an abandoned profile.
4. **Set disclosure boundaries before adding content.** Activate `writing_control_public_career_profile_visibility_and_disclosure`. Decide what strangers, professional contacts, recruiters, and current workplace connections may see. Clear work samples and recommendations for confidentiality, ownership, permission, and third-party privacy before linking them.
5. **Assemble the evidence overview.** Draw from `writing_build_career_evidence_inventory_before_drafting`, then activate `writing_build_discoverable_career_profile_as_broad_professional_overview`. Build the summary, history, achievements, credentials, skills, memberships, identity cues, response route, and approved samples. Return to the inventory when a field tempts invention or leaves a material timeline unexplained.
6. **Cross the truth and discovery gate.** Compare dates, titles, qualifications, claims, spelling, contact routes, links, and occupational terms with the candidate's current records and application materials. Inspect the public rendering and search view. Do not publish until important claims are defensible, the profile is coherent rather than half-finished, and the intended audience can find the main capabilities.
7. **Publish without changing the application contract.** Use the profile as a broad supporting presence. Continue supplying a targeted resume, cover letter, form, or supporting document whenever an opportunity requests one. Verify visibility from outside the account immediately after publication.
8. **Enter the maintenance loop.** Activate `writing_maintain_career_profile_through_current_evidence_and_useful_participation`. Schedule factual reviews, contribute useful field-related material, cultivate credible connections and recommendations, and assess reach against the intended audience. Completion is a current profile with an active review cadence; if that cadence cannot be sustained, deactivate the profile rather than letting it misrepresent the candidate.

## Notes
The order prevents reputation work from becoming surface decoration. The public baseline is inspected before promotion, the audience is proven before a platform is adopted, and disclosure boundaries are fixed before valuable but protected evidence is uploaded. Publishing is not the endpoint because a discoverable profile continues making claims after the initial job search; maintenance or deliberate removal is part of the completed action.
