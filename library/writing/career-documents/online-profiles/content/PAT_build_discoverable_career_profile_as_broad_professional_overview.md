# Build a Discoverable Career Profile as a Broad Professional Overview

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a public career profile must support discovery across a role family rather than answer one vacancy
**THEN** publish a complete truthful overview that makes professional identity, career evidence, qualifications, and searchable capabilities easy to find without pretending to replace a targeted application
**ELSE** tailor the resume and cover letter to the specific opportunity instead of directing the employer to a generic profile alone

## Do
- Open with a compact summary that names the current professional direction, main capabilities, and strongest relevant evidence.
- Populate current and prior roles, education, certifications, skills, awards, memberships, and other material sections far enough to prevent unexplained partial-history impressions.
- Add a result or meaningful contribution to important roles rather than listing titles and dates alone.
- Use recognizable occupational terms naturally beside the experience or credential that supports them; keep spelling and naming conventions consistent throughout.
- Supply a stable identity-based profile address, current response route, relevant work links, and a recognizable current photograph when the medium and professional context make those elements useful.

## Don't
- Copy one vacancy-targeted resume unchanged and treat it as a durable public overview.
- Falsify dates, degrees, titles, achievements, or gaps that former colleagues and later checks can expose.
- Repeat a keyword mechanically to manipulate discovery at the expense of readable evidence.
- Leave prominent sections half-complete while publishing strong self-promotional claims elsewhere.
- Send only a profile link when the employer requests a resume, cover letter, form, or other application material.

## Checklist
- A reader can identify the candidate's professional direction and main evidence without reconstructing it from scattered fields.
- Career history, qualifications, and memberships agree with the candidate's other current materials.
- Important roles include contribution or achievement evidence as well as position labels.
- Search terms are truthful, readable, and attached to supporting experience or credentials.
- Every link, contact route, image, and public sample is current and strengthens the intended professional presentation.
- The profile remains a broad companion to, not a substitute for, a vacancy-specific application.

## Notes
A resume argues for one opening; a public profile must remain coherent for several related searches and audiences. That broader scope does not justify vagueness or incompleteness. The profile should expose enough chronology and evidence to be credible, enough shared occupational language to be discoverable, and enough boundaries that the candidate can still tailor the actual application.
