# Draft a Graduate Resume from Academic and Transferable Evidence

## Objective
Produce a targeted graduate résumé that establishes the required academic standing while proving employability through relevant placements, other work, projects, competency evidence, extracurricular contribution, and credible observers.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Freeze the graduate opportunity.** Activate `writing_target_application_materials_to_employer_criteria` and record the program or role, organization, degree requirements, requested capabilities, application components, deadline, and delivery instructions. A generic graduate target is not enough when employers weight grades, discipline, language, work exposure, and personal attributes differently.
2. **Map evidence beyond the degree.** Use `writing_application_targeting_variant_transferable_skill_bridge` inside the targeting owner. Pair each important requirement with support from study, projects, placements, paid or temporary work, volunteering, campus activity, sport, professional involvement, or another credible setting. Stop before drafting if the planned opening contains a capability with no concrete source.
3. **Establish contact and direction.** Activate `writing_make_resume_contact_details_professionally_reachable`. Then use `writing_open_resume_with_employer_relevant_evidence`; include a brief career objective only when it clarifies a realistic contribution or target path that the current record does not already make obvious.
4. **Pass the academic-status gate.** Activate `writing_present_education_and_training_by_hiring_relevance`. State the qualification, institution, major or specialization, completion status, and supportable expected completion point. Feature grades, relevant subjects, awards, or academic recognition only when they strengthen this hiring decision. When a transcript or credential copy is requested, route it through `writing_submit_credential_evidence_without_risking_originals_or_sensitive_data` instead of overloading the résumé with a full result table.
5. **Choose one experience structure deliberately.** Activate `writing_choose_resume_format_by_evidence_visibility`. Keep one reverse-chronological sequence when it already exposes the match. Use `writing_resume_variant_professional_and_other_experience_split` when a small amount of profession-related placement or internship evidence would otherwise be buried by a larger casual, temporary, or volunteer history. Preserve dates and meaningful evidence in both sections; a secondary label is not permission to discard transferable work.
6. **Write role evidence and take the competency branch only when useful.** Use `writing_write_specific_responsibility_statements` and `writing_turn_added_value_into_resume_achievements` for the experience entries. If important capabilities still remain hard to see across several settings, activate `writing_build_resume_competency_statements_from_fit_and_verifiable_evidence`. Omit competency paragraphs when direct experience or education already proves the same point clearly.
7. **Use non-work sections to close real evidence gaps.** Activate `writing_include_optional_resume_sections_only_as_evidence` for campus offices, community work, sport, professional memberships, languages, technical skills, training, and interests. Describe an office, project, event, or result rather than assuming membership or participation itself proves leadership, initiative, or professional commitment.
8. **Prepare credible observers.** Activate `writing_prepare_referees_before_submission`. Prefer supervisors from placements, paid, temporary, or volunteer work; use a lecturer, thesis supervisor, or project supervisor when that person directly observed performance relevant to the target. Permission, current contact details, and the observer's actual knowledge remain completion gates.
9. **Finalize the exact program or role version.** Hand the stable draft to `writing_ap_finalize_and_submit_resume`. A change to academic status, competency evidence, experience grouping, or target fit reopens the affected upstream step and all downstream proof checks. Completion requires a readable delivered file whose academic claims are accurate, whose non-academic capabilities are traceable, and whose submission satisfies the frozen contract.

## Notes
Graduate selection tests more than possession of a degree. The order prevents academic detail from crowding out employability and prevents casual or volunteer work from being dismissed merely because it is outside the target profession. Competency paragraphs are a conditional bridge, not a required section: they earn space only when they connect scattered evidence to an employer requirement more clearly than the existing record does.
