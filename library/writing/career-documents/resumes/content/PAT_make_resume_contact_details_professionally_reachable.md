# Make Resume Contact Details Professionally Reachable

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a resume identifies the candidate and provides response channels
**THEN** supply contact details that are private enough for a job search, reliable during business hours, and consistent with a professional identity

## Do
- Use the preferred professional name consistently, adding pronunciation guidance only when it would prevent a likely error.
- Supply a telephone number the candidate can answer discreetly or monitor through a clear, neutral voicemail greeting.
- Use a name-based email address that is checked frequently throughout the application period.
- Include a portfolio, professional profile, or work-related site only when it is current and strengthens the application.
- State work authorization or another application-critical eligibility fact when the employer needs it to assess the candidacy.

## Don't
- Route calls through a workplace, shared household, or message service that cannot protect the candidate's privacy or deliver messages accurately.
- Let a novelty email handle or joking voicemail greeting become the reviewer's first evidence of judgment.
- Add a broken, stale, or unrelated web address merely to make the header look complete.
- Fill the header with sensitive personal facts, photographs, or cover-page decoration that the role does not require.

## Checklist
- The displayed name matches the identity used throughout the application package.
- Telephone and email routes have been tested from outside the candidate's own accounts.
- Voicemail can accept a message and identifies the candidate appropriately.
- Every linked page opens, is current, and presents material suitable for an employer.
- Optional personal or eligibility details have a specific application purpose.

## Notes
The contact block is operational, not biographical. Its job is to let an employer identify the applicant, make contact without delay, and inspect only the professional material the candidate deliberately offers. A channel that is hard to monitor or embarrassing to answer can waste otherwise strong evidence before an interview is arranged.
