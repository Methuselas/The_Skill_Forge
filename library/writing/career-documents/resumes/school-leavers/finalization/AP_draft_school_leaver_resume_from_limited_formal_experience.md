# Draft a School-Leaver Resume from Limited Formal Experience

## Objective
Produce a targeted, truthful résumé that lets an employer evaluate a candidate's potential through relevant evidence from education, training, paid and informal work, community activity, and supervised conduct even when conventional employment history is short or absent.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Freeze the target before judging experience.** Activate `writing_target_application_materials_to_employer_criteria` and record the role's requested capabilities, organization, delivery instructions, and deadline. If no target exists, define a narrow entry-level role family before selecting content.
2. **Build the evidence bridge.** Use `writing_application_targeting_variant_transferable_skill_bridge` inside the targeting owner. Search study, group projects, presentations, sport, clubs, volunteering, fundraising, caregiving, casual tasks, part-time work, and small ventures for observed actions that match the target. If useful experience is hard to recognize, run `writing_drill_map_first_job_skills_to_evidence_from_everyday_experience`. Advance only when every intended skill claim has a truthful example and a later proof location.
3. **Establish a professional response route.** Activate `writing_make_resume_contact_details_professionally_reachable`. Verify the candidate's name, email, telephone route, and voicemail from a caller's perspective before placing them at the top; an unreachable or unsuitable route blocks completion.
4. **Choose the opening branch.** Activate `writing_open_resume_with_employer_relevant_evidence`. Use a compact key-skill preview when the later sections substantiate it. Add a career objective only when a career-related opportunity needs a realistic direction clarified; omit it from a casual application when it would consume space without improving the match.
5. **Order proof by current strength.** Activate `writing_present_education_and_training_by_hiring_relevance` for recent subjects, results, awards, vocational study, and useful training. Follow with paid, casual, informal, volunteer, or self-directed work rather than hiding legitimate responsibility because it was not full-time employment. Keep chronology truthful while giving the strongest target-matched section early visibility.
6. **Turn activity into assessable evidence.** Use `writing_write_specific_responsibility_statements` for entrusted work and `writing_turn_added_value_into_resume_achievements` only for a distinct result, recognition, improvement, or promotion. Trace each opening skill claim to one of these statements, an education result, or another named section; remove any claim that remains unsupported.
7. **Add extras and referees selectively.** Activate `writing_include_optional_resume_sections_only_as_evidence` for sport, clubs, interests, awards, and community roles. Retain an item only when it demonstrates relevant capability or useful commitment without inviting a stronger distracting inference. Activate `writing_prepare_referees_before_submission`; when no workplace supervisor exists, choose an informed teacher, coach, volunteer coordinator, customer, or other independent observer rather than a family member who knows the candidate only personally.
8. **Finalize the exact application version.** Hand the stable draft to `writing_ap_finalize_and_submit_resume`. A failed truth, relevance, proof, presentation, contact, or referee check returns to the owning step. Completion requires a readable final file in which the employer can find the requested potential quickly, every material claim is corroborated, and the delivered version passes the submission gates.

## Notes
The protocol reverses the common early-career mistake of treating a short job history as an absence of evidence. Target requirements come first, because the same school subject, casual task, or community role can matter differently to a retail, technical, service, or professional opening. The invariant is not that every activity appears; it is that each retained claim connects a real action to the employer's decision and remains consistent through the final delivered file.
