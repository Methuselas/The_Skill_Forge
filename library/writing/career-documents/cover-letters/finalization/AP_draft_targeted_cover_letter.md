# Draft a Targeted Cover Letter

## Objective
Produce a truthful, employer-specific cover letter whose contact context is clear, whose selected evidence establishes a plausible match, and whose final delivered form gives the reader an accurate route to the next step.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Freeze the application contract.** Record the opportunity, submission instructions, required format, deadline, recipient information, reference code, and any requested topics. If the employer asks for a selection statement, salary information, or another distinct requirement, preserve it instead of applying a generic letter convention.
2. **Research before selecting claims.** Activate `writing_target_application_materials_to_employer_criteria`. Mark the role's strongest requirements and learn enough about the organization, sector, work, and stated priorities to distinguish a genuine reason for contact from generic praise. Do not draft the argument until each intended claim has supporting evidence.
3. **Establish a reliable route.** Activate `writing_route_cover_letter_to_correct_reader_and_opportunity`. Verify the reader or responsible function, vacancy identifiers, organization name, and candidate response channels. A routing uncertainty blocks personalization; use a verified neutral route rather than guessing.
4. **Choose the contact branch.** Activate `writing_choose_cover_letter_frame_by_contact_context`. Decide whether the approach answers an advertisement, uses a permitted referral, explores an unannounced need, or must expose temporary-work constraints. Carry that branch into both the opening and the requested next action.
5. **Build the argument in dependency order.** Activate `writing_structure_cover_letter_around_contact_purpose_evidence_and_next_step`. Draft the contact purpose first, then a selective body that interprets the strongest matching evidence, then a close that states availability and a feasible route forward. If the body merely retells the resume or cannot explain why this organization is being approached, return to step 2.
6. **Run the truth and continuity gate.** Confirm that names, relationships, qualifications, results, employer knowledge, availability, and promised follow-up can all be defended. Remove or correct any claim that outruns its evidence. Put every promised action on a schedule the candidate will actually keep.
7. **Revise language and presentation.** Activate `writing_keep_cover_letter_confidence_specific_and_employer_focused`, then `writing_present_cover_letter_as_concise_companion_document`. Cut repetition and self-focused material before compressing the layout. Reopen the rendered document or delivered message view whenever revision changes page flow.
8. **Proof and package the exact submission.** Activate `writing_proofread_application_materials_with_independent_passes` after content and layout stabilize. When the letter and resume travel as attachments, activate `writing_package_emailed_application_documents_for_reliable_routing`; otherwise apply the stated channel requirements directly. Completion requires the correct recipient and opportunity, defensible evidence, a credible next step, error-free final wording, and a reopened delivery copy that remains legible and complete.

## Notes
The sequence prevents polished genericity. Research and evidence selection precede prose because later tone and layout cannot repair an irrelevant case. Routing precedes personalization because an invented name or stale vacancy identifier can misdirect the whole package. Proofreading and delivery checks come last because every substantive revision invalidates their assurance. The protocol applies to attached letters, inline messages, and portal submissions; the employer's explicit contract overrides customary presentation advice.
