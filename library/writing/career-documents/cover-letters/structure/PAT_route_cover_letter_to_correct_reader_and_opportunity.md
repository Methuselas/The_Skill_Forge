# Route a Cover Letter to the Correct Reader and Opportunity

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a cover letter must identify who should act on it and which opening it concerns
**THEN** verify the responsible reader, label the exact opportunity, and preserve a direct response route in every form the letter may take
**ELSE** identify the relevant function and contact purpose without inventing a person, title, or vacancy

## Do
- Use the advertisement, position description, referral, or organization contact route to find the person responsible for the application rather than defaulting to the most senior visible employee.
- Check the spelling of the reader's name, role, organization, vacancy title, and reference code against the current application material.
- Put the vacancy title and supplied reference number in the document heading or message subject so the application remains identifiable when separated from its surrounding message.
- Include the candidate's reliable telephone and email details in an attached letter, and repeat the necessary response route when an inline message may be forwarded without its original account context.
- Use a neutral role-appropriate salutation when a name cannot be verified; personalize only from information that is actually known.

## Don't
- Guess a personal title, level of familiarity, or decision-maker merely to make the greeting look personalized.
- Recycle a prior recipient, employer name, vacancy code, or subject line from another application.
- Leave a message subject blank or use a generic label that forces the recipient to open files to discover the vacancy.
- Assume the sender address alone will remain visible wherever the letter is downloaded, printed, or forwarded.

## Checklist
- The named reader is the current contact for this application or is supported by a credible referral or organizational route.
- Every personal, organizational, and vacancy identifier has been checked character by character.
- The opportunity can be identified from the letter or subject line without opening another document.
- The candidate can be contacted from the letter in the form the recipient will actually receive.
- No greeting or title asserts personal information that has not been verified.

## Notes
A cover letter can arrive as an attachment, an inline message, a downloaded file, or a forwarded printout. Routing therefore has two layers: direct the application to the person or function able to evaluate it, and keep enough vacancy and response information attached to the letter for that context to survive separation. Personalization is useful only when accurate; a verified neutral route is safer than a confident guess.
