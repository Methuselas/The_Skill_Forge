# Choose a Cover-Letter Frame by Contact Context

## Pattern Rule
**IF** the reason for approaching an employer changes what the reader must understand before considering the candidate
**THEN** make the opening, supporting details, and requested next action follow the actual advertised, referred, speculative, or availability-constrained context
**ELSE** use the advertised-vacancy route when a defined opening and submission process already govern the application

## Do
- State the reason for contact early enough that the reader does not have to infer whether the letter answers an advertisement, uses an introduction, or explores an unannounced need.
- Obtain permission before naming a referrer, and explain the connection briefly before moving to the candidate's own evidence.
- Base an unsolicited approach on a researched event, direction, client need, capability gap, or other credible reason the organization might value the proposed contribution.
- For casual or temporary work, disclose the days, hours, duration, assignment types, locations, and transport facts that materially constrain a useful offer.
- Align the close with the route: enter the stated selection process, request a discussion, or promise a specific follow-up only when that action is appropriate and feasible.

## Don't
- Present a speculative approach as though a vacancy exists when none has been identified.
- Treat a respected contact's name as a substitute for demonstrating role fit.
- Name a person who has not agreed to be used as a referral.
- Hide availability or location constraints that would make the proposed temporary assignment impossible.
- Add every contextual detail to every letter; include only facts that change how this opportunity should proceed.

## Checklist
- The first paragraph makes the contact context unmistakable.
- The evidence and requested next step fit that context rather than a recycled template.
- Any named referrer has granted permission and the relationship is described accurately.
- A speculative approach rests on a specific researched basis and plausible contribution.
- Temporary-work constraints are complete enough for the recipient to assess operational fit.

## Notes
`writing_cover_letter_variant_advertised_vacancy_response` follows a defined opening: identify it, answer its criteria, and enter its stated process. `writing_cover_letter_variant_referral_introduction` changes the opening by naming a permitted introducer while leaving the candidate responsible for proving fit. `writing_cover_letter_variant_speculative_employer_approach` replaces vacancy evidence with a researched organizational reason for contact and a proportionate proposed follow-up. `writing_cover_letter_variant_temporary_work_constraints` adds scheduling and assignment facts when those facts determine whether an offer can be made.
