# Preserve Online Application Work Across Form Constraints

## Pattern Rule
**IF** application answers and documents must pass through an online form with session, field, formatting, or upload constraints
**THEN** preserve a local plain-text master, save at safe checkpoints, and verify the final submitted state independently of the live form
**ELSE** retain a complete copy and submission record through the non-web delivery route

## Do
- Draft long answers in a local document that can survive a timeout or failed submission, while keeping a plain-text version suitable for constrained fields.
- Save the form whenever the service permits and complete the most failure-prone uploads or long responses with enough time to recover.
- Check character limits and inspect pasted text for lost paragraphs, substituted symbols, doubled spaces, or hidden formatting before advancing.
- Upload current final files in the requested formats, then verify their displayed names, versions, and successful attachment to the application.
- Record the applicant or application number, preserve the exact final answers and uploaded documents, and retain the confirmation message or receipt.
- Confirm that the supplied email address and telephone route work before submission so later contact is not lost.

## Don't
- Keep the only copy of a substantial answer inside one browser session.
- Assume a saved draft contains the latest pasted text or uploaded version without reopening it.
- Force rich formatting into a field that accepts plain text and leave the resulting corruption unchecked.
- Treat a button click or redirected page as proof that the employer received a complete application.
- Close the process without retaining identifiers and a copy of what was actually submitted.

## Checklist
- Long answers exist outside the live form in recoverable text.
- Field limits and pasted formatting have been inspected in the service's displayed result.
- Every requested upload is the current final version and appears attached under an identifiable filename.
- The complete final application has been copied or otherwise preserved.
- An application identifier and submission confirmation have been retained.
- Contact routes supplied to the employer have been tested.

## Notes
Online submission introduces two separate risks: losing authored work and losing evidence of the final transaction. Local drafting protects the first; saved copies, identifiers, and confirmation protect the second. The form's visible post-paste and post-upload state is the relevant artifact because formatting and version changes can occur during transfer.
