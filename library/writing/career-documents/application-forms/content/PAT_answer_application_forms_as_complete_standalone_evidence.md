# Answer Application Forms as Complete Standalone Evidence

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an employer requires its own application form in addition to or instead of a resume
**THEN** complete every required field with a truthful, specific answer that can be evaluated without sending the reader elsewhere
**ELSE** use the requested resume and cover-letter route while preserving the same factual consistency

## Do
- Read the entire form, its instructions, and any declaration before drafting so later fields do not change the meaning or required scope of earlier answers.
- Research the role and organization, then select examples that answer the stated requirements rather than pasting generic career prose.
- Draft substantial responses separately before entering them, especially when a field limit requires evidence to be compressed without losing specificity.
- Give dates, employer names, qualifications, and other repeated facts consistently across the form and accompanying documents.
- Enter an explicit permitted not-applicable response when a required field genuinely does not apply, and attach extra material only when the instructions allow it.
- Re-read the declaration against the completed answers before signing or submitting it.

## Don't
- Write `see resume` where the form asks for information or evidence.
- Leave a required field blank and expect the employer to infer that it does not apply.
- Paste the same broad answer into several questions whose wording tests different requirements.
- Conceal uncertainty in dates or credentials by inventing precision.
- Sign a declaration before checking that every claim and attachment agrees with it.

## Checklist
- Instructions, field limits, attachment rules, and the declaration have all been read.
- Every required field contains an answer or an explicitly allowed not-applicable notation.
- Each evidence question includes a concrete example aligned with the role.
- Repeated names, dates, qualifications, and employment details agree across the package.
- Supplemental pages or documents are included only where permitted and clearly identified.
- The signed or submitted declaration is true for the final version, not an earlier draft.

## Notes
Employers use forms to collect comparable information and may assess a field independently of attached documents. Completeness is therefore substantive, not clerical. A concise direct answer preserves the employer's evaluation structure; a cross-reference forces the reader to reconstruct the response and can be treated as no answer at all.
