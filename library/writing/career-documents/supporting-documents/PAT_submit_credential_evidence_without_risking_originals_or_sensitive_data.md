# Submit Credential Evidence Without Risking Originals or Sensitive Data

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an application requires proof of qualifications, registrations, licences, training, identity, or work eligibility
**THEN** supply the minimum current evidence requested through the instructed route, using ordinary or certified copies as required while retaining irreplaceable originals and limiting sensitive disclosure
**ELSE** keep the evidence ready but do not add private documents merely to make the package look more complete

## Do
- Inventory every required credential and distinguish academic results, professional registrations, occupational licences, safety training, identity, and work-eligibility evidence.
- Verify current employer and jurisdiction requirements, including currency, certification, issuing authority, permitted format, and whether inspection of an original occurs later.
- Use a clear copy or properly certified copy when requested and retain the original under the candidate's control during ordinary submission.
- Check that names, dates, credential numbers, expiry information, and issuing organizations are readable and agree with the application.
- Submit sensitive material only through the instructed legitimate route and retain a record of what was disclosed, to whom, and for which application.
- Ask the receiving organization to clarify a requirement when it appears to demand unnecessary sensitive information or surrender of an irreplaceable document.

## Don't
- Mail or leave an original certificate, licence, transcript, identity document, or other irreplaceable record without a verified requirement and safe return process.
- Attach every credential on file when only one qualification or licence has been requested.
- Assume an old copy remains acceptable after a registration, licence, or training credential can expire or change.
- Send identity or eligibility evidence through an unverified contact or improvised channel.
- Alter a copy in a way that misrepresents its status, completeness, or certification.

## Checklist
- Every supplied document answers a stated application requirement.
- Currency, certification, format, and jurisdiction-specific conditions have been verified from current instructions.
- Originals remain controlled or have an explicitly verified inspection and return process.
- Names, dates, numbers, issuing bodies, and expiry details are legible and consistent.
- Sensitive content and the delivery route are no broader than necessary.
- A record identifies the exact documents and recipient for the submission.

## Notes
Credential evidence is both evaluative and sensitive. Sending too little can leave a requirement unproved, while sending everything increases privacy and identity risk without improving the application. Requirements vary by employer, profession, and jurisdiction, so current instructions govern the copy, certification, and inspection process rather than a permanent document rule.
