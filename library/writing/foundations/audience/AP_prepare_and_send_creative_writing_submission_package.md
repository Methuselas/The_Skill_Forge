# Prepare and Send a Creative-Writing Submission Package

## Objective
Turn one polished creative work and one researched destination into a complete, receiver-compliant package, transmit it through the specified route, and preserve an exact record for response handling and later submissions.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Set the entry conditions.** Select one work and one publication, imprint, magazine, competition, agent, or other receiving desk. For fiction and poetry, finish and polish the amount of work the receiver expects before proposing it; for nonfiction or another form explicitly acquired from proposals, establish how much may remain unwritten. If the destination is not yet current and evidence-backed, activate `writing_ap_research_and_shortlist_publication_outlets` before assembling anything.
2. **Freeze the current receiving contract.** Copy the destination's current first-party instructions into a package checklist: accepted form and length, required and forbidden materials, formatting, file type and naming, contact person or department, transmission route, deadline, eligibility constraints, and any rule governing simultaneous or prior use. Resolve contradictions through the receiver's stated priority or a direct clarification; do not substitute customary practice for an explicit instruction.
3. **Stabilize the offered text.** Confirm that the manuscript version is the one intended for this destination. Activate `writing_proofread_stable_draft_through_independent_passes`, then lock a submission copy so later drafting cannot silently change the package. **Readiness gate:** no unresolved structural revision, placeholder, tracked change, comment, or known surface error remains in the offered version.
4. **Conform the manuscript.** Activate `writing_conform_manuscript_to_receiver_current_requirements`. Apply the specified layout, identification, pagination, file, and naming rules; where the receiver is silent, use a restrained, readable, internally consistent presentation. Inspect the final rendered or uploaded form rather than trusting the editable source.
5. **Prepare the first-contact text.** When a query, cover letter, explanatory email, or form response is required, activate `writing_write_factual_outlet_specific_query`. Address the relevant person or desk, identify exactly what is offered, establish work-to-outlet fit from research, include only pertinent credentials or experience, and state which requested materials accompany or remain available.
6. **Build only the required supporting set.** For a single story, poem group, or short work, attach the complete work or requested selection exactly as instructed. For long fiction, add the requested sample and activate `writing_fiction_write_full_disclosure_synopsis` when a synopsis is required. For nonfiction requested at proposal stage, supply the specified combination of overview, author relevance, audience or market, comparable or competing work, promotion capacity, chapter outline, and samples. Treat these as possible components, not a universal bundle.
7. **Audit the package as one object.** Compare every item and field with the frozen checklist. Verify that titles, author identity, contact details, word counts, version labels, and project descriptions agree across the query, manuscript, synopsis, filenames, and form. Remove unsolicited extras. **Package gate:** a receiver can identify the work, understand the offer, open every required item, and find neither an omitted requirement nor an invented addition.
8. **Transmit once through the specified route.** Submit to the named person, department, portal, or address using the required subject line, fields, attachments, and acknowledgements. After the final action, verify the confirmation page, sent item, receipt, or other available evidence rather than assuming the transmission completed.
9. **Create the transaction record.** Activate `writing_track_each_submission_as_distinct_transaction` immediately. Record the exact work version, destination, date, route, materials, applicable response window or follow-up point, restrictions, confirmation, and current status.
10. **Route the outcome.** On a request for more material, build the requested follow-up from the same verified project identity and record it as a linked transaction. On acceptance, pause conflicting routes and review the offered terms before acting. On rejection or expiry of the stated response window, close the record and return to the researched alternatives rather than sending an unchanged package blindly.

## Notes
A submission is judged first as a received object: the writing, presentation, explanation, requested evidence, and delivery route must agree. The procedure therefore separates destination research from package construction, lets explicit current instructions override inherited conventions, and delays transmission until the rendered package has been inspected as a whole. It does not prescribe one universal proposal. Different forms and receivers request different evidence, so professionalism consists in satisfying the actual contract with clarity and restraint.
