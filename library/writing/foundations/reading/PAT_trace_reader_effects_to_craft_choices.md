# Trace Reader Effects to Craft Choices

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a passage strongly moves, surprises, persuades, bores, confuses, or repels you but the lesson remains a general reaction
**THEN** name the experienced effect, locate the language or structural choices that coincide with it, form a testable explanation of how they work together, and try an adapted version in new material
**ELSE** record the response briefly and continue reading when no specific choice can yet be isolated

## Do
- Separate observation, inference, and taste: identify what the work does before proposing why it works or whether you value the result.
- Mark the smallest useful evidence, then inspect its context for setup, repetition, contrast, omission, sequence, viewpoint, pacing, syntax, sound, imagery, or characterization that changes the effect.
- Compare a successful moment with a nearby weaker or differently functioning moment so the technique is not reduced to a favorite sentence.
- Phrase the lesson as a transferable relationship between choice and effect, including the conditions under which it may fail.
- Test the relationship on different subject matter and preserve the underlying function rather than copying distinctive language or surface mannerism.

## Don't
- Substitute plot summary, biography, reputation, or broad praise for craft evidence.
- Treat your response as proof that every reader experienced the same effect.
- Credit one visible device when accumulation, placement, or surrounding context did the real work.
- Turn imitation into transcription or reproduce a writer's verbal signature without transforming it.
- Keep diagnosing indefinitely after a plausible craft hypothesis is ready to test in writing.

## Checklist
- The reader effect is named more precisely than good, bad, powerful, or beautiful.
- Each causal claim points to an observable choice and acknowledges relevant context.
- The explanation distinguishes evidence from inference and personal preference.
- The resulting craft proposition can guide a new decision without the original passage present.
- A practical test reveals whether the proposed mechanism transfers beyond the example.

## Notes
Reading like a writer converts reception into an apprenticeship loop. An intense response identifies where to look, not what conclusion to draw. Close comparison exposes how local language, larger arrangement, and withheld or repeated information cooperate, while a fresh test separates an actual mechanism from admiration of an inimitable surface. The purpose is not to explain away the reading experience but to leave with a choice the writer can deliberately make, vary, or refuse.
