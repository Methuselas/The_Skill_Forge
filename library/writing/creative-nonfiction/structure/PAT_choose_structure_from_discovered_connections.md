# Choose Structure from Discovered Connections

## Pattern Rule
**IF** exploratory drafting has produced multiple times, topics, images, or reflections but an inherited outline does not reveal how they belong together
**THEN** identify the relations the material has discovered and organize them through a chronological spine, thematic braid, or purposeful segmentation
**ELSE** retain the existing structure when it already makes the inquiry's accumulating movement legible

## Do
- Mark repeated images, questions, contrasts, causes, and changes before deciding which relation should control the sequence.
- Give every paragraph or segment a local center so a reader can understand the unit before connecting it to its neighbors.
- Use explicit bridges when the relation needs explanation and white space when juxtaposition itself should make the reader perform the connection.
- Reintroduce an anchor with altered meaning or pressure; repetition should register development rather than simple recurrence.
- Check each transition for reader relocation: time, place, speaker, subject, and the reason for the move should be recoverable where they matter.

## Don't
- Preserve the order in which material was drafted merely because discovery happened that way.
- Treat chronology as a list of consecutive events with no selected narrative pressure.
- Alternate thematic strands on a fixed schedule when their meetings produce no new implication.
- Use fragmentation to disguise paragraphs that lack focus or connections that have not been conceived.
- Explain every juxtaposition so fully that the space between segments stops doing work.

## Checklist
- One governing relation explains why this section follows the previous one.
- Each unit has a clear local subject, action, image, or reflective task.
- Recurring material changes significance as the essay advances.
- Abrupt transitions ask for a productive inference rather than leaving accidental confusion.
- The final sequence reveals stronger connections than the draft order or a default outline did.

## Notes
Exploratory drafting can discover an essay's connections before it discovers its design. Selection and arrangement shape factual material without authorizing changes to what happened.

`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_use_chronology_as_narrative_spine` uses time as the principal route and allows reflective asides to depart from and return to it. It works when change or consequence accumulates through events, but not when chronology reduces the essay to a diary-like sequence.

`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_braid_themes_around_recurring_anchor` rotates among distinct strands held together by a recurring post. Use it when each return alters the reader's understanding of the other strands; avoid it when repetition supplies only a decorative motif.

`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_segment_by_juxtaposition_and_jump_cut` makes adjacency and white space meaningful transitions. It suits an inquiry whose force depends on contrast, omission, or collision, provided each segment is locally coherent and readers receive enough orientation to make the jump.
