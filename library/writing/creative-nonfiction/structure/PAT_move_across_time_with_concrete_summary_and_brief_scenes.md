# Move across Time with Concrete Summary and Brief Scenes

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a creative nonfiction narrative must cover a broad stretch of time without becoming bland overview or an aimless sequence of fully rendered memories
**THEN** carry the temporal movement through concrete summary and punctuate it with compact scenes at the moments where enacted detail changes the inquiry
**ELSE** remain in continuous scene when one bounded event contains the necessary development

## Do
- Anchor summary in selected actions, objects, sensory facts, or repeated conditions rather than naming an era with an abstract verdict.
- Enter scene where a choice, encounter, image, or spoken phrase deserves immediate experience and leave once its consequence is established.
- Let each compact scene alter the meaning of the surrounding overview instead of merely illustrating what the summary already said.
- Vary narrative speed according to significance: cross routine intervals quickly and spend space where pressure concentrates.
- Check that a leap across time preserves causal, emotional, or thematic continuity even when intermediate events are omitted.

## Don't
- Summarize years with generic judgments that contain no concrete evidence.
- Render every remembered outfit, room, conversation, or daily action because it remains available to memory.
- Alternate scene and summary mechanically when no change in pressure justifies the shift.
- Let a lively mini-scene distract from the temporal development it was meant to sharpen.
- Omit so much connecting material that readers cannot infer how one condition becomes the next.

## Checklist
- Summary covers duration while retaining concrete language and discernible movement.
- Every scene marks a consequential concentration rather than an arbitrary pause.
- Changes in narrative speed correspond to changes in importance.
- Readers can follow the relation between separated moments without a complete chronology.
- The combined sequence feels both selective and complete for the essay's active inquiry.

## Notes
Narrative compression fails at two extremes: generalized life history erases experience, while exhaustive recollection gives equal weight to everything. Concrete summary preserves texture while crossing distance; short scenes briefly restore full presence where the essay's meaning turns. Their interaction creates pace without confusing speed with haste.
