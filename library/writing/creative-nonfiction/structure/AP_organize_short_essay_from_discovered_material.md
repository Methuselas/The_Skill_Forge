# Organize a Short Essay from Discovered Material

## Objective
Turn an exploratory creative nonfiction draft or fragment set into a complete, concentrated short essay whose structure reveals discovered connections, whose opening and ending carry the same inquiry, and whose shaping remains accountable to fact.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Name the active inquiry and factual contract.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_choose_subject_for_open_ended_exploration` to state what remains genuinely unresolved and `writing_creative_nonfiction_preserve_factual_accountability_while_using_literary_craft` to identify which events, claims, reconstructions, and interpretations must remain supportable. Treat selection and arrangement as available craft; do not improve actuality to fit a cleaner arc. **Advance gate:** the inquiry can change through the available material, and its consequential factual claims have a clear accountability standard.
2. **Inventory material by function and strand.** Mark each usable passage as event, image, context, reflection, information, complication, or turn, and group recurrent subjects without yet preserving draft order. Set aside pieces that have energy but no current relation to the inquiry; they may return only if a later structural need names their function.
3. **Choose the governing relation.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_choose_structure_from_discovered_connections`. Use chronology when change through time supplies the accumulating pressure, thematic braiding when several strands transform one another around a recurring anchor, or segmentation when juxtaposition and omission should carry meaningful leaps. **Advance gate:** one relation explains the order better than composition history or a default school outline does.
4. **Map local focus and reader relocation.** Give every paragraph or segment one principal job, then mark how the reader crosses into the next unit. Preserve explicit transitions where time, place, speaker, causality, or subject would otherwise be lost; preserve white space where the intended inference is the transition. If a segmented sequence feels random, strengthen adjacency or a recurring anchor; if that still fails, return to step 3 and test chronology or braiding.
5. **Select an entry that activates the same inquiry.** Use `writing_creative_nonfiction_open_with_specific_question_or_pressure` to choose an exact detail, situation, request, contrast, or question that creates curiosity and enough footing. If the strongest candidate is detachable spectacle, generic background, or a mystery the essay never develops, replace it with the earliest specific pressure the later material actually answers or complicates. **Advance gate:** the opening's promise remains active in the structural map.
6. **Compress without breaking completion.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_compress_short_essay_around_active_inquiry`. Cut or combine units that duplicate a function, prefer particulars that carry context and implication together, and reread transitions after every large removal. If cutting makes a turn abrupt or a claim unsupported, restore the missing function in its smallest effective form rather than restoring all prior material.
7. **Locate the earned stopping point.** Use `writing_creative_nonfiction_end_at_resonant_turn_or_deepening` to find where an established image, relation, irony, or question changes pressure and gathers the strands. Cut summary and moralizing after that point one sentence at a time. If the ending feels forced, reopen the latest material that genuinely complicates the inquiry and stop at its consequence instead of importing a new revelation.
8. **Run the whole-draft audit.** Activate `writing_revise_whole_draft_toward_intended_form`. Trace the inquiry from opening pressure through every structural move to the ending, then verify that local units support the governing relation and that no transition conceals a factual or logical break. Route an inert inquiry to step 1, disconnected material to step 2, navigational confusion to step 4, and padding or truncation to step 6.
9. **Complete at shaped sufficiency.** The action is complete when the essay creates immediate specific curiosity, develops one active inquiry through a legible structural principle, gives every included unit necessary work, reaches an earned resonant stopping point, and remains accountable for what it asks readers to accept as true. Stop organizing and move to sentence-level revision or proofing rather than reopening the structure without a named mismatch.

## Notes
The protocol begins after exploration has produced enough material to discover relations; it does not require a polished draft. Its dependency order matters: compression before an inquiry and structural principle are stable can remove the very evidence needed to find the essay, while choosing an opening or ending before the middle is mapped can force the work to honor a detachable flourish. Chronology, braiding, and segmentation are routes through the same action, not mandatory ingredients of every essay.
