# Draft and Revise Short Creative Nonfiction

## Objective
Develop a live inquiry into a finished short creative nonfiction essay whose discoveries arise from supportable material, whose literary shaping preserves its factual promise, whose portrayals of real people survive ethical review, and whose structure and prose have passed complete revision.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Establish the inquiry and truth contract.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_choose_subject_for_open_ended_exploration` and `writing_creative_nonfiction_preserve_factual_accountability_while_using_literary_craft`. Name the unresolved question, the writer's stake in it, what the work will ask readers to accept as true, and which kinds of selection, reconstruction, compression, or identity protection remain permissible. For a biographical or autobiographical project, use `writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_revisit_multiple_motives_during_life_writing` to state the current motives, their conflicts, and the experience promised to readers without forcing them into one permanent or morally pure reason. **Advance gate:** the subject can still surprise the writer, and the factual and reader promises are explicit enough to govern later craft choices.
2. **Plan evidence around consequential uncertainty.** Mark what comes from direct observation, direct memory, another person's report, records, research, inference, unconscious impression, reconstruction, or invention. Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_interrogate_memory_before_claiming_precision` for recollection whose detail outruns confidence, `writing_creative_nonfiction_verify_checkable_facts_and_research_necessary_context` for claims or missing context, and `writing_conduct_prepared_and_responsive_interviews` when another person's knowledge can materially test or deepen the inquiry. Use `writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_weight_competing_accounts_by_position_evidence_and_consequence` when versions of one event differ. Run `writing_creative_nonfiction_drill_compare_associative_and_narrative_recall` when a remembered event's fluent story may hide selection or implied causality, `writing_creative_nonfiction_drill_interrogate_photograph_as_bounded_evidence` when an image appears to supply missing scene detail, and `writing_creative_nonfiction_drill_build_attributed_documentary_montage_without_faking_voices` when several records or voices will be arranged together. For a diary-derived passage, run `writing_creative_nonfiction_drill_audit_diary_across_entry_revision_and_publication_layers` before treating the surviving text as contemporaneous evidence. Do not postpone every uncertainty until final fact-checking: later structure must not depend on an event, quotation, or causal claim that may disappear. **Evidence gate:** the material supporting the essay's central pressure is either supportable, responsibly qualified, or clearly marked as an open research task.
3. **Choose the self that can conduct the inquiry.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_select_a_candid_essay_self_for_the_inquiry`. Decide which experiences, limitations, contradictions, and later reflections readers need in order to understand the writer's position. Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_separate_experiencing_self_from_reflective_narrator` to hold the experiencing self within the limits of its moment while the reflective narrator frames and interprets it from a distinct vantage; do not let retrospective authority erase uncertainty or make the earlier self artificially coherent.
4. **Choose and protect the drafting mode.** Activate `writing_separate_generative_and_evaluative_writing_modes`. When the governing route is already understood, use its plan-first variant to map the essential moment, evidence, and reflection before drafting forward. When the meaning remains unknown, use its discovery-first variant with `writing_collect_fragments_before_demanding_complete_draft` to generate scenes, observations, remembered moments, information, reflection, and possible turns before forcing a finished order. In either branch, follow productive contradictions and associations, but label unsupported precision instead of making it fluent enough to escape later notice. Preserve superseded versions until structural choices stabilize. Continue until the material contains more than an opening premise: it must include a complication, changed pressure, or discovery capable of shaping the essay.
5. **Resolve the pre-structure gate.** Review every element on which the emerging interpretation depends. If a checkable claim fails, correct or remove it and reconsider the conclusion it supported. If a memory remains uncertain, qualify it, seek corroboration, or reduce its narrative precision. If research produces no meaningful complication, return to step 1 and sharpen the inquiry rather than arranging inert information. Advance only when enough trustworthy material exists to discover relations without inventing connective facts.
6. **Organize the discovered material.** Invoke `writing_creative_nonfiction_ap_organize_short_essay_from_discovered_material` as the subordinate structural action. Preserve the truth contract and evidentiary qualifications while selecting the governing relation, entry, transitions, compression, and stopping point. When audited diary entries supply the material, activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_build_self_portrait_from_selected_diary_entries` under `writing_creative_nonfiction_choose_structure_from_discovered_connections` so selection and arrangement reveal a bounded self without claiming a complete record. If organization exposes a missing causal link or makes a qualification feel inconvenient, return to step 2 or 5; do not solve the break by smoothing uncertainty out of the prose.
7. **Audit every consequential portrayal before polishing.** Activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_portray_real_people_through_selective_behavioral_detail` so each portrayal rests on supportable actions, speech, gestures, and contradictions rather than a single judgment. When affection, grief, admiration, anger, or condemnation dominates a portrayal, first run `writing_creative_nonfiction_drill_balance_emotional_regard_with_particular_character_evidence` so the emotional claim rests on particular conduct, objects, habits, contradictions, and consequences rather than generic sentiment. Then activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_audit_harm_and_privacy_when_portraying_real_people`. Test recognizability, factual support, necessity, privacy intrusion, foreseeable harm, and likely circulation. Cut gratuitous exposure, use bounded disclosed identity protection where it preserves responsibility and meaning, and seek qualified review for unresolved high-consequence concerns. If the necessary truth and the proposed form cannot coexist safely or responsibly, change the form, narrow circulation, defer publication, or stop; polish is not an override.
8. **Run complete revision.** Invoke `writing_ap_revise_creative_draft_from_diagnosis_to_final_proof`. Diagnose whole-draft failures before sentence editing, gather only the feedback evidence appropriate to the work and its intended audience, make high-consequence changes first, then cross deliberately into editing and proofreading. After every structural revision, recheck any factual qualification, ethical protection, or distinction between past experience and present reflection that the change may have weakened.
9. **Verify completion across all contracts.** Stop when the essay develops rather than merely announces its inquiry; every consequential claim is verified, qualified, or removed; literary shaping has not manufactured actuality; the selected essay-self is candid and relevant; portrayals of real people have passed the harm and privacy audit; the opening, structure, and ending form a concentrated whole; no consequential global mismatch remains; and the final proof is clean. Route a failure back to its owner—question to step 1, evidence to step 2 or 5, self-presentation to step 3, insufficient discovery to step 4, structure to step 6, ethics to step 7, and form or prose to step 8—instead of repairing every problem at sentence level.

## Notes
This protocol owns the complete genre-specific action, not the full craft rules it coordinates. It begins before a substantial draft exists, calls the established organization protocol only after discovery has produced trustworthy material, and calls the general revision protocol only after the essay has a viable governing structure. The order protects against three common continuity failures: arranging an account before its evidence is trustworthy, compressing away qualifications that preserve the nonfiction contract, and polishing a portrayal before deciding whether it should circulate. Research, interviewing, subject review, and outside feedback are conditional branches; their use depends on the material's uncertainties and stakes rather than on ceremony.

For a book-length, sequential, documentary, biographical, or autobiographical project whose basic scope and method are not yet stable, begin with `writing_creative_nonfiction_ap_design_and_stress_test_life_writing_project_contract`. Return here once a bounded short essay or excerpt has enough trustworthy material to draft and revise. The project contract coordinates intentions and constraints; it does not replace the discoveries, evidence checks, ethical review, and structural decisions required by an actual manuscript.
