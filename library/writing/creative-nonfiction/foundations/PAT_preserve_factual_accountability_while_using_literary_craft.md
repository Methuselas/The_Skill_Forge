# Preserve Factual Accountability While Using Literary Craft

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a nonfiction draft uses scene, dialogue, imagery, nonlinear structure, or another technique associated with imaginative writing
**THEN** shape the factual material for literary effect while keeping every consequential claim, reconstruction, and interpretation answerable to what occurred or can be responsibly supported

## Do
- Distinguish selection and arrangement from invention: omit, compress, juxtapose, and emphasize without manufacturing events the work asks readers to accept as true.
- Use formal flexibility to reveal relations among experience, information, memory, and reflection rather than to conceal evidentiary gaps.
- Mark uncertainty, reconstruction, or competing interpretations when the record cannot support one definitive account.
- Disclose material compression, compositing, or identity changes whenever readers could otherwise mistake the shaped version for an unaltered record.
- Let imagery, rhythm, characterization, and narrative movement intensify readers' experience of actuality rather than substitute for it.

## Don't
- Treat the word creative as permission to improve facts until they fit a cleaner plot.
- Assume emotional or interpretive accuracy excuses a fabricated consequential event.
- Present access to another person's thoughts as fact merely because interior narration would make the scene vivid.
- Remove a detail for privacy or pacing without checking whether its absence materially distorts the meaning of what remains.

## Checklist
- The genre signal tells readers that consequential claims are meant to be true.
- Every scene and quotation has a supportable basis or is transparently qualified.
- Structural experimentation changes experience and emphasis without changing what happened.
- Omission preserves the account's interpretive accuracy rather than creating a false impression.
- Any disclosed alteration protects privacy or focus without changing responsibility, outcome, or another consequential truth.
- Literary devices remain effective after unsupported invention is removed.

## Notes
Creative nonfiction shares tools with fiction and poetry, not their full license to invent. Its freedom lies in voice, selection, sequence, form, image, and reflection under a factual promise. That promise does not require a neutral or exhaustive record, but it does require the writer's shaped version to remain recognizably accountable to actuality.

`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_disclose_privacy_protecting_alterations` addresses the constraint created when accurate identifying detail would expose other people unnecessarily. It permits bounded changes to names, places, descriptions, and similar identifiers when the class of alteration is disclosed and the account's responsibility, outcome, and meaning remain intact. A disclaimer cannot convert invented consequential events into nonfiction or excuse a distortion that privacy did not require.
