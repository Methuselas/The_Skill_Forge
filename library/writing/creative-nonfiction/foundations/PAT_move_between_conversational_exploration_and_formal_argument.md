# Move Between Conversational Exploration and Formal Argument

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a creative nonfiction draft defaults entirely to either academic certainty or unstructured personal wandering
**THEN** position and adjust its voice and structure along the continuum between exploratory conversation and formal argument according to the work's inquiry, claim, and reader needs

## Do
- Decide whether the primary reader experience is following a mind at work, receiving a compressed argument, or moving deliberately between both.
- Let changes in certainty alter the prose: speculate while testing, state firmly where evidence supports firmness, and reopen questions when complications remain.
- Use conversational voice to create intimacy and movement without abandoning factual precision.
- Use formal order for genuine clarity rather than as a costume of authority.
- Cross the continuum at purposeful turns, such as moving from anecdote into claim or from confident information into personal doubt.

## Don't
- Treat informal and formal essays as mutually exclusive species whose features cannot mix.
- Equate a conversational tone with careless reasoning or unsupported confession.
- Assume a thesis, moderate register, and orderly paragraphs automatically make a nonfiction work trustworthy.
- Keep readers entertained by performing personality when the essay withholds the information or reflection its subject requires.

## Checklist
- The dominant mode serves a named inquiry, claim, and audience rather than habit.
- Shifts in register correspond to changes in evidence, certainty, or reflective purpose.
- Exploratory passages accumulate meaning instead of merely meandering.
- Formal passages remain open to relevant complication rather than dictating beyond their support.
- Voice engages readers without replacing the essay's substantive work.

## Notes
`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_use_conversational_exploration_to_discover` foregrounds a mind testing its subject. It suits personal essays in which candor, irony, minutiae, and changing thought generate the experience, but it still needs purposeful accumulation.

`writing_creative_nonfiction_variant_use_formal_argument_to_compress_claim` foregrounds clarity, order, and concision. It remains useful when a settled claim needs efficient delivery, but becomes a poor fit when certainty is imposed before discovery. Strong essays may cross between these methods rather than pledging allegiance to one tradition.
