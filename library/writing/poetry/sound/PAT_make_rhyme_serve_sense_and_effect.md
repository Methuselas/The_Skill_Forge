# Make Rhyme Serve Sense and Effect

## Pattern Rule
**IF** rhyme is shaping a poem's diction, line endings, or larger scheme
**THEN** keep each correspondence only when it strengthens meaning, emphasis, order, or a chosen sonic effect without forcing weaker language
**ELSE** release the rhyme or replace it with a looser sound relationship

## Do
- State what the rhyme is meant to contribute, such as closure, authority, comic pressure, emphasis, or a connection between ideas.
- Inspect paired words for the similarity, contrast, or irony created by placing their meanings in audible relation.
- Map a recurring end-rhyme scheme when order matters so the pattern signals a larger purpose rather than accidental matching.
- Replace padding, inverted phrasing, and elaborate setup written only to deliver a matching terminal word.
- Widen the search from exact end rhyme to slant, internal, or visual correspondence when the most accurate diction falls outside the first pattern.

## Don't
- Select an end word for sound alone when another word would make clearer or more specific sense.
- Treat perfect rhyme as the sole measure of musical skill; its ready-made pairs often invite predictability.
- Assign a fixed tone to polysyllabic rhyme: an extended match can sharpen comedy or carry serious verbal intensity.
- Preserve a rhyme scheme merely because abandoning work already invested in it feels wasteful.

## Checklist
- The poem has a defensible reason to rhyme rather than a habit of matching endings.
- Each exposed rhyme word is fresh, precise, and necessary apart from its sound partner.
- Corresponding words create a meaningful relation rather than an empty chime.
- No line is padded, garbled, or syntactically distorted to complete a match.
- The chosen degree and location of correspondence produce the intended closure, flexibility, or tension.

## Notes
Exact correspondence can make a thought feel decisively complete, but that same force magnifies cliche and filler. `writing_poetry_variant_use_perfect_end_rhyme_for_closure` accepts that pressure when conspicuous finality and order are useful. `writing_poetry_variant_use_slant_rhyme_for_flexibility` loosens the match so diction has more room while a relationship remains audible. `writing_poetry_variant_use_internal_rhyme_without_end_word_pressure` moves the echo inside the line when terminal words need other jobs. `writing_poetry_variant_use_sight_rhyme_for_visual_correspondence` makes the page promise a sound that speech withholds, a poor fit when performance removes the spelling from view.

`writing_poetry_variant_vary_rhyme_distance_to_enact_semantic_movement` treats full and slant rhyme as positions on a changing audible field rather than fixed moods. Establish a perceptible relation, then let its approach, recession, or rupture coincide with a meaningful change.

`writing_poetry_variant_use_conspicuous_rhyme_for_comic_anticipation` exposes construction on purpose. Place an almost unavoidable rhyme after a rhythmically fluent setup, delay it until expectation becomes pressure, and make the semantic completion surprising enough that predictability becomes comic rather than facile.
