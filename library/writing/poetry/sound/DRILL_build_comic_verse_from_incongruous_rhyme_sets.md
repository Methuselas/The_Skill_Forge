# Build Comic Verse from Incongruous Rhyme Sets

## Practice Task
Compose a short journey from deliberately incongruous rhyme words, then revise its conspicuous matches and rhythm toward a comic payoff.

## Target Skill
Turning conspicuous rhyme and incongruous diction into timed comic movement.

## Setup
Select unlike words from several rhyme fields and add one full match for each. Choose an improbable mode of transport or destination.

## Instructions
1. Draft a bizarre narrative using as many pairs as produce movement.
2. Identify the match readers can predict most strongly and reserve it for a delayed payoff.
3. Scan the setup and completion; repair stress, syntax, and line length until delivery is fluent.
4. Cut arbitrary oddity that produces no expectation or reversal.
5. Read aloud without explanatory performance and retain only independently audible humor.

## Success Check
- The incongruous words produce a narrative or argumentative movement, not a sequence held together only by rhyme.
- One match is identified as the most predictable and withheld to a delayed position.
- The delay creates anticipation a reader feels before the payoff arrives, rather than merely postponing a word.
- Rhythm delivers the completion, and lines that obstruct it are repaired rather than excused as deliberate.
- Oddity producing no expectation or reversal is cut, including oddity the writer found funny.
- The humour survives a reading without performance; a version that needs delivery to land is rejected on that ground.

## Common Failures
- Assuming bizarre vocabulary guarantees laughter.
- Distorting syntax so the audience hears effort before setup.
- Delivering the obvious rhyme before anticipation develops.

## Notes
Comic rhyme points toward its own construction, but timing converts exposure into pleasure. A failed comic draft may still yield serious material; the generative permission and the final tone are separate decisions.
