# Withhold Motive in Narrative Poetry Only When Detail Supports Inference

## Pattern Rule
**IF** explaining why an unusual event occurred flattens a narrative poem's mystery or pressure
**THEN** remove explicit motive only after actions, objects, sequence, setting, and consequences provide enough evidence for active inference
**ELSE** restore the causal information readers need to understand the event's governing change

## Do
- Draft possible motives and surrounding events first so omission becomes selection rather than ignorance disguised as mystery.
- Focus the finished account on a consequential interval and arrange clue-bearing details in an intelligible sequence.
- Use commands, questions, witness reports, or another narrative frame when that form makes partial access credible.
- End on an image or consequence that concentrates the unresolved pressure without explaining it away.
- When using real incidents, separate verified material from invention and transform or qualify the result according to its truth contract.

## Don't
- Delete the only fact that makes the sequence physically or causally possible.
- Withhold information merely because the writer has not decided what happened.
- Add invented motive to a real tragedy and present the speculation as report.
- Substitute macabre subject matter for controlled selection and narrative movement.

## Checklist
- Readers can reconstruct the event's essential sequence even if motive remains open.
- Every retained detail either orients, supplies evidence, redirects expectation, or intensifies consequence.
- At least one plausible inference is supported without becoming the only forced answer.
- The final image gathers prior pressure rather than arriving as an unrelated shock.
- Factual and invented elements obey the work's declared truth contract.

## Notes
Poetic compression can make absence active. A missing explanation invites readers to assemble relations only when the poem supplies something to assemble; otherwise omission is a blank. Expanding the event before cutting motive lets the writer know which concrete traces can carry implication and which facts are structural necessities.
