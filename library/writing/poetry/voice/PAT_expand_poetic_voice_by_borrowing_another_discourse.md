# Expand Poetic Voice by Borrowing Another Discourse

## Pattern Rule
**IF** repeated personal lyric subjects keep returning the poem to the same scale, vocabulary, or emotional posture
**THEN** enter a contrasting discourse and adapt its terms, structures, questions, and ways of knowing to a live poetic pressure
**ELSE** retain the familiar mode while continuing to test the world beyond the speaker's immediate feelings

## Do
- Choose a field with an actual point of contact: history, science, music, comedy, ritual, public inscription, narrative, craft, or another practice encountered in life or study.
- Collect its characteristic nouns, verbs, classifications, forms of evidence, and sentence movements before deciding how the poem will use them.
- Let the new discourse change what can be noticed and asked, not merely provide unusual terminology.
- Bring unlike domains into confrontation when their relation generates insight, tension, humor, or a new scale.
- Return every borrowed element to the poem's speaker, subject, and movement; cut material that remains an informational display.

## Don't
- Paste jargon into an unchanged personal lyric and call the vocabulary shift a new voice.
- Treat one inherited catalogue of subjects as a universal taxonomy of poetry.
- Borrow sacred, cultural, or technical language without understanding the commitments and meanings it carries.
- Abandon emotional or sensory pressure merely to demonstrate intellectual range.

## Checklist
- The selected discourse changes the poem's attention, syntax, structure, or reasoning as well as its nouns.
- The writer understands the borrowed terms well enough to use them accurately.
- The encounter produces a relation unavailable in the default mode.
- Specialized language remains connected to human, material, or imaginative stakes.
- The result reads as a poem using another discourse, not notes copied from that field.

## Notes
Poetic voice can widen by changing the kinds of messages it attempts to receive. A writer may remain grounded in one place or personal history while reaching toward astronomy, public history, ritual, comedy, or invented translation. The useful expansion is not novelty for its own sake; another discourse earns entry when it generates language and thought the existing mode could not reach alone.
