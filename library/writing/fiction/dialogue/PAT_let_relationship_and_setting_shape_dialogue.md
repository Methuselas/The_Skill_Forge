# Let Relationship and Setting Shape Dialogue

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a line could be transferred unchanged to different speakers, power relations, or locations
**THEN** revise its diction, directness, rhythm, and permitted subjects around who is speaking to whom and where and when the exchange occurs

## Do
- Specify the speakers' relative age, intimacy, authority, dependence, and immediate risk before settling their language.
- Test whether the same thought would be phrased differently in private, before witnesses, during danger, or inside a formal institution.
- Map how audience and occasion alter formality, slang, profanity, occupational vocabulary, code-switching, repeated phrases, and willingness to repair or explain a word.
- Inventory ownership, privacy, witnesses, noise, access, exits, interruptions, concurrent tasks, and environmental conditions that change what the speakers can attempt or perceive.
- Establish each participant's immediate readiness, fatigue, pain, distraction, hope, impairment, or urgency when that state changes attention and conduct.
- Use a time limit only when its expiry removes an option, creates exposure, forces a decision, or changes the cost of delay.
- Let shared habits, jokes, evasions, and verbal games reveal the history of a relationship without explaining that history.
- Use harsh, prejudiced, profane, or nonstandard language when it truthfully belongs to the character and situation, while keeping the work's stance legible through context and consequence.

## Don't
- Give a person the same freedom of speech with a parent, subordinate, stranger, lover, and judge.
- Add dialect or profanity as detachable color unrelated to identity, pressure, or audience.
- Freeze one register into a permanent identity marker when the character would speak differently across intimacy, authority, community, work, or risk.
- Sanitize a character into implausibility solely to prevent readers from confusing representation with endorsement.
- Assume that a vivid exchange remains plausible after moving it to a socially different setting.
- Paste in weather, passersby, symbolic accidents, or a countdown that neither speaker must respond to.
- Treat time of day or a temporary state as a universal behavioral code.

## Checklist
- Each speaker's language reflects a specific relation to the listener.
- The location and occasion affect what can be said and how directly.
- At least one verbal choice reveals power, intimacy, distance, or shared history.
- Charged language belongs to the represented person rather than serving as authorial decoration.
- Slang, profanity, jargon, and repeated wording respond to this listener and occasion; any period-specific language dates the scene by design rather than accident.
- Moving the exchange elsewhere would require meaningful revision.
- Environmental constraints and participant state affect speech, attention, action, or interpretation rather than supplying backdrop alone.
- Any deadline has a credible expiry event and a named consequence.

## Notes
Context dictates dialogue because speech is social action. A line addresses a particular person under particular permissions and pressures. Truthful representation can include offensive language without making the writer's approval the default reading, but the surrounding characterization and consequences must establish that distinction.

`writing_fiction_variant_let_communication_channel_shape_dialogue` extends the context test from physical setting to mediated access. Inventory what each participant can see, hear, touch, edit, preserve, replay, conceal, and interrupt, then make at least one difference change interpretation or action. Use the variant when the channel alters the exchange; omit channel business when the same dialogue and consequence would survive unchanged.

`writing_fiction_variant_constrain_dialogue_with_consequential_deadline` makes limited time part of the speakers' available choices. Define the event that ends or transforms the opportunity, what becomes impossible or newly costly afterward, and how each person responds to that knowledge. Use the deadline when delay itself becomes action; remove it when the same lines and outcome would survive without the clock.
