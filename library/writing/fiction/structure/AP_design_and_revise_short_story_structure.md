# Design and Revise Short Story Structure

## Objective
Shape a short fiction premise or draft into a temporally coherent, consequential story whose causal movement or patterned accumulation begins at a live entry, reaches an earned culmination, and stops after the last necessary turn.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Name the governing story.** Use `writing_fiction_drill_reduce_story_to_logline` to state the active situation, relation, or pattern and its pressure. If several statements compete, draft each as a possible center and choose the one whose movement the intended form can sustain. **Advance gate:** one governing movement or pattern can be stated without listing subplots.
2. **Pass the scope gate.** Activate `writing_fiction_match_story_scope_to_required_accumulation`. List the worldbuilding, suspense, obstacles, and delayed effects required before the climax can matter. If the available length cannot carry them, narrow the premise or expand the form before arranging scenes.
3. **Choose the structural carrier.** When motive, choice, and consequence drive the story, activate `writing_fiction_build_plot_from_causal_consequences` and record how every major event changes later options. When recurrence, ritual, instruction, or associative accumulation carries the movement, activate `writing_fiction_reinforce_story_structure_through_repetition_and_variation` and record how each return adds pressure or changes meaning. Combine the carriers when both perform necessary work. Return to step 1 if incidents or repetitions accumulate without a governing consequence or transition.
4. **Map time and allocate narrative speed.** Activate `writing_fiction_map_chronology_before_compressing_story_time`. Establish the underlying chronology, mark any dramatic present, and justify every habitual passage, flashback, forward move, or compressed transition. Activate `writing_fiction_anchor_narrative_time_in_dramatic_present_and_habitual_world` when a present action and recurrent life must work together. Then activate `writing_fiction_allocate_scene_and_summary_by_narrative_pressure`: expand the turns whose exact action, perception, or speech carries the story, and summarize connective duration or background. For movement between locations, activate `writing_fiction_omit_eventless_travel_between_consequential_scenes`; dramatize the route only when transit changes pressure, knowledge, relation, or consequence. Preserve temporal and spatial orientation and knowledgeable omission as invariants.
5. **Choose the live entry.** Activate `writing_fiction_begin_at_point_of_attack`. Test the latest point that supplies enough footing for the active situation, relation, pattern, decision, crisis, or encounter. Add `writing_fiction_foreshadow_consequence_while_withholding_route` only when a prospective question should organize anticipation. **Advance gate:** the opening already contains the governing pressure or pattern, and earlier material has been condensed, redistributed, or removed without creating a factual hole.
6. **Make the middle move.** For conflict-led stories, activate `writing_fiction_escalate_central_conflict_through_middle` and grow pressure through narrowing focus, selected scenes, intensifying perception, or causal escalation. For patterned stories, accumulate recurrences whose changed context, voice, scale, or implication makes movement legible. Cut or combine a middle unit that neither changes conditions nor deepens the governing pattern.
7. **Place the culmination and ending.** Locate the point of maximum dramatic attention, decisive variation, or terminal reinterpretation produced by the prior design. For flash fiction, activate `writing_flash_fiction_compress_complete_story_without_dead_space`; stop at impact when the reader can supply the response, or retain only the brief denouement that changes or completes a remaining complication. Test deletion of the final paragraph or recurrence before accepting it.
8. **Run the discovery recovery.** After a complete draft, compare the designed center with what the writing actually made consequential. If discovery exposed a stronger story or turned the sequence into a maze, activate `writing_fiction_variant_redesign_structure_after_draft_discovery` through `writing_revise_whole_draft_toward_intended_form`, revise the governing logline, and return to the earliest invalidated step.
9. **Complete structurally.** Stop when the story has one intelligible governing movement or pattern, temporal relations are oriented, pressure or recurrence develops toward an earned culmination, the beginning is the latest viable live entry, the ending contains no trailing explanation, and removing any retained unit would break character, pressure, progression, pattern, or consequence.

## Notes
The protocol orders decisions because later structure depends on earlier clarity. A live entry cannot be chosen reliably before the central story and temporal design are known; a culmination cannot be earned before the middle creates development through consequence, accumulation, or variation; an ending cannot be judged before that development is legible. The flow remains recursive: drafting may discover a better center, but recovery returns to the decision that changed rather than patching downstream scenes around an obsolete plan.
