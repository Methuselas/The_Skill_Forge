# Design and Revise Short Story Structure

## Objective
Shape a short fiction premise or draft into a temporally coherent, consequential story whose causal movement or patterned accumulation begins at a live entry, reaches an earned culmination, and stops after the last necessary turn.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Name the governing story and experienced meaning.** Use `writing_fiction_drill_reduce_story_to_logline` to state the active situation, relation, or pattern and its pressure. Privately name the larger issue or judgment the story may be testing. If that thematic hypothesis remains abstract or supplies no events, activate `writing_fiction_drill_turn_a_theme_map_into_dramatic_choices_and_consequences`; then activate `writing_fiction_embody_story_meaning_in_concrete_action_and_detail` so the resulting pressure is enacted rather than pasted onto the draft. If several centers compete, choose the one whose movement the intended form can sustain. **Advance gate:** one governing movement or pattern can be stated without listing subplots, while its meaning remains open to what the story discovers.
2. **Pass the scope and promise gates.** Activate `writing_fiction_match_story_scope_to_required_accumulation`. List the worldbuilding, suspense, obstacles, and delayed effects required before the culmination can matter. If the available length cannot carry them, narrow the premise, alter the treatment, or expand the form before arranging scenes. Activate `writing_fiction_signal_and_revise_genre_expectations` to name the primary reader promise and remove, transform, or support cues that advertise another story accidentally.
3. **Choose the structural carrier.** When motive, choice, and consequence drive the story, activate `writing_fiction_build_plot_from_causal_consequences` and record how every major event changes later options. When recurrence, ritual, instruction, or associative accumulation carries the movement, activate `writing_fiction_reinforce_story_structure_through_repetition_and_variation` and record how each return adds pressure or changes meaning. Combine the carriers when both perform necessary work. Return to step 1 if incidents or repetitions accumulate without a governing consequence or transition.
4. **Map time, knowledge, and narrative speed.** Activate `writing_fiction_map_chronology_before_compressing_story_time`. Establish the underlying chronology, mark any dramatic present, and justify every habitual passage, flashback, forward move, or compressed transition. Activate `writing_fiction_anchor_narrative_time_in_dramatic_present_and_habitual_world` when a present action and recurrent life must work together. Use `writing_fiction_stage_disclosure_to_sustain_reader_inference` to mark what the narrator, characters, and readers know at each turn and schedule exposition where it creates footing, pressure, or reinterpretation; when a mistaken recollection drives action, use its false-memory correction variant to plant fair friction and deliver the correction through consequential evidence. Then activate `writing_fiction_allocate_scene_and_summary_by_narrative_pressure`: expand the turns whose exact action, perception, or speech carries the story, and summarize connective duration or background. For movement between locations, activate `writing_fiction_omit_eventless_travel_between_consequential_scenes`; dramatize the route only when transit changes pressure, knowledge, relation, or consequence. Preserve temporal, spatial, and knowledge orientation as invariants.
5. **Choose the live entry.** Activate `writing_fiction_begin_at_point_of_attack`. Test the latest point that supplies enough footing for the active situation, relation, pattern, decision, crisis, or encounter. Add `writing_fiction_foreshadow_consequence_while_withholding_route` only when a prospective question should organize anticipation. **Advance gate:** the opening already contains the governing pressure or pattern, and earlier material has been condensed, redistributed, or removed without creating a factual hole.
6. **Make the middle move.** For conflict-led stories, activate `writing_fiction_escalate_central_conflict_through_middle` and grow pressure through narrowing focus, selected scenes, intensifying perception, or causal escalation. For patterned stories, accumulate recurrences whose changed context, voice, scale, or implication makes movement legible. Cut or combine a middle unit that neither changes conditions nor deepens the governing pattern.
7. **Place the culmination and ending.** Locate the point of maximum dramatic attention, decisive variation, or terminal reinterpretation produced by the prior design. Activate `writing_fiction_end_selected_story_without_exhausting_larger_life` to separate the governing story's necessary completion from every later fact about the characters' lives. For flash fiction, also activate `writing_flash_fiction_compress_complete_story_without_dead_space`; stop at impact when the reader can supply the response, or retain only the brief denouement that changes or completes a remaining complication. Test deletion of the final paragraph or recurrence before accepting it.
8. **Run the discovery recovery.** After a complete draft, compare the designed center with what the writing actually made consequential. If discovery exposed a stronger story or turned the sequence into a maze, activate `writing_fiction_variant_redesign_structure_after_draft_discovery` through `writing_revise_whole_draft_toward_intended_form`, revise the governing logline, and return to the earliest invalidated step.
9. **Complete structurally.** Stop when the story has one intelligible governing movement or pattern; temporal and knowledge relations are oriented; its genre promise is fulfilled, combined, or deliberately redirected; concrete action and detail carry its experienced meaning; pressure or recurrence develops toward an earned culmination; the beginning is the latest viable live entry; the selected story ends without trailing explanation; and removing any retained unit would break character, pressure, progression, pattern, inference, or consequence.

## Notes
The protocol orders decisions because later structure depends on earlier clarity. A live entry cannot be chosen reliably before the central story and temporal design are known; a culmination cannot be earned before the middle creates development through consequence, accumulation, or variation; an ending cannot be judged before that development is legible. The flow remains recursive: drafting may discover a better center, but recovery returns to the decision that changed rather than patching downstream scenes around an obsolete plan.

`writing_fiction_variant_generate_structure_backward_from_desired_culmination` provides an alternate invention entry point rather than a different theory of structure. Start from a desired culmination and derive its prerequisites backward, then reverse direction and test the resulting story forward so every necessary condition is actually caused, motivated, and earned. The ending remains a design hypothesis rather than a commitment: if forward validation requires implausible character behavior or reveals a stronger governing story, revise or discard the planned culmination instead of forcing the draft to reach it.
