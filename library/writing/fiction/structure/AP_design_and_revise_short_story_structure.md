# Design and Revise Short Story Structure

## Objective
Shape a short fiction premise or draft into a causal, temporally coherent, escalating story that begins at its live point of attack, reaches an earned climax, and stops after the last necessary consequence.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Name the governing story.** Use `writing_fiction_drill_reduce_story_to_logline` to state the active situation and trouble. If several loglines compete, draft each as a possible center and choose the one whose consequence the intended form can sustain. **Advance gate:** one governing movement can be stated without listing subplots.
2. **Pass the scope gate.** Activate `writing_fiction_match_story_scope_to_required_accumulation`. List the worldbuilding, suspense, obstacles, and delayed effects required before the climax can matter. If the available length cannot carry them, narrow the premise or expand the form before arranging scenes.
3. **Build the causal chain.** Activate `writing_fiction_build_plot_from_causal_consequences`. For every major event, record its cause, what it changes, and which later option it creates or removes. Return to step 1 if the draft remains a sequence of incidents with no governing consequence.
4. **Map time and allocate narrative speed.** Activate `writing_fiction_map_chronology_before_compressing_story_time`. Establish the before-during-after timeline, mark the narrated present, and justify every flashback, forward move, or compressed transition. Then activate `writing_fiction_allocate_scene_and_summary_by_narrative_pressure`: expand the turns whose exact action, perception, or speech carries the story, and summarize connective duration or background. For movement between locations, activate `writing_fiction_omit_eventless_travel_between_consequential_scenes`; dramatize the route only when transit changes pressure, knowledge, relation, or consequence. Preserve temporal and spatial orientation and knowledgeable omission as invariants.
5. **Choose the point of attack.** Activate `writing_fiction_begin_at_point_of_attack`. Test the latest moment that still supplies enough footing for the imminent decision, crisis, or encounter. **Advance gate:** the opening contains live pressure, and earlier material has been condensed, redistributed, or removed without creating a factual hole.
6. **Make the middle move.** Activate `writing_fiction_escalate_central_conflict_through_middle`. Identify the trouble early and choose how pressure will grow: narrowing focus, selected scenes across time, intensifying perception, or another causal escalation. If a middle scene changes nothing, cut it, combine it, or make its consequence alter what follows.
7. **Place the climax and ending.** Locate the point of maximum dramatic attention produced by the prior chain. For flash fiction, activate `writing_flash_fiction_compress_complete_story_without_dead_space`; stop at impact when the reader can supply the response, or retain only the brief denouement that changes or completes a remaining complication. Test deletion of the final paragraph before accepting it.
8. **Run the discovery recovery.** After a complete draft, compare the designed center with what the writing actually made consequential. If discovery exposed a stronger story or turned the sequence into a maze, activate `writing_fiction_variant_redesign_structure_after_draft_discovery` through `writing_revise_whole_draft_toward_intended_form`, revise the governing logline, and return to the earliest invalidated step.
9. **Complete structurally.** Stop when the story has one intelligible causal movement, all temporal shifts are oriented, conflict develops toward an earned climax, the beginning is the latest viable entry, the ending contains no trailing explanation, and removing any retained scene would break character, pressure, progression, or consequence.

## Notes
The protocol orders decisions because later structure depends on earlier clarity. A point of attack cannot be chosen reliably before the central story and chronology are known; a climax cannot be earned before the middle creates rising pressure; an ending cannot be judged before the climax is placed. The flow remains recursive: drafting may discover a better center, but recovery returns to the decision that changed rather than patching downstream scenes around an obsolete plan.
