# Structure Multiple-Viewpoint Narratives by Function and Orientation

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a fiction project has committed to more than one viewpoint and must arrange their accounts across the whole narrative
**THEN** choose a macrostructure that sequences each viewpoint by narrative function, gives it enough space to fulfill that function, and identifies every access change before new private knowledge depends on it
**ELSE** consolidate the story under one governing account when additional positions add relocation cost without necessary work

## Do
- Map the underlying chronology for every viewpoint before choosing the presented order; mark encounters, overlaps, absences, knowledge changes, and events available to only one position.
- Assign each section a viewpoint-specific job such as supplying exclusive evidence, changing allegiance, advancing an independent pressure, reframing a shared event, or transmitting another account.
- Allocate space by the work each strand must complete rather than forcing equal percentages; give a secondary viewpoint enough recurrence or concentration to keep its promise legible.
- Establish the first access change early enough to teach the transition system, then use headings, dates, names, opening actions, observations, voice, or visible section boundaries in combinations suited to the design.
- Inspect long absences and dense clusters as possible failures of continuity, then retain them only when delay, acceleration, or changed emphasis is the intended effect.
- Remove the smallest or least independent viewpoint provisionally and route its indispensable information through observation, dialogue, testimony, documents, or report before deciding that direct access is necessary.

## Don't
- Equate balance with equal page count when the viewpoints perform unequal but necessary work.
- Obey a fixed rotation after causality or pressure makes another sequence stronger.
- Depend on a character-name heading to repair an opening whose knowledge, attention, and voice do not establish the active position.
- Shift access inside an unbroken beat before readers receive evidence that relocation has occurred.
- Preserve an embedded story, relay, or alternative reading order because its architecture looks ingenious while its placement changes nothing.

## Checklist
- Every viewpoint section has a necessary job that cannot be transferred without a named loss.
- The underlying character timelines remain coherent beneath the presented sequence.
- Gaps, clusters, and unequal shares have functional reasons rather than arising from drafting convenience.
- Readers can locate the active viewpoint before encountering knowledge available only to it.
- Repeated events, frame returns, and handoffs alter evidence, interpretation, pressure, allegiance, or consequence.
- The chosen topology produces an effect that a simpler single account or linear rotation would lose.

## Notes
Balance is functional before it is numerical. Length strongly influences reader allegiance, but equal shares can be just as misleading as accidental imbalance when one strand carries the central action and another performs a concentrated revelation. Map quantity so it becomes visible, then judge whether each allocation fulfills the promise created when that viewpoint first appears.

`writing_fiction_variant_reinterpret_complete_account_through_later_account` uses large blocks rather than frequent alternation. The first account must feel provisionally meaningful, while the later account contributes access or motive that makes rereading mental work necessary; replay only the portions whose meaning changes.

`writing_fiction_variant_rotate_parallel_viewpoint_strands` interleaves ongoing accounts. A recurring order can teach expectation, but consequence outranks symmetry: break or vary the cycle when the deviation itself is legible and necessary. Chapter labels are one orientation aid, not the source of viewpoint identity.

`writing_fiction_variant_pass_narrative_through_relay_or_nested_frames` makes transmission visible. Establish who tells whom, how the account reaches the next level, what each intermediary can comment on or distort, and how the narrative returns. An enclosing story remains active when receiving the embedded account changes its listener, teller, or present action.

`writing_fiction_variant_permit_reader_selected_account_order` treats sequence as a controlled variable. Each starting point must supply enough footing to stand first, while the second account should feel different because the first established questions and allegiance. If every order produces the same understanding, reader choice adds handling without narrative consequence.
