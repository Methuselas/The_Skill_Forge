# Develop and Reveal a Short-Story Character

## Objective
Design and draft a short-story character whose desire, contradictions, viewpoint, behavior, and degree of change form a coherent person that readers discover through consequential action rather than explanatory inventory.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Name the story function without reducing the person to it.** State what the character wants in this story, what obstructs that desire, and which choice or pressure will make the person consequential. Preserve the central desire and story pressure as invariants. **Advance gate:** the character can alter events rather than merely occupy a role.
2. **Build usable complexity.** Activate `writing_fiction_build_round_characters_from_contradiction_and_desire`. Pair the desire with countervailing motives, qualities, or loyalties that can collide under pressure. If the character derives from life, use `writing_fiction_transform_real_people_into_composite_characters` before drafting so loyalty to biography does not determine the fiction.
3. **Establish interior credibility.** If judgment or dislike is keeping the character external, activate `writing_fiction_inhabit_unsympathetic_characters_from_inside`. When consequential lived conditions fall outside the writer's experience, activate `writing_fiction_portray_characters_beyond_direct_experience_with_accountable_imagination` before treating empathy as knowledge. When the body materially affects access, perception, conduct, relationship, or consequence, activate `writing_fiction_treat_character_bodies_as_lived_conditions`; distinguish physical facts, environmental interaction, social treatment, self-interpretation, and adaptation before inferring personality. If illness, injury, diagnosis, or acute bodily stress changes immediate capacity, use `writing_fiction_variant_test_character_under_acute_bodily_stress_without_treating_response_as_essence`; run `writing_fiction_drill_draft_illness_as_a_situated_character_pressure` when symptoms, care, institutional conditions, or disclosure remain generic. Then use `writing_fiction_drill_build_hidden_character_profile` to generate private history, habits, fears, capabilities, and relationships; use its private-resume variant when education, work, competence, or institutional access remains generic. When family inheritance, an ensemble, concealed alliance, indirect dependency, or uneven knowledge drives the pressure, use `writing_fiction_drill_map_character_relationships_by_knowledge_and_pressure`, including its generational-kinship variant when relevant. Use `writing_fiction_drill_trace_character_continuity_across_a_whole_life_timeline` when the visible story window cannot yet be distinguished from the larger life. **Advance gate:** the writer knows more than the story will state, has investigated material gaps, and can explain the character's harmful or surprising choices without excusing them.
4. **Choose the revealing access.** Activate `writing_fiction_choose_point_of_view_by_access_distance_and_effect`. Decide whether this character narrates, becomes the focal consciousness, or is observed by someone whose bias creates the stronger revelation. If essential complexity remains inaccessible, change the viewpoint or create behavior through which it can be inferred.
5. **Bind person to place and relation.** Activate `writing_fiction_filter_setting_through_character_consciousness` so attention, naming, competence, and omission reveal the character's relation to the setting. Make any consequential embodiment interact with actual distances, tools, clothing, architecture, tasks, resources, and other people rather than remaining a portrait fact. When the character's room, workplace, clothing, or belongings must reveal them in absence or before direct explanation, activate `writing_fiction_reveal_character_through_possessions_and_inhabited_space`; use its social-context presentation variant when work, privacy, occasion, reunion, disguise, or status changes what the character wears or carries. For spoken scenes, use `writing_fiction_combine_dialogue_with_action_thought_and_silence` so gestures, pauses, and unspoken reactions qualify what is said.
6. **Stage incremental evidence.** Activate `writing_fiction_reveal_character_incrementally_through_behavior`. Order details and choices so each appearance confirms, complicates, or overturns an initial reader judgment; keep observable bodily fact, viewpoint interpretation, and later behavioral evidence distinct. When routine conduct remains generic or one activity is being mistaken for a complete personality test, run `writing_fiction_drill_reveal_character_through_contrasting_everyday_conduct`; use its competitive-or-cooperative-play variant when a shared activity should expose relationship pressure. If embodiment remains static or deterministic, run `writing_fiction_drill_trace_one_embodied_condition_through_a_characters_life`. If a past event must enter through present consciousness, use the present-triggered-memory variant of `writing_drill_dramatize_bounded_memory_without_faking_certainty` and require the recollection to alter the live moment. If speech remains interchangeable, use `writing_fiction_drill_differentiate_character_voices_through_parallel_monologues`; if characterization relies on one channel or repeats the same fact across several, use `writing_fiction_drill_render_one_character_through_five_portrayal_channels`. When internal deliberation detaches from the immediate scene or arbitrary activity merely spaces the thoughts, use that drill's **Interleave Deliberation with Consequential Action and Perception** variant; preserve uninterrupted interiority when its mental movement already changes interpretation, commitment, decision, or available action. Route a static trait list back to step 2; route an unprepared reversal back to earlier behavioral evidence.
7. **Fit the cast and arc to the form.** Use `writing_flash_fiction_compress_cast_without_flattening_main_characters` when secondary figures multiply beyond the story's capacity. Activate `writing_fiction_scale_character_change_to_story_length` to choose a credible magnitude of change. **Advance gate:** every retained character creates distinct pressure, and the focal character's turn can be earned within the available span.
8. **Audit and complete.** Remove profile facts that never affect perception, relation, decision, or consequence. Stop when the character pursues a legible desire, contains activated contradiction, behaves with surprising coherence, is revealed through selected evidence, and changes—or meaningfully resists change—at a scale the story can support.

## Notes
The flow separates private construction from public revelation. A hidden profile supplies causal depth, but the story earns character through attention, choices, speech, silence, and consequences. Imagination can cross biography only when the writer treats confidence as a starting permission rather than evidence of competence. Short fiction cannot display every known fact; compression should remove redundant cast functions and inert biography while preserving the contradictions that make the central person difficult to summarize.
