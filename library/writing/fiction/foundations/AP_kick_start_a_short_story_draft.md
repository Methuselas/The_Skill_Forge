# Kick-Start a Short-Story Draft

## Objective
Move from blankness, scattered fragments, or an inert premise to a complete private short-story draft with a character under pressure, a credible narrative stance, and a consequential change ready for later revision.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Set a reachable drafting contract.** Activate `writing_recover_block_by_lowering_hurdle`. Name a short form, a private first-draft session, and an observable finish line; suspend sentence polish, proofreading, and external judgment. Preserve only two invariants during generation: the draft remains short enough to finish, and it develops one central pressure. **Advance gate:** the current task asks for produced story material rather than excellence.
2. **Enter through the available-material branch.** If charged fragments already exist, retrieve them with `writing_collect_fragments_before_demanding_complete_draft` and select a cluster that creates further questions. If the problem is known but options are scarce, activate `writing_generate_relevant_options_with_goal_directed_brainstorming`. If the page is wholly blank, use `writing_fiction_turn_concrete_stimuli_into_story_pressure` on an observed object, image, person, anecdote, or place. Stop generating options once one route produces a character with trouble; continued collection then becomes avoidance.
3. **Ignite the story engine.** Activate `writing_fiction_build_round_characters_from_contradiction_and_desire`. State what the central character wants badly enough to act, what resists that desire now, and which conflicting motive can complicate the choice. If the premise remains only an atmosphere, object, or topic, route it back through concrete stimulus questions until someone can make a consequential move. **Advance gate:** desire and resistance can generate at least one action and a changed condition.
4. **Fit the ambition to the span.** Activate `writing_fiction_match_story_scope_to_required_accumulation`. Reduce elapsed time, cast, locations, or backstory when the intended change needs more development than the short form can hold. Expand the target only when the material's required accumulation is the point and the task permits a longer story. Preserve the chosen character pressure while changing scope.
5. **Choose a working narrative stance.** Use `writing_fiction_ap_choose_and_stabilize_narrative_perspective` only far enough to establish a narrator, access boundary, and tense that can carry the first draft. If viewpoint testing consumes the session without producing events, choose the simplest position that grants the essential knowledge and defer fine calibration to revision.
6. **Find the live entry.** Activate `writing_fiction_begin_at_point_of_attack`. Begin where delay, decision, encounter, or trouble is already active and let background arrive only when present action needs it. When attachment to the first idea prevents selection, use `writing_fiction_drill_generate_and_cull_multiple_story_openings`; otherwise draft one viable opening and continue. **Advance gate:** the opening supplies footing and pressure, not merely biography or mood.
7. **Draft through consequence.** Follow the character's attempts and the causal resistance they create without stopping to correct every sentence. When motion stalls, ask what the character tries next, what that attempt changes, or which contradiction makes the obvious response impossible. Route missing material to step 2, inert motive to step 3, oversized development to step 4, and exposition-heavy delay to step 6.
8. **Reach rough completion.** Use `writing_flash_fiction_compress_complete_story_without_dead_space` as the completeness test, not as a demand for polished compression. Carry the initiating condition through a turn, break, repair, recognition, or other consequence that changes the story's available meaning or action. Do not force a surprise twist when another consequential ending completes the movement.
9. **Stop at the correct boundary.** Mark gaps, doubtful sentences, and research needs without repairing them during this pass. The action is complete when one private draft has a discernible entry, development, and consequence; its viewpoint permits the necessary access; and its central character's desire has produced change. Move next to revision rather than reopening idea generation by default.

## Notes
The protocol protects generation from two opposite failures: waiting for a complete plan before writing and accumulating prompts long after a viable story has appeared. Its gates deliberately require only enough structure, scope, and perspective to sustain a first pass. A rough draft may be awkward or incomplete at the sentence level while still completing the action; final compression, style, and correctness belong to revision.
