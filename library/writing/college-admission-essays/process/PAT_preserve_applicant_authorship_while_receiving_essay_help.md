# Preserve Applicant Authorship While Receiving Essay Help

## Pattern Rule
**IF** another person is invited to assist with a college admission essay's subject, evidence, structure, prose, or revision
**THEN** scope the help to questions, factual recall, reader experience, and clearly marked suggestions while the applicant retains every consequential choice and all submitted language
**ELSE** work independently and resolve uncertain assistance boundaries against the current application's rules before accepting help

## Do
- Check the live application instructions for authorship, assistance, and disclosure obligations before deciding what outside involvement is permissible.
- Ask for a named kind of help: memories about an event, questions about a topic, places a reader became confused, the impression the draft creates, or errors to inspect.
- Treat another person's recollection as a perspective to compare with memory and records; omit, qualify, or responsibly frame a detail when its factual basis remains uncertain.
- Ask readers to report their experience and explain suggested changes, then decide whether each change serves the prompt, intended meaning, and applicant's actual voice.
- Keep shared-document feedback in comments or visible suggestions until the applicant has reviewed and accepted or rejected each change individually.
- Answer any required question about assistance truthfully and preserve enough control to explain every consequential decision in the submitted essay.

## Don't
- Buy, copy, commission, or submit prose another person produced as the applicant's own response.
- Let a helper choose the final topic, arrange the outline, rewrite a passage, or manufacture a strategic persona while the applicant merely approves the result.
- Treat a knowledgeable or forceful helper's preference as authority over the applicant's truthful material and intended meaning.
- Accept a batch of digital edits without understanding how each one changes content, structure, implication, or voice.
- Invent a missing fact or quotation because a helper believes the imagined version makes the story stronger.

## Checklist
- The assistance complies with the current application's stated rules and any required disclosure is accurate.
- The applicant made the final decisions about topic, selection, order, interpretation, and wording.
- Factual contributions from helpers remain distinguishable from unsupported invention or overconfident memory.
- Every retained suggestion is understood and could be defended as serving the applicant's purpose rather than the helper's preferred essay.
- The final voice remains recognizably the applicant's, with no unreviewed language or invisible edits from another writer.

## Notes
Legitimate assistance increases the applicant's access to memories, questions, and reader evidence without transferring authorship. The practical boundary is control: a helper may expose a decision the applicant needs to make, but may not make that decision and disguise the result as support. Visible suggestions are safer than direct revision only when the applicant still evaluates them one by one; change tracking is not an integrity guarantee by itself.
