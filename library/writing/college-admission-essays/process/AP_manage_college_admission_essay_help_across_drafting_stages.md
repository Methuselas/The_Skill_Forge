# Manage College Admission Essay Help Across Drafting Stages

## Objective
Obtain useful outside perspective, factual assistance, and revision evidence throughout a college admission essay process without surrendering the applicant's authorship, voice, judgment, or compliance with the live application's rules.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Establish the assistance contract.** Read the current application's authorship, assistance, and disclosure instructions, then activate `writing_college_admission_essays_preserve_applicant_authorship_while_receiving_help`. State which decisions and language must remain the applicant's and what help is currently being requested. **Entry gate:** do not invite assistance whose permissibility or required disclosure remains unresolved.
2. **Choose a primary early reader.** Select one trusted person who respects privacy, asks before editing, and can describe reader experience without taking control. Use this person for continuity while the topic and early draft remain fragile. If the person presses to dictate subject, structure, language, or persona, narrow the request; if the boundary still fails, replace the helper rather than negotiate authorship away.
3. **Bound help during topic development.** Use the topic-development protocol while allowing the primary reader to ask retrieval questions, recall possible episodes, or report which proposed subject they understand. The reader may broaden the option pool, but the applicant runs the admission-material gate and chooses the final subject. If a helper argues from another applicant's acceptance or from a supposedly guaranteed topic, discard that rationale.
4. **Gather factual detail without importing prose.** When another participant can recover an event, activate `writing_conduct_prepared_and_responsive_interviews`; when accounts conflict or precision outruns confidence, activate `writing_creative_nonfiction_interrogate_memory_before_claiming_precision`. Record facts and perspectives as material for the applicant to shape. **Evidence gate:** no helper-supplied sentence enters the draft as a substitute for the applicant's writing, and no uncertain detail is upgraded into fact for vividness.
5. **Protect structural and drafting ownership.** During outlining, ask helpers where sequence, emphasis, or relation becomes unclear, but require the applicant to decide what moves, enters, or leaves. During prose generation, work independently enough for the applicant's language and associations to form before exposing them to revision pressure. A helper may answer a bounded factual or usage question; they may not compose the passage.
6. **Request diagnosis after a substantial draft exists.** Tell the primary reader which evidence is needed: where attention drops, what impression the applicant creates, which relation is unclear, or whether the response answers the prompt. Invite questions and reader reports before prescriptions. Then invoke the general revision protocol and have the applicant perform every rewrite.
7. **Add secondary readers only for named gaps.** After the draft is structurally stable, invite a small number of additional readers only when each has a defined diagnostic job, such as prompt fit, unfamiliar-reader comprehension, voice recognition, or final error detection. If responses conflict, activate `writing_weight_feedback_by_criteria_and_experience`; seek the shared diagnosis beneath incompatible remedies and choose according to the essay's purpose.
8. **Control the collaboration interface.** Keep edits visible as comments, suggestions, or a marked copy. Review each proposed change individually for meaning, fact, prompt fit, and voice before accepting, adapting, or declining it. If a reader supplies extensive replacement language, return the draft to the last applicant-authored version and use the comments only to identify problems.
9. **Manage over-involvement without abandoning support.** Share the work schedule and next review point with anxious stakeholders, make specific bounded requests, and redirect willing help toward appropriate application tasks. When ordinary boundaries repeatedly fail, ask a trusted counselor or other qualified adult to reinforce the authorship requirement. Preserve private exploratory writing until the applicant chooses to circulate it.
10. **Complete with an authorship audit.** Stop when the applicant can explain every consequential topic, evidence, structural, interpretive, and wording choice; all outside language has been removed or consciously rewritten into the applicant's own expression; factual uncertainty is responsibly handled; reader feedback has been resolved rather than accumulated; the essay still sounds like the applicant; and any required assistance disclosure is truthful.

## Notes
This protocol manages assistance rather than drafting the essay itself. One primary early reader reduces competing visions while the work is unstable; it is not a permanent ban on additional review. Later readers earn access by supplying a named perspective, not by increasing the vote count. Questions and reader effects preserve applicant agency more reliably than prescriptive rewrites, but even a well-phrased suggestion remains advice rather than authority.
