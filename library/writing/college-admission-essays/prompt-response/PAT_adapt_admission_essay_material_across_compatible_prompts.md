# Adapt Admission Essay Material Across Compatible Prompts

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a drafted experience or idea seems usable for more than one college admission prompt
**THEN** compare the prompts' underlying obligations and reuse only compatible material while rewriting each response for its exact question, institution, and word limit
**ELSE** draft a distinct response rather than forcing a generic fit

## Do
- State what each prompt asks the reader to understand about the applicant before deciding that two prompts are equivalent.
- Reuse a topic, experience, detail set, or opening more readily than a complete response; rebuild the interpretation and ending around the current question.
- Meet a shorter limit by selecting the material that carries the required meaning instead of merely cutting sentences from a longer answer.
- Verify every institution name, program reference, and claim of fit after adapting existing material.
- Build an institution-specific fit response from that school's actual opportunities and the applicant's reasons for valuing them.

## Don't
- Assume that prompts with similar keywords ask for the same reflection, quality, or evidence.
- Paste a complete answer unchanged merely because both applications allow it.
- Swap one institution's name for another while leaving generic praise and the old response's logic intact.
- Reuse a fit response across institutions as if the reasons for choosing them were interchangeable.
- Leave the former prompt's emphasis, length, or institution name inside an adapted draft.

## Checklist
- The response answers the current prompt rather than the prompt that first produced its material.
- Reused events and details remain truthful, relevant, and proportioned to the requested meaning.
- Reflection and conclusion serve this question instead of preserving an earlier answer's destination.
- Every institution-specific reference is accurate and belongs to the intended school.
- The response fits the current word limit without reading like a mechanically shortened copy.
- A response that claims institutional fit could not be sent unchanged to another school.

## Notes
Reuse saves time when prompts genuinely overlap, but the reusable unit is often the life material rather than the finished essay. The same experience can support different interpretations, and each application must make the interpretation relevant to its own question. Institution-specific fit is excluded from generic reuse because an honest answer depends on distinct evidence and reasons, not a replaceable school name.
