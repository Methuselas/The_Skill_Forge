# Balance Discussion Voices by Stretching Both Extremes

## Pattern Rule
**IF** discussion learning is narrowed because some participants habitually remain silent while others occupy most of the available space
**THEN** give the quiet participants a small explicit contribution obligation and give frequent speakers an explicit room-making obligation
**ELSE** preserve organic participation while continuing to watch whose evidence and questions are absent.

## Do
- Define a minimum contribution small enough to make entry possible, such as one relevant observation or question.
- Ask frequent speakers to pause, invite others, and build on contributions that might otherwise be overlooked.
- Route substantive exchanges among participants instead of making every comment travel through the facilitator.
- Judge balance by whether the group gains access to more perspectives, not by mechanical equality of sentence counts.
- Provide another participation route when public speech would create an access or safety barrier.

## Don't
- Do not shame quiet participants or treat temperament as disengagement.
- Do not silence confident contributors after they make room; their attentive responses remain part of peer learning.
- Do not call on someone merely to display compliance when the discussion gives them no meaningful entry point.
- Do not let the facilitator's voice replace the peer perspectives the adjustment is meant to surface.

## Checklist
- Has each participant been given a viable way to enter the discussion?
- Do frequent speakers know how to make and protect room for others?
- Are participants responding to one another rather than only to the facilitator?
- Is the group hearing perspectives that its previous pattern excluded?
- Is there an alternative route for learners who cannot safely participate aloud?

## Notes
Starkey assigns complementary stretches: quiet students should contribute at least once, and confident speakers should make room and sometimes solicit a quieter classmate's view. The goal is not punitive airtime control. It is access to the learning that comes from other participants' readings and judgments. `VAR_starkey_balance_writing_discussion_participation` preserves the creative-writing discussion context and its link between participation and developing an aesthetic.
