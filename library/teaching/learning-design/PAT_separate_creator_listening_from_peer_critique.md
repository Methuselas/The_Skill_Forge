# Separate Creator Listening From Peer Critique

## Pattern Rule
**IF** live critique is being displaced by the creator's explanations or immediate self-defense
**THEN** give peers an uninterrupted artifact-centered response interval, keep the creator listening, and delay answers until the evidence has been heard
**ELSE** allow ordinary dialogue while preserving enough space for independent reader observations.

## Do
- State the listening interval and response order before critique begins.
- Keep comments attached to concrete parts of the artifact and to the creator's stated goal.
- Let peers complete both converging and conflicting observations before asking the creator to interpret them.
- Return written or marked evidence after the discussion so the creator can revisit it outside the pressure of the room.
- End the interval with time to ask clarifying questions and name revision decisions without litigating every comment.

## Don't
- Do not let the creator's intended meaning overwrite what peers actually encountered.
- Do not turn silence into humiliation, passivity, or permission for unbounded personal judgment.
- Do not require the creator to accept every comment merely because they could not answer immediately.
- Do not end the session without a later route for clarification and use.

## Checklist
- Did peers form and state observations before the creator explained the work?
- Was critique anchored to the artifact rather than the person?
- Could disagreement remain visible long enough to be compared?
- Did the creator leave with durable notes or marked evidence?
- Was there a bounded opportunity to clarify and choose what to revise afterward?

## Notes
Starkey's workshop rule interrupts a recognizable failure: discussion becomes an extended defense of the manuscript, so reader evidence never accumulates. `VAR_starkey_run_a_silent_author_workshop_with_marked_manuscripts` retains the exact writing protocol of author silence during discussion and returned marked copies afterward. The portable foundation separates listening from answering without making silence the creator's permanent role or treating every peer response as correct.
