# Construct an Animal Head From Skull Type to Living Features

## Objective
Construct an animal head from species-appropriate skull architecture through living soft tissue and selected features, preserving viewpoint, structural type, and functional coherence while increasing information only when the current drawing stage permits it.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Enter from a trustworthy head placement and intended level of resolution.** Establish the head axis, viewpoint, connection to the neck/body when present, and the species/type evidence that is actually known. If the whole-animal pose is not structurally settled, return to `AP_construct_animal_pose_from_action_type_and_structure` before resolving the head in isolation.
2. **Build the generic skull architecture at Stage 2.** Apply `PAT_construct_animal_skull_from_cranial_facial_masses_and_load_paths` at this decision. Separate cranial and facial masses, major cavities, jaw architecture, and load-bearing continuities before surface features. Preserve the observed or canonical animal type rather than averaging toward a familiar mammal.
3. **Use spatial skull construction only when it solves a real rotation problem.** When planes, cross-sections, or a difficult turn are not staying coherent, apply `PAT_construct_animal_skull_from_type_block_through_planes_and_cross_sections`. Otherwise keep the lighter skull construction.
4. **Branch into the applicable head specialization without making it universal.** When a projecting mammalian muzzle requires a serial cranial-base, nasal-bridge, and muzzle solution, apply `PAT_construct_animal_head_from_cranial_base_nasal_bridge_and_muzzle`. When family, genus, species, breed, age, or other accepted head specializations exist, follow only the branch supported by the subject. Do not load unrelated taxonomic specializations merely because they are reachable.
5. **Pass the Stage-2 head gate.** The skull type, head turn, major proportions, jaw/muzzle architecture, and neck/body attachment must survive reduction to simple masses. Do not resolve finished eyes, ears, lips, nostrils, coat, wrinkles, or expression to compensate for weak structure.
6. **Convert structure into living head mass at Stage 3.** Apply `PAT_model_animal_head_soft_tissue_by_plastic_significance_and_aperture_function`. Add the soft forms that materially alter the skull, keeping chewing masses, aperture controls, and species-specific flesh subordinate to the accepted cranial/facial architecture.
7. **Resolve only the facial systems that the drawing actually needs.** Apply `PAT_construct_animal_eye_as_sphere_with_mass_bearing_lids` when the eye must survive turn or foreshortening; `PAT_construct_animal_ear_as_rooted_flexible_cartilage_with_turning_rims` when ear root, shell, or rotation matters; and `PAT_construct_animal_nose_mouth_as_coupled_soft_tissue_system` when the muzzle's soft nose-mouth relationship needs explicit construction. Feature Patterns are conditional decisions, not a checklist requiring equal detail everywhere.
8. **Preserve type through expression and local specificity.** Any expression, behavioral cue, or local feature adjustment must remain attached to the same skull, jaw, soft-tissue, and aperture system. If a feature changes the head into a different animal type, return to the structural owner instead of polishing the mismatch.
9. **Finish only after the living head is structurally secure.** At Stage 4, when the head is actually being brought to a finished drawing statement, apply `PAT_integrate_animal_head_structure_with_experiential_emphasis`. Use selected marks, texture, tone, or omission to communicate the intended living impression without redesigning the accepted head.
10. **Stop when the requested head decision is complete.** A construction study may correctly stop at Stage 2 or Stage 3. Do not add finish, taxonomic specificity, or rendering merely because later knowledge exists.

## Notes
Animal-head knowledge is deliberately progressive and branchable. The AP owns the general sequence; taxonomic family/genus/species knowledge remains specialization beneath applicable structural owners so it stays reachable without becoming default context. A creature or fantastic-animal workflow may recruit this AP for a donor-derived or canonical head subsystem, but unfamiliar topology has authority over any animal analogy.
