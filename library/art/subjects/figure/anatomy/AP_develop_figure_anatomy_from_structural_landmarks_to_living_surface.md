# Develop Figure Anatomy From Structural Landmarks to Living Surface

## Objective
Develop human or humanoid figure anatomy from trustworthy parent masses into specific living form while preserving action, proportion, attachment, body type, viewpoint, and the current drawing-stage ceiling. Use anatomy to explain the figure already constructed; do not use anatomy to redesign or rescue unresolved construction.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Enter from a structurally trustworthy figure or bounded anatomy study.** Establish the current stage, viewpoint, body type, action, and the parent rib-cage, pelvic, limb, and joint masses that the anatomy must inhabit. If those masses, attachments, or support relationships are still uncertain, return to the appropriate Figure Construction owner before developing surface anatomy.
2. **At Stage 2, add only structural anatomical information that materially affects mass or later placement.** Keep the figure at block resolution. Use bony landmarks, broad tissue masses, and local anatomical specializations only where they clarify the existing rib cage, pelvis, limb, or silhouette. Do not turn the block into an ecorche, label map, or finished body surface. Local Stage-2 anatomy Patterns beneath the shared surface-anatomy foundation are optional branches whose IF conditions must actually match the present body and view.
3. **Pass the Stage-2 anatomy gate.** Remove local detail mentally and confirm that the same action, body type, major masses, attachments, and perspective still read. If anatomy is doing the work that the mass block should have done, roll back to construction.
4. **At Stage 3, design the living surface over the accepted forms.** Apply `PAT_design_surface_anatomy_as_microgesture_on_parent_forms`. Select visible skeletal, muscular, fat, skin, compression, stretch, and other tissue evidence for its effect on the living surface rather than displaying anatomy as an inventory. The parent form remains the authority underneath every local bulge, crease, ridge, or hollow.
5. **Keep anatomy attached to the camera-facing form.** When remembered anatomy begins to flatten, mirror, or drift on a turned or foreshortened body, apply `PAT_project_form_curves_from_camera_view`. Reproject anatomical routes around the actual volume and viewpoint instead of pasting a front-view map onto the figure.
6. **Carry form through joints instead of assembling body parts.** Apply `PAT_carry_form_flow_through_joint_transitions` when anatomy around a shoulder, hip, knee, elbow, neck, wrist, ankle, or other junction becomes segmented or mechanically disconnected. Let the transition redirect one continuous articulated system while preserving the accepted joint location and range.
7. **Pass the limb-mechanics gate before surface finish can disguise an inversion.** Invoke `AP_audit_articulated_limb_identity_and_joint_mechanics` at the current permitted resolution. For ordinary human limb joints, the returned audit must preserve correct root-to-endpoint identity and mechanically coherent elbow/knee/wrist/ankle relationships from anatomical surfaces rather than screen direction. If the audit requires reversing a hinge, exchanging an endpoint, or rerouting a limb chain, roll back to Figure Construction instead of painting anatomy over the error.
8. **Follow only the local specializations the present figure needs.** Chest, rear pelvis, back, arm, knee, fat-distribution, age, sex/body-type, and other anatomy specializations may activate beneath the shared anatomy foundation when their IF conditions match. Do not load every regional or demographic specialization into every figure. Observed or canonical subject evidence overrides any average-body heuristic.
9. **Delegate anatomy that has its own workflow.** When the head requires cranial/facial, likeness, feature, age, or expression development, use `AP_construct_figure_head_from_cranial_structure_to_living_character`. When a hand requires functional palm/digit/contact construction, use `AP_construct_hand_from_function_contact_and_articulated_form`. Return those bounded results to the same figure without changing the accepted action or parent masses.
10. **Pass the living-form gate.** Reduce the figure or suppress surface lines. The anatomy should strengthen rather than replace the underlying gesture, mass, joint, and proportion read; body type should remain specific; and no local anatomical correction should require silently relocating an accepted structural decision.
11. **Stop at the requested drawing resolution.** A Stage-2 anatomy block may correctly stop with landmarks and broad tissue masses; Stage 3 may resolve the living surface. Return finished-pencil integration to the Drawing Stage-4 owner and leave Lighting, Rendering, Color, Paint, Ink, and other downstream specialties to their own workflows.

## Notes
Figure Anatomy is its own Art specialty but shares foundations with Figure Construction, Heads, Hands, Observation, Form Construction, Creature/Character work, and later rendering-oriented workflows. Shared knowledge does not make those categories identical: this AP owns the decision to turn accepted human/humanoid structure into anatomically coherent living form.

Regional anatomy Patterns are intentionally progressive and conditional. The AP claims the reusable anatomy foundations; local body-region specializations remain reachable beneath those foundations so the artist can use the right anatomy at the right stage without loading unrelated body knowledge as default context.
