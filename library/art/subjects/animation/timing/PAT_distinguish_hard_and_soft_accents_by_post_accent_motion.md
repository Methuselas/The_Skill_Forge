# Distinguish Hard And Soft Accents By Post Accent Motion

## Pattern Rule
**IF** two actions hit an accent but need different sensations of hardness or softness after the emphasized instant
**THEN** Differentiate hard and soft accents by what the motion does immediately after the emphasized instant

## Do
- For a hard accent, arrive fast and recoil or bounce away.
- For a soft accent, arrive fast but continue or cushion through the accented state.
- Tune the post-accent spacing to preserve the intended feel.

## Don't
- Do not classify accents only by the size of the pose.

## Checklist
- The aftermath clearly makes the accent feel hard or soft.
