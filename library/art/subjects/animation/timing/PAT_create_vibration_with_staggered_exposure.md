# Create Vibration With Staggered Exposure

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a small repeated motion must feel like vibration or shake rather than a clean periodic oscillation
**THEN** Create shake or vibration by exposing a small motion range in a deliberately non-linear back-and-forth order

## Do
- Build a compact set of positions.
- Play them in jumps, reversals, repeats, and uneven holds.
- Adjust skip size and hold duration to control violence and irregularity.

## Don't
- Do not simply loop the positions in smooth numerical order when vibration is the goal.

## Checklist
- The motion feels like tremble, shiver, flutter, or mechanical shake rather than a clean cycle.
