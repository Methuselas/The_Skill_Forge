# Interleave Offset Motion Series For Organic Wobble

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a wobble or irregular oscillation looks too periodic when driven by one clean repeating series
**THEN** Interleave two similar but slightly offset motion series so their interference creates irregular organic wobble

## Do
- Construct two orderly versions of the same small motion with a slight displacement or phase difference.
- Alternate or interleave them.
- Adjust the relationship until the resulting wobble feels organic rather than periodic.

## Don't
- Do not add random noise that destroys the underlying action.

## Checklist
- The combined motion is irregular but still causally related to the base movement.
