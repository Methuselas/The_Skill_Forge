# Use Micro Anticipation To Add Snap Without Telegraphing

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a sudden action needs more snap but a readable full anticipation would reveal it too early
**THEN** Use a very brief reverse or preparatory move before a sudden action to add mechanical snap without giving the audience enough time to consciously predict it

## Do
- Keep the preparation extremely short.
- Move slightly against the coming action or compress the driving mass.
- Test that it improves snap without turning into an obvious wind-up.

## Don't
- Do not use micro-anticipation when surprise depends on literally no preparatory motion.

## Checklist
- The action feels sharper while remaining surprising.

## Notes

The visibility threshold depends on shot scale, contrast, and playback speed, so use the smallest preparation that improves the action. If it reads as a separate idea, creates a position pop, or delays the main event, reduce or remove it rather than hiding it with more frames.
