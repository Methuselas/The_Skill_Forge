# Balance Primary Motion With Counteraction

## Pattern Rule
**IF** one major body action dominates so completely that the figure loses balance, opposition, or coordinated response
**THEN** Use meaningful opposite motion in another mass to balance, clarify, or energize a strong primary action

## Do
- Identify the dominant directional action.
- Choose a body mass or secondary part whose opposite movement improves balance or vitality.
- Keep the counteraction subordinate enough that the primary action still reads.

## Don't
- Do not mirror every movement with an equal opposite.

## Checklist
- Counteraction supports balance and energy without cancelling the main action.
