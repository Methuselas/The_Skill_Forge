# Stage Contact Before Deformation To Strengthen Impact

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an impact feels mushy because squash or compression obscures the instant of contact
**THEN** Show a readable initial contact before major squash or compression when separating contact from deformation makes impact feel more causal and forceful

## Do
- Place the contact state where the object first meets the surface.
- Follow with compression or deformation as the consequence.
- Keep enough structure at contact for the collision to read.

## Don't
- Do not deform so early that the viewer cannot tell when contact occurred.

## Checklist
- The compression reads as caused by impact.

## Notes

The readable contact may be extremely brief at high speed, so judge it in playback rather than by imposing a fixed frame count. If removing that state makes compression appear to begin in empty space, restore clearer collision evidence before increasing the squash.
