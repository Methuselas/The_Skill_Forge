# Phase Offset Body Parts To Break Mechanical Locomotion

## Pattern Rule
**IF** head, torso, pelvis, limbs, or extremities are peaking and reversing together, making locomotion feel mechanical
**THEN** Offset reversals and peaks of head, torso, pelvis, arms, hands, and feet so the body contains related rhythms instead of one synchronized pendulum

## Do
- Choose the primary locomotion beat.
- Advance or delay selected secondary masses by meaningful phases.
- Keep the offsets causally related to the main action.

## Don't
- Do not make every part reverse on the same frame by default.

## Checklist
- The body feels coordinated but not mechanical.

## Notes
- The same phase-offset logic applies to animals and non-locomotion acting: pelvis, chest, head, arms, clothing, tail, and other masses can peak on different but related schedules to keep the body organic.
