# Encode Locomotion Weight Through Vertical Mass Shift And Support

## Pattern Rule
**IF** locomotion feels weightless because the body mass does not visibly settle onto and rise from support
**THEN** Make locomotion feel weighted by showing the body mass drop, recover, and reorganize over the current support rather than merely moving limbs through a cycle

## Do
- Track the supporting leg.
- Coordinate body high/low phases with support and push-off.
- Use joint bend and recovery to show load acceptance.

## Don't
- Do not make a heavy walk only slower; show the mass responding to support.

## Checklist
- Vertical mass travel and support phase agree.
