# Propagate Wave Or Whip Action Through Articulated Chain

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a flexible chain must carry a directional change from one segment into the next rather than bend everywhere at once
**THEN** Drive a directional change into one end of a flexible chain and propagate that change successively through its segments, using a sharp terminal snap when a whip is intended

## Do
- Define the driving end and initial directional change.
- Pass curvature or reversal through adjacent segments with delay.
- Cushion a wave through the end, or compress the final change into a sharp whip accent.
- Let the chain relax after the propagation.

## Don't
- Do not reverse every segment simultaneously.

## Checklist
- The wave visibly travels through the chain.
- Whip action terminates in a distinct snap rather than a uniform bend.

## Notes
- An animal tail is a clear biological application: the tail root follows hindquarter drive while the remaining chain continues the delayed propagated motion.
