# Preserve Support Logic While Distorting Locomotion Design

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a stylized locomotion pose is being exaggerated far enough that contact and weight-transfer logic could become unclear
**THEN** Preserve readable support and weight transfer while aggressively bending, reversing, stretching, or inventing locomotion poses for character

## Do
- Lock the support relationship first.
- Distort joints, passing shapes, torso, or foot behavior only after the weight logic works.
- Retest the cycle after each exaggeration.

## Don't
- Do not let a stylized pose lose the load-bearing relationship that makes the gait believable.

## Checklist
- The invented gait can be impossible in detail yet convincing as locomotion.
