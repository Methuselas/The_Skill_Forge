# Show Change Of Thought At A Readable Moment Before Or After Broad Motion

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a change of thought or expression occurs near broad movement that could hide the facial acting
**THEN** Stage a change of expression or thought where the audience can actually read it, either just before broad motion or immediately after the face becomes visible again

## Do
- Identify the thought change.
- Place it before the large move when thought should cause action.
- Place it after the move when the broad action would otherwise hide the facial change.

## Don't
- Do not bury the only readable thought change inside fast full-body motion.

## Checklist
- The audience can see when the character’s internal state changes.

## Notes

Readability may come from a brief hold, a clear silhouette, gaze, or a whole-pose change rather than facial detail alone. Test at final shot scale and speed; if the thought vanishes, move or lengthen the acting beat before adding more expression detail.
