# Use Eye Direction And Blinks To Externalize Thought

## Pattern Rule
**IF** the acting depends on a subtle change of attention or thought that should read without large body motion
**THEN** Use gaze shifts, eyelid shape, pupil placement, and blinks to show changing attention and thought with minimal body motion

## Do
- Track what the character is attending to internally or externally.
- Move the eyes before larger head/body action when attention leads.
- Use blinks as phrasing or thought punctuation rather than automatic noise.

## Don't
- Do not move the eyes randomly just to keep the face alive.

## Checklist
- Eye behavior makes the character’s changing attention legible.
