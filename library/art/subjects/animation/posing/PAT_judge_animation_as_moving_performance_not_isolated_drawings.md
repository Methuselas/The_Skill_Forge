# Judge Animation As Moving Performance Not Isolated Drawings

## Pattern Rule
**IF** individual drawings look acceptable but the action must be evaluated for behavior, continuity, and performance in motion
**THEN** Evaluate animation primarily as behavior unfolding through time, using individual drawings only in service of the moving performance

## Do
- Play or mentally traverse the sequence before judging a frame.
- Repair weight, intention, continuity, or rhythm even when individual drawings look attractive.
- Accept simplified or distorted frames when they make the action read better in motion.

## Don't
- Do not optimize every frame as an independent illustration.

## Checklist
- The action communicates when viewed as a sequence.
- Frame-level beauty does not override motion clarity.

## Notes
- Individually strange, broken, smeared, or highly distorted intermediate drawings can be valid when their temporal function improves the moving performance. Judge them by what they do between the important poses.
- Weight and effort are often revealed most strongly in preparation, support, resistance, braking, and recovery rather than in a single “heavy” pose.
