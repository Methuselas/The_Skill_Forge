# Design Breakdowns As Authored Motion Decisions

## Pattern Rule
**IF** two important poses are fixed but the path, orientation, overlap, or character of the transition is still undefined
**THEN** Design breakdown or passing poses to define path, arc, spacing bias, attitude, and transition character instead of placing a neutral geometric midpoint

## Do
- Decide what the transition should feel like.
- Choose a passing pose that creates that path and spacing.
- Move the breakdown nearer an endpoint when acceleration or cushioning requires it.

## Don't
- Do not let interpolation accidentally choose the transition.

## Checklist
- The breakdown explains how the action travels, not only where halfway is.
