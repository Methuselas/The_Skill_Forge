# Lead Dialogue Accent With Body Or Head Action

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a spoken accent needs physical emphasis without making gesture land mechanically on the same frame as the word
**THEN** Let head, shoulders, or body prepare an emphasized spoken beat before the mouth lands on the vocal accent

## Do
- Identify the important word or vocal modulation.
- Place the physical intention slightly before the mouth accent when useful.
- Keep the mouth close enough to the sound to preserve speech sync.

## Don't
- Do not make head, body, blink, and mouth all accent on the same frame by default.

## Checklist
- The physical intention appears to cause or support the vocal accent.
