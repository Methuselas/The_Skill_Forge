# Offset Gesture From Spoken Word To Strengthen Acting Point

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a gesture and its spoken idea feel mechanically synchronized or visually redundant
**THEN** Separate a meaningful gesture from the matching spoken word so each event can carry force instead of landing simultaneously by habit

## Do
- Choose whether the gesture should lead or follow the word.
- Give each event a readable beat.
- Keep the offset motivated by the character’s thought and phrasing.

## Don't
- Do not illustrate every noun or deictic word with a simultaneous gesture.

## Checklist
- Gesture and speech reinforce the acting point without feeling mechanically synchronized.
