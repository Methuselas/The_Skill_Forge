# Choose Panel Shape To Serve Narrative Function

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a story beat needs a panel but its spatial or temporal emphasis is not yet encoded by the frame
**THEN** Choose panel shape and proportion according to the spatial or temporal job of the beat rather than as decoration

## Do
- Use tall formats for vertical travel, height, or separation when useful.
- Use panoramic formats for breadth, distance, or lateral movement.
- Use irregular shapes only when their tension or emphasis helps the beat.

## Don't
- Do not choose unusual panel shapes only for graphic novelty.

## Checklist
- The panel format reinforces the action, space, or emphasis it contains.
