# Fragment Scene To Control Sequential Pacing

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a sequential scene reads too quickly, too slowly, or with the wrong emphasis for its beats
**THEN** Control perceived time by deciding how much action or observation each panel contains and how panel size/shape changes through the sequence

## Do
- Use repeated stable panels to slow observation.
- Increase fragmentation or narrow panels to accelerate reading and tension.
- Use a sudden large or changed panel to release buildup or reveal the key event.

## Don't
- Do not fragment uniformly when no pacing change is intended.

## Checklist
- Panel segmentation produces the intended slow, fast, tense, or released rhythm.
