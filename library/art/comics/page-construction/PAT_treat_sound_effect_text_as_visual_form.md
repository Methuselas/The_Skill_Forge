# Treat Sound Effect Text As Visual Form

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a sound effect must communicate intensity, rhythm, or depth inside the picture rather than sit as a detached label
**THEN** Use sound-effect lettering as compositional form whose scale, placement, perspective, repetition, and overlap can express intensity, rhythm, and depth

## Do
- Scale and orient lettering according to the sound’s role.
- Let repeated sound forms build rhythm when appropriate.
- Allow a dominant sound to become a major graphic mass when the event overwhelms the scene.

## Don't
- Do not treat sound text as neutral captioning when its visual behavior matters.

## Checklist
- The lettering contributes to the same depth, rhythm, and hierarchy as the image.
