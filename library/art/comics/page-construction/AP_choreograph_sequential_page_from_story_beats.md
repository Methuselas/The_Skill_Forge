# Choreograph Sequential Page From Story Beats

## Objective
Build a sequential page as one storytelling unit by assigning story beats to panel count, format, rhythm, reading path, focal handoffs, and text space before finishing individual panels.

## Steps / Flow
1. List the narrative beats and rank their relative importance.
2. Assign panel count and approximate size/shape according to pacing and emphasis.
3. Establish an unmistakable reading path across the page.
4. Coordinate focal positions, directional flow, and visual weight from panel to panel.
5. Reserve dialogue and sound-effect space during rough layout.
6. Vary or break the page rhythm only when the story beat benefits.
7. Test the page without explanatory labels; repair navigation before detail.

**Completion check**
- The page reads in the intended order without decoding effort.
- Important beats receive proportionate space and emphasis.
- Text and image were composed together rather than competing after the fact.

## Notes

Judge the page first at thumbnail size with dialogue and sound represented as plain masses. If the route works only after reading the words or inspecting finished detail, the page-level choreography is not yet carrying the sequence.
