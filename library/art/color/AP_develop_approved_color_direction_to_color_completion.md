# Develop an Approved Color Direction to Color Completion

## Objective
Develop the exact approved Broad Color Direction into a coherent completed Color image by increasing causal local Color, form-linked variation, chroma control, material response, atmospheric depth, edges, and focal hierarchy while preserving both the inherited Drawing lockset and the ratified global Color direction, then stop before automatically entering another medium.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Pass the exact approved-Color predecessor gate.** Begin from the actual approved Broad Color Direction artifact. Register it through `PAT_develop_scene_through_registered_successors`. Do not restart from the original Drawing, reconstruct the palette from prose, or substitute a visually similar Color rough. When native edit/reference continuity is required, separately confirm that this exact approved Color artifact is accessible; otherwise fail closed and recover/request it.
2. **Carry both locksets forward.** Preserve the inherited Drawing lockset—camera, crop, composition, pose, perspective, placement, proportions, major design/form, scene inventory, important overlaps/contacts—and the approved Color-direction lockset—major light/value organization, global Color strategy, broad palette/gamut, dominant Color families, broad warm/cool relationships, major Color masses, and broad chroma hierarchy. Development may increase resolution inside these commitments but may not silently replace them.
3. **Reconfirm the large read before local work.** Use `PAT_preserve_value_structure_when_translating_tone_into_color` as a continuing invariant. If the approved broad Color direction no longer reads at reduced size after development begins, diagnose whether the defect is local or whether the ratified broad direction itself must be reopened.
4. **Resolve visible Color causally.** Apply `PAT_resolve_visible_color_from_local_color_light_and_reflection`. Develop local-color identity through the accepted illumination, form turning, reflected surroundings, and environmental influence so object Color belongs to the scene rather than appearing independently assigned.
5. **Refine chroma where light and form support it.** Coordinate `PAT_position_peak_chroma_across_light_halftone_and_shadow`. Preserve the approved broad chroma hierarchy while adding local variation; do not flatten every surface to equal saturation or move the global peak merely because a local passage can be made more vivid.
6. **Develop material appearance from optical response.** Use `PAT_render_material_from_optical_response` where material differentiation matters. Rendering owns the causal optics; this Color operation integrates their visible Color consequences without redesigning the inherited picture or turning material detail into a new global Color strategy.
7. **Grade depth and atmosphere within the approved Color world.** Use `PAT_grade_depth_with_atmospheric_effect` when the scene requires atmospheric separation. Atmosphere may shift visible value, chroma, contrast, and hue with depth, but it must remain consistent with the ratified Color/light environment rather than becoming a late competing palette.
8. **Integrate edge and focal hierarchy.** Apply `PAT_control_edge_hardness_from_form_light_and_focus` and accepted focal owners as appropriate. Coordinate sharpness, contrast, chroma, detail, and accents so the intended focus strengthens while subordinate passages remain subordinate.
9. **Diagnose before broad correction.** If the image becomes muddy, dead, disconnected, over-greyed, over-saturated, or hierarchically weak, route to `AP_diagnose_and_repair_dead_color_relationships`. Repair the smallest Color cause that explains the symptom. Rendering knowledge may diagnose illumination/material/atmosphere behavior without becoming a separate visible production stage.
10. **Rollback by productive ownership.** If diagnosis shows that the approved palette, global Color strategy, major warm/cool organization, dominant Color families, major Color/value relationship, or broad chroma hierarchy must be replaced, return to `AP_establish_broad_color_direction_from_authoritative_drawing`. If camera, composition, pose, perspective, major silhouette/design, placement, or scene inventory must change, return to Drawing. Keep local Color/material/atmosphere/chroma/edge/focal repairs inside this AP when the ratified global direction remains valid.
11. **Pass the Color-completion gate.** Complete when the exact inherited Drawing remains recoverable; the approved broad Color direction is still recognizable; the large Color/value read works; palette and illumination agree; visible Color behaves causally; important material and atmospheric differences read where relevant; chroma and edge hierarchy support focus; and remaining work is optional polish or belongs to another medium-specific workflow.
12. **Stop at Color completion.** Surface the completed Color artifact for approval or same-operation revision. Do not automatically start Painting, Ink, Manga/B&W, watercolor, or another downstream workflow. If the user later requests another medium, that medium must pass its own legal entry contract and inherit every still-authoritative Drawing/Color commitment.

### Productive Image Contract
- **Artifact form:** one developed coherent Color image whose productive source is the exact approved Broad Color Direction artifact.
- **Preserve:** the complete Drawing lockset plus the approved global Color strategy, major light/value organization, broad palette/gamut, dominant Color families, broad warm/cool structure, major Color masses, and broad chroma hierarchy.
- **May develop:** causal local/visible Color, form-linked Color variation, reflected/environmental influence, chroma nuance, material appearance, atmospheric depth, edge hierarchy, focal integration, and Color-specific correction.
- **Rendering causality:** use illumination, reflection, transmission, material optics, atmosphere, and edge behavior as explanatory knowledge; do not promote Rendering into an extra visible approval stage merely because it owns those causes.
- **Rollback:** global Color-direction replacement returns to Broad Color Direction; structural picture replacement returns to Drawing; local Color-development repair stays here.
- **Medium boundary:** do not claim physical Paint handling, Ink execution, Manga/B&W treatment, watercolor process, or another medium-specific finish unless the user explicitly enters that owning workflow.
- **Stop:** when Color relationships and causal appearance are coherent enough that further work is optional polish or another medium's responsibility.
