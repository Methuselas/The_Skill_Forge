# Control Brush-Ink Tone With Moisture and Pressure

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a brush-and-ink passage needs a controlled soft tone, broken texture, or darker mass without changing medium
**THEN** adjust how much ink remains in the brush and how firmly the brush meets the paper, testing excess load off the brush before committing the mark
**ELSE** use the value controls native to the chosen medium rather than imitating dry-brush behavior mechanically

## Do
- For a drier broken mark, work excess ink out on scratch paper first instead of taking the fully loaded brush straight to the drawing.
- Let the tip flatten enough that only the thinner outer hairs remain lightly wet when a soft, interrupted tone is wanted.
- Treat brush moisture and contact pressure as separate controls: a drier brush can still make a stronger mark under more pressure, while a wetter brush can flood a passage even with a light touch.
- Test a few strokes before the final passage so the brush arrives on the drawing at the intended load instead of forcing the correction after the mark is down.

## Don't
- Expect one fixed brush load to produce every value and texture simply by changing stroke direction.
- Use a saturated brush when the goal is the broken paper-catching quality of dry brush.
- Scrub randomly to manufacture texture; the mark should still follow the intended form or coat direction.

## Checklist
- The same ink can produce a visibly different value range through controlled load and pressure.
- Dry passages leave intentional paper breaks instead of accidental gaps caused by a failing brush.
- A tested stroke on scratch paper closely predicts the mark that appears on the drawing.
- Texture variation does not destroy the large light-and-form statement underneath it.

## Notes
Hultgren separates brush handling from the animal subject itself: he demonstrates that the brush can be prepared before the stroke, then tuned through remaining wetness and paper pressure. His swatches show this as a controllable rendering variable rather than a one-off texture trick. The medium-specific procedure belongs late in the drawing process, after the larger form and value organization already read.
