# Build Visual Humor from Expectation, Twist, Predicament, and Behavior

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a visual idea is intended to be funny rather than merely exaggerated or odd
**THEN** establish a recognizable expectation, role, status, rule, or causal setup and make a specific reversal, contradiction, predicament, or surprise visible through character behavior
**ELSE** do not force a comedy structure onto material whose intended effect is different.

## Do
- Give the viewer enough setup to understand what normally should happen before presenting the turn.
- Search for reversals of outcome, status, convention, seriousness, competence, or expected cause and effect when they fit the subject.
- Make the twist visible through action, pose, expression, timing, props, or relationship between characters.
- Use characterization and observed behavior to make the situation specific rather than relying on distortion alone.
- Keep the idea legible enough that the viewer can reconstruct both the expectation and the thing that violated it.

## Don't
- Do not assume caricature, ugliness, or extreme anatomy is funny by itself.
- Do not treat humiliation, injury, or suffering as automatically comic; the intended audience and framing still determine the effect.
- Do not bury the conceptual turn under excessive detail or explanation.
- Do not reduce humor to a fixed inventory of supposedly universal joke types.

## Checklist
- A clear expectation or normal relationship is established.
- The comic turn changes that expectation in a specific way.
- Character behavior helps carry the joke rather than merely decorating it.
- The idea reads without requiring a caption to explain the basic contradiction.
- The humor depends on the situation, not only on exaggerated drawing style.

## Notes
Loomis repeatedly locates comic ideas in reversals, the unexpected, predicaments, status changes, and other twists of an established situation, while warning that exaggerated drawing alone does not create humor. The durable craft lesson is structural: build the setup, make the turn visible, and let believable behavior carry the contradiction.
