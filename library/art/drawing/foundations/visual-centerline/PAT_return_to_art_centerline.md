# Return to the Art Centerline

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a task begins or resumes the creation, development, correction, or finishing of visual art
**THEN** recover the latest approved image and its textual freeze record, preserve every commitment already owned by an earlier stage, and build the next pass as a registered layer that proves only its assigned class of information
**ELSE** when no approved visual artifact is available, establish Stage 0 before claiming staged continuity

## Do
- Begin with the reflex: the user wants art, so recover the controlling image, identify the current approved stage, and load the freeze record before generating.
- Freeze a property at the first stage where disagreement about it becomes decidable; do not confuse caring about a property with being able to judge it yet.
- At Stage 0, ratify only camera or viewpoint, framing, and story intent. Gesture, silhouette, placement, and value grouping may guide the thumbnail while remaining unratified.
- At Stage 1, make anatomical inventory, attachment points, skeletal pose, joint configuration, line of action, and relative skeletal proportion explicit enough to count and compare.
- At Stage 2, wrap the accepted skeleton in primitive volumes, then ratify silhouette, mass distribution, overlap, and depth order.
- At Stage 3, develop and ratify the specific surface form, anatomical detail, designed structure, and edge quality. Stage 4 adds no new freeze; it must satisfy all earlier commitments while releasing presentation.
- Use the exact preceding artifact as the active underdrawing, edit target, or registration source, and carry the short text record beside it. The image carries geometry; the text carries intent and commitments.
- Ask two questions at every transition: does the new pass prove its assigned problem, and did it preserve every property already frozen beneath it?
- When drift appears, return to the stage that owns the violated property, correct it there, re-ratify, and propagate the repair forward.
- Channel the model's learned drive to recognize, resolve, beautify, and complete by assigning it the correct job at the correct stage rather than trying to suppress it.

## Don't
- Generate each stage as a separate interpretation of the verbal prompt and call similarity an overlay.
- Freeze inventory, pose, proportion, silhouette, depth order, or surface detail in the thumbnail before the artifact can make the disagreement decidable.
- Let a later pass silently redesign a property already frozen by an earlier stage.
- Use anatomy, facial rendering, texture, dramatic value modeling, or polish to make Stage 1 or Stage 2 look successful.
- Advance because an image is attractive. A stage passes only when its visible evidence proves the question assigned to it.
- Quietly fix a Stage 1 problem in Stage 2, a Stage 2 problem in Stage 3, or a Stage 3 problem in Stage 4. Backward routing protects the audit trail.
- Treat the final render as permission to hide an unresolved structural failure beneath lighting, costume, smoke, fur, armor, or effects.
- Overload the freeze record with preferences that are not load-bearing. Every frozen property becomes a simultaneous constraint on later work.

## Checklist
- The current approved stage, controlling artifact, verified edit target, and textual freeze record are identified before generation.
- Stage 0 freezes camera or viewpoint, framing, and story intent; Stage 1 freezes inventory, pose, joints, action, and proportion; Stage 2 freezes silhouette, masses, overlap, and depth; Stage 3 freezes surface form, detail, and edge quality; Stage 4 freezes nothing new.
- The newest pass preserves all inherited commitments and appends only the properties owned by its current stage.
- Every visible mark has a current-stage job, and information belonging only to a later pass has been removed.
- The output reports a verified parent artifact rather than merely resembling the predecessor.
- Any failure names the violated property and routes to its owning stage before the sequence continues.
- The sequence grows like a crescendo: each stage adds force because the commitments beneath it remain intact.

## Notes
Wu Sao is the working mnemonic for this initiating stance: return to center before extending. In visual production, the active centerline is the pair formed by the latest approved image and its compact freeze record. The process is one image accumulating separated classes of information, not five unrelated images that happen to depict the same subject.

The thumbnail remains an idea. It can suggest a compact body, a rough stance, or a broad silhouette without making those decisions binding. The first definitive construction may improve them legally because Stage 1 is where pose and proportion become countable, and Stage 2 is where silhouette and depth order become mechanically comparable. This keeps the system flexible where evidence is weak and strict where evidence is strong.
