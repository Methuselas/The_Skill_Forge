# Build Creative Tension by Straddling Opposites

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a composition has become one-sided, predictable, or visually inert
**THEN** identify a relevant opposing quality and let both poles contribute meaningfully to the picture rather than averaging them into a bland midpoint
**ELSE** keep the dominant quality clear when the subject genuinely benefits from one-sided simplicity, stillness, clarity, or uniformity

## Do
- Name the dominant visual quality before adding its opposite.
- Choose an opposition that serves the subject: simple/complex, clear/ambiguous, balanced/precarious, active/passive, repeated/varied, hard/soft, or another relevant pair.
- Preserve the strongest useful contribution of both poles rather than compromising each until neither remains legible.
- Rebalance the pair at the whole-picture level after local changes.
- Let one side dominate when appropriate; straddling does not require equal amounts.

## Don't
- Add contradiction for novelty when it has no visual or narrative job.
- Treat the midpoint between two qualities as automatically sophisticated.
- Apply every opposition simultaneously.
- Use ambiguity, imbalance, or complexity as cover for unresolved drawing problems.

## Checklist
- Both opposing qualities are perceptible.
- Their relationship produces useful tension rather than confusion.
- One pole may dominate, but the other contributes enough to matter.
- The opposition supports the subject, mood, or design goal.

## Notes
Dodson's key distinction is that a productive "straddle" is not a compromise. Creative tension often comes from allowing apparently conflicting qualities to remain alive together. Chapter 7 demonstrates the principle through composition; broader metaskill promotion is intentionally deferred until another domain supplies operational evidence.

The existing Repeat With Variation Pattern is already one strong example of this structure: recurrence supplies coherence while variation supplies difference. The four variants here retain Dodson's other major composition applications without multiplying independent Patterns for each pair.

`VAR_dodson_straddle_simplicity_and_complexity` applies the parent decision to information density: preserve a clear whole while adding selected richness, or simplify a chaotic field without flattening its identity.

`VAR_dodson_straddle_clarity_and_ambiguity` uses recognizable evidence and selective withholding together when interpretation and discovery are part of the intended experience.

`VAR_dodson_straddle_balance_and_imbalance` permits deliberately stable or precarious placement when other visual forces counterbalance the choice at the whole-picture level.

`VAR_dodson_straddle_active_and_passive_shape_behavior` contrasts quieter contained regions with more projecting, irregular, detailed, or gestural regions so activity is not distributed uniformly by habit.

`VAR_loomis_balance_unequal_masses_by_visual_weight_and_distance` makes the balance/imbalance straddle easier to diagnose: compare unequal masses as perceptual weights, generally allowing a heavier mass nearer the center and a lighter counterweight farther away while rechecking the whole picture rather than applying a literal lever equation.
