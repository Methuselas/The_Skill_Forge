# Draw a Figure Through Onion-Skinned Stages

## Objective
Carry one intended image from an approved Stage 0 proposition through readable construction, complete mass blocking, serious form realization, and final presentation while preserving each accepted decision and allowing the artist to choose the most useful working method at every stage.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Select and announce Stage 0.** Resolve Stage 0A, 0B, or 0C with `PAT_calibrate_stage_information_density_against_precedent`. State that the artifact is an approval thumbnail or reference rather than the final result.
2. **Generate Stage 0 and inspect its profile.** When composition remains open, any selected profile defaults to one four-candidate contact sheet. Stage 0 establishes camera, crop, composition, story, focal relationship, broad value pattern, and general light direction without authorizing a later stage in the same operation.
3. **Present the Stage 0 gate and stop.** Selection activates a candidate; only explicit `advance` ratifies it. Preserve the approved picture as the root of every later pass.
4. **Stage 1 — make the plan readable.** Apply `PAT_choose_stage1_construction_by_readability`. The artist chooses blocking, scribbling, or a hybrid. Construct the full positional figure where continuity matters, use the generic Torso → Legs → Arms → Head order unless a justified exception applies, include horizon or eye level when later perspective needs it, and spend more construction on principal subjects that carry structural risk.
5. **Inspect, gate, and ratify Stage 1.** Ask whether the artist can build Stage 2 without guessing at inventory, attachments, pose, axes, proportions, contacts, perspective relationships, or important hidden paths. Accept a heavier Stage 1 when its extra lines solve uncertainty rather than render later information.
6. **Register the next pass to both carriers.** Use the exact approved predecessor as the edit source and carry the complete freeze record beside it. The image carries geometry; the record carries ratified conclusions.
7. **Stage 2 — build the complete minimum mass inventory.** Apply `PAT_block_complete_stage2_inventory`. Expand the Stage 1 plan into connected solid forms while preserving anchors, horizon, perspective, overlap, and negative spaces. Every element intended for Stage 3 must already exist here at minimum block level. Use no shading unless a small structural accent is necessary to explain depth or attachment.
8. **Inspect, gate, and ratify Stage 2.** Confirm that Stage 1 can be recovered by reducing the masses and that Stage 3 can begin without inventing, relocating, multiplying, or repurposing a major form. Ratify silhouette, mass distribution, overlap, depth order, and complete block inventory.
9. **Stage 3 — make the image become itself.** Apply `PAT_commit_stage3_form_realization`. Use the exact approved Stage 2 artifact as the controlling parent. Turn generic masses into specific anatomy, architecture, vehicles, machines, props, clothing, effects, and designed forms. Expect many lines. Choose intended detail locations and light direction, and use only the local hatching, sparse shading, or simple arrows needed to explain the working drawing.
10. **Compare while developing Stage 3.** Keep the Stage 0 thumbnail and Stage 2 block visible as control references. Dream most here, but guard camera, crop, action, inventory, major shapes, negative spaces, focal hierarchy, and scene identity. A rich fresh generation of the same verbal idea is not continuity.
11. **Inspect, gate, and ratify Stage 3.** Approve only when the serious drawing works at full size and still reduces to the approved thumbnail proposition. Ratify specific form design, anatomy, clothing, object identity, major contour decisions, and intended detail locations. This is the commitment point; later structural change is a return.
12. **Stage 4 — complete the active drawing medium.** Use the exact approved Stage 3 artifact and complete freeze record. Resolve the major detail, line/value finish, material cues, texture, accents, and presentation required for the intended drawing handoff without redesigning Stage 3. Do not force ink or color when pencil is the requested final medium; if a downstream craft begins, start its own four-stage cycle.
13. **Inspect and present the Stage 4 gate.** Verify parentage and every inherited freeze. Stage 4 adds no new structural freeze and may not rescue an underdeveloped or drifted Stage 3.

## Notes
The stage summary is:

- **Stage 0:** choose the picture.
- **Stage 1:** make the plan readable enough to build.
- **Stage 2:** state every intended form as connected minimum mass.
- **Stage 3:** realize the specific forms as a complete working drawing.
- **Stage 4:** complete the active drawing medium to its intended handoff standard.

Stage boundaries protect information classes, not one compulsory drawing ritual. The artist chooses the method. The skill guards continuity.
