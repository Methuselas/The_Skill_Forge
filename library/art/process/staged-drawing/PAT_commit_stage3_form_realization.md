# Commit Stage 3 Form Realization Against the Thumbnail

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an approved Stage 2 block-in is entering Stage 3
**THEN** use the actual approved Stage 2 image as the immediate visual anchor, keep the approved Stage 0 root as the composition compass, realize every block as its intended specific subject, and keep the pass within Stage 3's working-drawing information ceiling
**ELSE** do not treat a fresh illustration of the same verbal premise as continuity

## Do
- Treat Stage 3 as the principal form-realization stage: generic masses become specific anatomy, architecture, vehicles, machines, props, clothing, effects, and designed forms.
- In visible staged mode, carry `ROOT = approved Stage 0` and `IMMEDIATE = approved Stage 2`, plus the compact prompt carry. Label attempts `S3-r1`, `S3-r2`, and so on.
- Target the actual approved Stage 2 image as explicitly as the host permits. If the host exposes a real edit control, use it. If not, do not pretend one exists; rely on the visible approved image plus prompt carry and perform visual continuity checks.
- Dream most here, but locally. Invent inside the approved masses; do not dream sideways into another camera, another composition, another scale relationship, or another story.
- Compare camera, crop, dominant action, major subject apparent scale/placement, large shapes, negative spaces, focal hierarchy, broad value/light proposition, and scene identity against Stage 0 while preserving Stage 2 mass arrangement.
- Refine silhouettes, interconnections, contours, anatomy, designed structure, mechanisms, and object identity without migrating the approved masses.
- Decide intended detail concentration and quiet zones. Use only the line, sparse local shading/hatching, or simple light cues needed to explain specific form and working light direction. Leave final pencil contour cleanup, line hierarchy, pervasive hatching/texture, edge integration, and tertiary finish for Stage 4; leave color, paint, ink, atmosphere, and other medium-specific finish to downstream APs.
- Enforce inventory continuity. Every major visible Stage 3 subject must map to a Stage 2 predecessor. New castles, vehicles, characters, major props, buildings, large effects, or terrain features require rollback rather than silent insertion.
- Judge the image reduced or blurred as well as close-up. It must still read as the same Stage 0 picture and the same Stage 2 mass solution.

## Don't
- Do not move or repurpose Stage 2 blocks to make a more exciting illustration.
- Do not rescale the primary subject or change its screen position merely to increase drama.
- Do not add major subjects or architecture absent from Stage 2.
- Do not allow rendering quality to excuse continuity failure. **Beautiful but unauthorized = failed Stage 3.**
- Do not let Stage 3 become finished pencils or a downstream finished medium. Stage 3 is specific rough realization, not Stage 4 pencil finish.
- Do not claim parentage is verifiable unless the host actually exposes that information.

## Checklist
- The actual approved Stage 2 image is identifiable in the conversation and is the immediate visual anchor.
- The approved Stage 0 root remains identifiable as the composition compass.
- Every Stage 2 major block has become a specific intended form without migration or replacement.
- No major Stage 3 subject appears without a Stage 2 predecessor.
- Camera, crop, action, major subject scale/placement, overlaps, negative spaces, hierarchy, and scene identity remain consistent with the approved anchors.
- Specific form and selected detail locations are strong enough for Stage 4 without finished-pencil cleanup leaking backward.
- A reduced or blurred comparison still reads as the approved Stage 0 proposition.
- If the picture drifted, the stage is rejected regardless of rendering quality.

## Notes
Stage 3 makes the drawing specific; Stage 4 cleans, resolves, and integrates those decisions as finished pencils.

The dragon regression case defines the adversarial standard: a technically impressive fantasy render with a much larger dragon, changed terrain, or a new castle is not “a strong Stage 3.” It is unauthorized Stage 0 ideation occurring at Stage 3 and must be treated as drift.
