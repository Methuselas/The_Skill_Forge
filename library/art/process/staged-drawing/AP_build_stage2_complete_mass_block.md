# Build the Stage 2 Complete Mass Block

## Objective
Convert the approved Stage 1 scene skeleton into complete minimum three-dimensional mass so every major later element already exists as a stable block, volume, overlap, or structural placeholder without adding premature rendering.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Enter from an approved Stage 1 skeleton.** Preserve the approved Stage 0 picture proposition and the complete Stage 1 scaffold. If the skeleton is missing an important object, contact, perspective relationship, or pose dependency, return to Stage 1 first.
2. **Register the exact approved skeleton as the productive predecessor.** Apply `PAT_develop_scene_through_registered_successors`. Develop each registered figure, weapon, prop, and environment scaffold into mass without independently relocating or reinterpreting it from prose.
3. **Expand anchors into masses.** Apply `PAT_block_complex_objects_with_perspective_boxes` and `PAT_construct_inclined_planes_from_base_vanishing_directions` at this decision. Use `PAT_block_complete_stage2_inventory` to turn figure skeletons into connected body masses and turn object/environment scaffolds into boxes, cylinders, planes, tubes, terrain masses, and other simple volumes.
4. **Account for every major intended element.** A figure, building, prop, vehicle, effect, support, attachment, or terrain feature that will matter during rough realization must already exist here in minimum solid form or as an explicit structural placeholder.
5. **Preserve the earlier picture and skeleton.** Centers, axes, joints, contacts, support, perspective, overlap order, camera, crop, subject scale/placement, negative spaces, and hierarchy remain authoritative. Stage 2 gains volume; it does not redesign placement.
6. **Compile the productive image through the artifact-only handoff.** Use `AP_prepare_artifact_only_image_generation_handoff` and the Productive Image Contract below. The whole image must remain at simple-volume resolution.
7. **Inspect reducibility.** Collapse the masses mentally back to the approved Stage 1 skeleton. If they no longer recover the same scaffold, repair the Stage 2 mass or rollback to Stage 1 according to the owner of the error.
8. **Complete when rough realization requires no new major invention.** Stop when every major form exists with enough volume, overlap, and depth to support Stage 3 without adding or relocating important blocks.
9. **Use the Stage 2 handoff prompt.** End with explicit next actions such as: “Approve this mass block to continue, tell me any mass or proportion revisions you want, or reject it and we'll return to the appropriate earlier stage.” Keep the language focused on volume, proportion, and inventory rather than finish.
### Productive Image Contract
- **Artifact form:** one full-frame volumetric drawing of the approved structure. No title, captions, legends, inset examples, or alternate finish states.
- **Preserve:** all established centers, axes, joints, contacts, perspective, overlap order, camera, crop, subject placement, negative spaces, and major environment arrangement.
- **Show:** connected ellipsoids, capsules, cylinders, boxes, wedges, planes, tubes, terrain masses, cross-contours, draw-through, and simple overlap cues for every major subject and environment element.
- **Identity visibility:** major body volumes, armor/gear masses, holster/equipment masses, and other silhouette-relevant volumes may appear, but not their surface design.
- **Withhold:** finished anatomy, facial features, hair strands, costume seam maps, emblems, small hardware, material description, texture, atmospheric effects, cinematic lighting, color rendering, polished contour, tertiary detail, and decorative surface treatment.
- **Tone:** only minimal neutral structural tone where line alone cannot explain overlap or depth.
- **Global ceiling:** every major intended element exists as a simple solid or explicit placeholder; no region jumps ahead into specific surface realization.
- **Stop:** when the scene can be developed into specific forms without inventing or relocating any major mass.

## Notes
Stage 2 answers **what solid forms occupy the approved scene**. It is the bridge between sparse structure and specific realization, and its restraint prevents lighting or texture from hiding unresolved volume.
