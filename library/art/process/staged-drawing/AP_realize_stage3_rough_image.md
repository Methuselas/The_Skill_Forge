# Realize the Stage 3 Rough Image

## Objective
Turn the approved Stage 2 mass block into a coherent **specific rough / developed-pencil drawing** with specific form, anatomy, design, costume/gear, architecture, props, and working form/value logic while keeping the approved composition and masses intact and reserving finished-pencil cleanup for Stage 4.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Enter from an approved complete mass block.** Keep Stage 0 as the picture compass and Stage 2 as the immediate geometry authority. If a major visible element has no Stage 2 predecessor, return to Stage 2 rather than inventing it here.
2. **Register the exact approved mass block as the productive predecessor.** Apply `PAT_develop_scene_through_registered_successors`. Specific anatomy, design, architecture, props, and light must develop from those registered masses rather than replacing them with a fresh interpretation of the original request.
3. **Realize what the masses already contain.** Use `PAT_commit_stage3_form_realization` to resolve anatomy, architecture, mechanisms, clothing, props, terrain character, contours, internal planes, and other specific identities without migrating the blocks. When surface anatomy is the active figure decision, apply `PAT_design_surface_anatomy_as_microgesture_on_parent_forms`; when fabric/folds are the active figure decision, apply `PAT_design_drapery_from_tension_points_and_forces`. Both must inherit the accepted parent forms rather than compensate for unresolved structure.
4. **Search locally, not compositionally.** Creative invention is welcome inside unresolved form, design, material indication, light direction, and detail hierarchy. Camera, crop, subject scale/placement, dominant action, major silhouette arrangement, negative spaces, inventory, and focal hierarchy are no longer Search territory.
5. **Compile the productive image through the artifact-only handoff.** Use `AP_prepare_artifact_only_image_generation_handoff` and the Productive Image Contract below. Resolve specific form and working drawing logic without converting the result into finished pencils or a downstream medium.
6. **Inspect both immediate and root continuity.** Reduced or blurred, the rough must still be the same Stage 0 picture; structurally, every realized form must still sit on the Stage 2 mass solution.
7. **Reject attractive re-conception.** A more dramatic camera, enlarged hero, new architecture, new subject, new terrain feature, or polished cinematic replacement is a failed Stage 3 even when artistically stronger. Roll back to the owning stage if such a change is desired.
8. **Complete when the final can focus on finish rather than repair.** The rough should contain the specific forms, working light, edge logic, and detail hierarchy needed for a finish pass, with no unresolved structural invention hiding underneath.
9. **Use the Stage 3 handoff prompt.** End with explicit next actions such as: “Approve this rough realization to continue, tell me any Stage 3 revisions you want, or reject it and we'll return to the appropriate earlier stage.” Keep the revision language inside rough-realization territory rather than final polish.
### Productive Image Contract
- **Artifact form:** one full-frame developed drawing of the approved masses. No surrounding board, captions, inset examples, alternate versions, palette chips, or auxiliary panels.
- **Preserve:** camera, crop, action, major silhouette arrangement, subject placement/apparent scale, inventory, negative spaces, focal hierarchy, perspective, contacts, and all approved major masses.
- **Show:** specific anatomy, face construction/read, hair grouping, costume/armor design, architecture, mechanisms, props, contours, internal planes, major folds/overlaps, spear/prop handling, and selective working line/value indications where they clarify form and design.
- **Identity visibility:** established character design may now become specific—face read, hair grouping, major costume/armor design, emblem/seam placement, and equipment configuration—without redesigning the approved silhouette or loadout.
- **Withhold:** finished-pencil contour cleanup, final line hierarchy, pervasive hatching/texture, exhaustive rubble/masonry/road description, final edge control, tertiary micro-detail, and all downstream Ink/Color/Paint/Manga/B&W finish.
- **Global ceiling:** the image is specific and coherent but visibly leaves meaningful finish work unresolved.
- **Stop:** when Stage 4 can concentrate on pencil cleanup, contour hierarchy, edge control, and drawing integration rather than structural repair or new design invention.

## Notes
Stage 3 is the serious specific rough / developed-pencil pass. It may look compelling, but it should still visibly leave meaningful pencil cleanup, hierarchy, and integration for Stage 4 to do. **Beautiful but unauthorized remains a failure.**
