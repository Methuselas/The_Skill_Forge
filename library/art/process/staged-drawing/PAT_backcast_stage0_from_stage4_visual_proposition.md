# Backcast Stage 0 From the Intended Stage 4 Visual Proposition

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a Stage 0 composition is readable but its information ceiling or finished-pencil potential remains uncertain
**THEN** hold the design container fixed, dream the same image forward to its intended Stage 4 finished pencils, collapse that proposition to low-frequency grayscale masses, and repaint only the minimum Stage 0 marks that preserve the same silhouette, gesture, hierarchy, and spatial design
**ELSE** retain the approved Stage 0 and do not manufacture a denser intermediate image merely to prove that it can finish

## Do
- Freeze the proposed camera, crop, pose, major silhouettes, inventory, object placement, perspective, focal hierarchy, and broad value arrangement before dreaming forward.
- Imagine the full Stage 4 **finished-pencil drawing** inside that fixed container: resolved anatomy/design and intentional pencil expression appropriate to the intended drawing, with no important Drawing decisions left exploratory. Do not import downstream color, paint, ink, or material-finish decisions into the Drawing backcast. An explicitly authorized temporary scratch drawing may be used as evidence only and never becomes an approved successor stage.
- Reduce the finished proposition conceptually to three or four broad grayscale families. Compare low-frequency structure rather than literal pixels or brush marks.
- Rebuild Stage 0 with rough line/mass shorthand, broken contours or edges where useful, broad value grouping, and only strategic identifiers. A tiny eye mark, book bar, or prop notch is allowed only when it confirms presence without beginning description.
- Compare the two directions: the viewer should mentally reconstruct the Stage 4 finished-pencil drawing from Stage 0, and the simplified Stage 4 should recover the Stage 0 composition without relocating major forms.
- Inspect silhouette, principal diagonals, figure gesture, focal light, dominant dark mass, negative space, overlap, and broad depth separation.
- Revise the earliest failing class of information. Fix composition or value architecture before adding any descriptive detail.

## Don't
- Trace, blur, posterize, or desaturate a finished image and call the result Stage 0. The backcast must be repainted as intentional shorthand.
- Let the forward dream redesign the camera, pose, inventory, perspective, or major placement.
- Use surface detail, polished perspective, architectural decoration, clothing folds, anatomy, material response, or cinematic light to rescue an unclear thumbnail.
- Require pixel-perfect alignment. The invariant is the visual proposition, not identical local edges.
- Create a temporary Stage 4 image without explicit production authorization, or imply that a private hidden render was retained when no actual artifact exists.

## Checklist
- Stage 0 and the intended Stage 4 share the same camera, crop, pose, inventory, major placement, perspective, and focal hierarchy.
- Three or four broad value families carry the composition at reduced size.
- The figure gesture, main diagonal, focal light, dominant dark mass, and negative spaces survive the backcast.
- Every small line is necessary to identify a major form; removing decorative marks does not collapse the scene.
- The viewer can mentally see the finished pencil drawing through the thumbnail without relying on resolved anatomy, texture, or final line/value finish.
- Simplifying the intended Stage 4 recovers the Stage 0 design without moving or adding major forms.
- Any temporary Stage 4 scratch remains noncanonical and leaves the stage freeze unchanged.

## Notes
This is the Rosetta test: dream forward at full resolution and validate backward at low resolution. Stage 0 is not an unfinished final illustration. It is the complete visual proposition compressed into the fewest rough marks, masses, and broad value decisions capable of preserving it.
