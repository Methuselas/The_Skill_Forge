# Finish Stage 4 as Finished Pencils

## Objective
Complete the approved Stage 3 specific rough as a clean, fully resolved, production-ready **finished pencil drawing** while preserving every accepted picture, structural, mass, and design decision upstream. Stage 4 closes the universal Drawing AP; Ink, Color, Paint, Manga/B&W finish, and other medium-specific crafts are separate downstream workflows.

## Steps / Flow
1. **Enter only from an approved Stage 3 rough.** Keep the approved Stage 0 picture root and the exact approved Stage 3 artifact as authorities. If anatomy, pose, perspective, inventory, or design still needs redesign, return upstream instead of hiding the problem with pencil finish.
2. **Confirm exact predecessor accessibility before generation.** Apply `PAT_develop_scene_through_registered_successors`. Canonical identity and edit-target availability are separate truths. If the requested operation requires editing/reference continuity and the exact approved Stage 3 artifact is not actually available to the native image tool, fail closed and recover or request a re-upload of that exact artifact. Do not reconstruct it from prose or substitute a near-match.
3. **Finish the registered drawing in place.** Use `PAT_preserve_structure_during_stage4_pencil_finish` plus `AP_prepare_artifact_only_image_generation_handoff`. Remove obsolete construction marks, clean exploratory lines, resolve contours, clarify anatomy/clothing/gear already established, and integrate the drawing without changing the accepted solution.
4. **Resolve focal drawing information first.** Tighten hands, feet, face/head construction, prop contacts, silhouette-critical edges, and other functional endpoints before spending detail on secondary texture.
5. **Establish deliberate pencil hierarchy.** Strengthen important contours, suppress secondary/tertiary contours, organize edge relationships, and use controlled pencil hatching/value only where it clarifies form, overlap, depth, or focal hierarchy. Do not spread equal line noise across the frame.
6. **Clean environment and props without redesign.** Resolve architecture, wagon/vehicle forms, rubble, terrain, stairs, doors, mechanisms, and other accepted objects as finished drawing shapes. Keep distant/subordinate regions quieter than the focal region.
7. **Inspect the finished drawing against both anchors.** Reduced to Stage 3 information, the result must recover the accepted rough; reduced further, it must still recover the Stage 0 picture. Recheck camera, crop, figure poses, spear/prop axes, contacts, major overlaps, negative spaces, perspective, and scene inventory.
8. **Stop at finished pencils.** Do not introduce ink treatment, color scripting, local color, painted lighting, final materials, painterly surface rendering, manga tones, post-processing, or another medium. If the user wants a downstream craft, route it to a separate AP without reopening the approved Drawing lockset.
9. **Close the Drawing thread.** A successful Stage 4 is the final artifact of universal Drawing. Final revisions that remain pencil-only may revise Stage 4 when the exact canonical predecessor is accessible; downstream craft begins only by separate instruction.

### Productive Image Contract
- **Artifact form:** one full-frame finished pencil drawing only. No process board, alternate panel, color version, inked version, or downstream-medium preview.
- **Preserve:** camera, crop, framing, perspective, composition, pose/action, subject scale/placement, major silhouettes, negative spaces, scene inventory, object placement, path geometry, contacts, overlaps, major proportions, and all approved Stage 3 design specificity.
- **Allowed change budget:** pencil cleanup; final contour decisions; line-weight hierarchy; selective hatching/value; clarified anatomy already present; clarified clothing/gear; hand/foot/prop-contact resolution; final fold/overlap cleanup; architecture/prop contour resolution; tangency cleanup; selective edge suppression or reinforcement; scene-wide pencil integration.
- **Forbidden downstream finish:** no color, painted local color, colored light, material-color rendering, painterly texture, ink treatment, comic/manga inking, screen tones, post-processing, or another craft's presentation effects.
- **Hierarchy:** highest resolution belongs to the focal figure and functional contacts; distant and secondary regions remain intentionally quieter.
- **Stop:** when the exact Stage 3 rough has been completed as clean, coherent, production-ready finished pencils and further work would begin a different medium or add nonessential pencil polish.

## Notes
Stage 4 is the terminal operation of the universal Drawing thread: **Finished Pencils**. Downstream Ink, Color, Paint, Manga/B&W finish, and other medium-specific workflows inherit the completed Drawing lockset without redefining Stage 4.

The Broken Gate canonical Stage 4 reference is the **first accepted finished-pencil artifact**. Its slightly soft/uniformly busy finish is an accepted minor limitation. The later tightening reconstruction is explicitly negative debugging evidence and must not replace it.
