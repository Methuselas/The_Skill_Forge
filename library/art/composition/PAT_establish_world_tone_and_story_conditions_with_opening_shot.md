# Establish World Tone And Story Conditions With Opening Shot

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an opening image must establish both where the story is and what kind of emotional world the audience has entered
**THEN** Use the opening or establishing image to define not only location but the emotional rules, scale, order, threat, and environmental character of the story world

## Do
- Decide what the audience should assume about the world after the first shot.
- Encode those assumptions through scale, value, space, weather, architecture, activity, and figure/environment relationship.

## Don't
- Do not treat an establishing shot as neutral geographic proof when it can carry story conditions.

## Checklist
- The audience can infer both where they are and what kind of situation/world they have entered.
