# Treat Environment As Narrative Character

## Pattern Rule
**IF** the setting needs to influence story meaning rather than function as neutral background
**THEN** Design the environment so its shapes, depth, openings, silhouettes, values, and rhythm actively express the scene rather than serving as neutral scenery

## Do
- State what the environment should communicate about the moment.
- Choose environmental masses and spatial behavior that support that statement.
- Let architecture or landscape pressure, shelter, expose, dwarf, or guide the characters when useful.

## Don't
- Do not decorate the background independently of the scene’s emotional job.

## Checklist
- The setting contributes story information even without character dialogue.
