# Escalate Sequence Intensity Through Progressive Compositional Contrast

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a sequence must grow in pressure or intensity instead of repeating one compositional level
**THEN** Build a sequence from a readable baseline toward progressively stronger framing, proximity, diagonals, depth, distortion, scale, value contrast, or spatial pressure

## Do
- Establish the calm or normal baseline first.
- Increase only the variables that support the rising dramatic beat.
- Reserve the strongest departure for the climax or point of no return.

## Don't
- Do not begin every shot at maximum intensity.
- Do not escalate every variable equally if one clear progression is stronger.

## Checklist
- Each successive beat feels intentionally stronger or more pressured than the previous one.

## Notes

Leave measurable headroom in the opening arrangement: if proximity, tilt, overlap, and value contrast all begin near their extremes, later shots can only repeat or become illegible. After the peak, a controlled reduction can make the climax register more clearly than continued escalation.
