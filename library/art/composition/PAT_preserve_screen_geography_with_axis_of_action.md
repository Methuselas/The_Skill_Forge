# Preserve Screen Geography With Axis Of Action

## Pattern Rule
**IF** successive viewpoints could reverse left-right relationships or confuse an ongoing interaction
**THEN** Maintain consistent left/right and interaction relationships by normally keeping successive viewpoints on one side of an established action axis

## Do
- Identify the primary line between interacting subjects or along movement.
- Keep cameras on one side when continuity should feel effortless.
- When crossing is necessary, show the crossing in continuous movement or use an on-axis neutral bridge.

## Don't
- Do not cross the axis invisibly and then expect the audience to recover orientation.
- Do not preserve the rule when deliberate disorientation is the story goal.

## Checklist
- Screen direction and character relationships remain legible across cuts.
