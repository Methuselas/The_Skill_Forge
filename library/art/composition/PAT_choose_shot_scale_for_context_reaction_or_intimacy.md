# Choose Shot Scale For Context Reaction Or Intimacy

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a story beat requires a framing choice between environmental context, readable reaction, and intimate detail
**THEN** Choose framing by deciding how much relationship between subject and environment the audience needs at that moment

## Do
- Use wide framing when circumstances and environment carry essential information.
- Use medium framing when subject action matters but context still supports it.
- Use close or extreme close framing when reaction, feature, or intimate detail should overtake context.

## Don't
- Do not choose shot size only because it looks cinematic.
- Do not remove context that the audience still needs to understand the beat.

## Checklist
- The chosen scale emphasizes the intended information relationship.
- The crop does not destroy required action or geography.
