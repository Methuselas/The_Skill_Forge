# Use Negative Space As Active Story Shape

## Pattern Rule
**IF** the empty intervals around or between subjects affect the action read, relationship, or focal hierarchy
**THEN** Shape empty or dark space deliberately so it isolates, pressures, conceals, exposes, suspends, or otherwise contributes to the story

## Do
- Design negative shapes with the same care as positive subjects.
- Use emptiness to control vulnerability, suspense, scale, or breathing room.
- Check how the negative space changes when the focal subject moves.

## Don't
- Do not treat unused space as accidental leftover canvas.

## Checklist
- The negative space has a clear compositional job.
- Removing or filling it would measurably change the story read.
