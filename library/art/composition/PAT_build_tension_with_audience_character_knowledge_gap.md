# Build Tension With Audience Character Knowledge Gap

## Pattern Rule
**IF** suspense depends on controlling whether the audience and a character know the same story information
**THEN** Create anticipation by letting the audience know a relevant threat, opportunity, or fact that a character does not, or withhold it when shared ignorance is the intended experience

## Do
- State what the audience knows and what the character knows.
- Intercut or reveal information only when the knowledge gap increases the intended tension.
- Close the gap when the story beat calls for recognition or consequence.

## Don't
- Do not give the audience privileged information that weakens a mystery meant to be shared with the character.

## Checklist
- The knowledge difference has a clear dramatic effect.

## Notes

Suspense requires the privileged fact to remain active in the audience's mind while the character acts without it. If intervening shots make the threat, opportunity, or consequence hard to remember, re-establish it visually before asking the gap to carry tension.
