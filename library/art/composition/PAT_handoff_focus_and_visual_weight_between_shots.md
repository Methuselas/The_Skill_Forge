# Handoff Focus And Visual Weight Between Shots

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a cut risks making the viewer search for the next focal point or misread the direction of visual emphasis
**THEN** Coordinate outgoing and incoming frames so focal position, dominant mass, direction, or visual weight changes intentionally across the cut

## Do
- Compare the last readable state of one shot with the first of the next.
- Preserve or shift focal position deliberately.
- Use a shift when it clarifies character identity or prevents accidental visual substitution.

## Don't
- Do not treat every new shot as a compositional reset.

## Checklist
- The eye has a clear next destination after the cut.

## Notes

Check the cut as a two-frame pair with all dialogue and motion removed. A near-identical focal position can create an unintended identity match, while a large jump can force a search; choose the displacement according to whether continuity or reorientation serves the beat.
