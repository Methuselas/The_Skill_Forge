# Bridge Spatial Transitions With Shared Geographic Anchor

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a cut or transition changes location and the audience may lose the connection between the two spaces
**THEN** Carry a character, prop, doorway, landmark, vehicle, or other shared anchor across a location change so the audience can connect the spaces

## Do
- Choose an anchor already understood in the first setup.
- Keep it visible or causally linked through the transition.
- Introduce the new framing after the connection is readable.

## Don't
- Do not make a radical spatial jump with no recoverable geographic evidence unless confusion is intended.

## Checklist
- The audience can infer how the new space relates to the old one.
