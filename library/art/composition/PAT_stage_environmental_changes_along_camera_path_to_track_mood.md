# Stage Environmental Changes Along Camera Path To Track Mood

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a continuous camera path crosses several spaces and the environment must support an evolving emotional beat
**THEN** Use successive regions of a continuous camera path to change environmental mood in step with the scene’s emotional development

## Do
- Plan how openness, light, clutter, architecture, background activity, and depth evolve along the path.
- Keep the principal action coherent while the setting carries successive mood beats.

## Don't
- Do not change environment arbitrarily when the move is supposed to feel continuous.

## Checklist
- Spatial progression and emotional progression reinforce each other.
