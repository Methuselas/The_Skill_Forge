# Preserve Visual Tone And World Consistency Across Sequence

## Pattern Rule
**IF** successive images need local variation without breaking the established visual and emotional world
**THEN** Keep successive frames coherent with the established emotional and visual contract while allowing local intensity to change

## Do
- Establish the sequence baseline in lighting, shape language, atmosphere, rhythm, and overall visual behavior.
- Let later frames vary only in ways that still feel native to the same story world.
- Use deliberate contrast as a story event rather than accidental style drift.

## Don't
- Do not make every frame visually identical.
- Do not let a locally attractive treatment break the established world without narrative cause.

## Checklist
- Local changes are readable as intentional development.
- The sequence still feels like one world and one visual language.
