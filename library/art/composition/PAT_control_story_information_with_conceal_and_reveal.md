# Control Story Information With Conceal And Reveal

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a beat depends on exactly when the audience learns an identity, expression, object, or spatial relationship
**THEN** Withhold or disclose faces, objects, identities, expressions, or spatial relationships according to what the audience should know at each beat

## Do
- Use crop, occlusion, silhouette, darkness, backlight, viewpoint, or staging to withhold information.
- Reveal only enough to orient the audience until the intended question is ready to resolve.
- Time the reveal so it changes the meaning or tension of the shot.

## Don't
- Do not hide essential orientation accidentally.
- Do not reveal a mystery early merely because the asset is available.

## Checklist
- The audience knows exactly as much as the beat requires.
- The reveal has a perceptible narrative consequence.
