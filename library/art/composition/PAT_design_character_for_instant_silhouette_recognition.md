# Design Character For Instant Silhouette Recognition

## Pattern Rule
**IF** a character must remain identifiable when internal detail, lighting, or pose information is reduced
**THEN** Design a character so identity survives simplified silhouette, lighting changes, pose changes, and reduced detail

## Do
- Differentiate major head/body contour, proportions, and dominant shape before relying on small features.
- Test the design as a filled silhouette at several poses and scales.
- Restore detail only after the large identity remains distinct.

## Don't
- Do not make identity depend on tiny costume marks or color alone.

## Checklist
- The character remains recognizable in simplified silhouette.
