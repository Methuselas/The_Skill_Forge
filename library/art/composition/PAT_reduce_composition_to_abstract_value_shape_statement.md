# Reduce Composition To Abstract Value Shape Statement

## Pattern Rule
**IF** subject detail is obscuring whether the composition works as a simple arrangement of dominant masses
**THEN** Reduce the intended image to a small set of dominant light/dark masses and directional shapes before solving subject detail

## Do
- Test whether the abstract mass pattern already communicates stability, tension, isolation, dominance, or another intended effect.
- Repair the large shape statement before adding anatomy, props, texture, or rendering.
- Preserve the successful mass hierarchy as detail returns.

## Don't
- Do not use detail to rescue an unreadable large-scale value design.

## Checklist
- The frame reads at thumbnail size.
- The primary value masses support the intended story effect.

## Notes

Blur, squint, or shrink the study until small subject distinctions disappear. If a detail is essential to the story, absorb it into a dominant mass or protect it as a deliberate accent rather than letting many local contrasts dissolve the large statement.
