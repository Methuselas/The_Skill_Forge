# Pose Out Approved Storyboard Action Into Layout Without Reauthoring Scene

## Pattern Rule
**IF** an approved storyboard shot must become an animation production layout
**THEN** redraw the character action so it fits the final perspective, scale, environment, model, and staging while preserving the storyboard's intended action, position, attitude, and dramatic function.

## Do
- Start from the approved storyboard pose and identify what must remain invariant: action, attitude, screen relationship, story beat, and major position.
- Reconstruct the character inside the layout's actual perspective field and scale rather than pasting the storyboard drawing into the finished environment.
- Keep the pose on model enough to provide a trustworthy spatial and acting target for Animation.
- Strengthen a weak silhouette, balance, contact, or perspective relationship when the change preserves the approved dramatic intent.
- Make the action definitive enough that downstream animators can understand what the body is doing without requiring finished rendering.
- Route major changes in action, staging, or position back through the storyboard/directing authority instead of silently rewriting the shot in Layout.

## Don't
- Do not treat Layout as permission to reauthor a shot that already works.
- Do not preserve a rough storyboard distortion when it breaks the final perspective or model.
- Do not over-detail the character when clear construction, action, and attitude are sufficient for handoff.
- Do not move the character to a materially different story position merely to make the layout easier.

## Checklist
- The final layout pose still communicates the approved storyboard beat.
- Character scale and perspective agree with the environment.
- Action and attitude are clear enough for Animation to use.
- Any strengthening preserves rather than replaces the approved staging.
- Material changes have been routed back for approval rather than hidden in the layout.

## Notes
“Posing-out” develops the storyboard pose into a spatially executable production drawing without granting the layout artist final authorship over the scene. The portable boundary is storyboard intent first, layout integration second.
