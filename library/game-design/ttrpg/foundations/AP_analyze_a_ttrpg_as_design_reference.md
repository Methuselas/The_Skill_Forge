# Analyze a TTRPG as Design Reference
## Objective
Study a TTRPG closely enough to extract a transferable design lesson rather than copying its surface features.
## Steps / Flow
1. Name the game’s apparent audience and promised experience.
2. During play or reading, use **Separate TTRPG Design Friction from Personal Preference** to record lookups, disputes, timing, and player choices.
3. Examine how rules, voice, difficulty, character investment, world detail, and layout support or contradict that promise; apply **Align TTRPG Rules Voice Difficulty and Character Investment** for stakes and commitment.
4. Separate an observable cause from a personal preference. If the cause is uncertain, retain it as a question rather than a lesson.
5. Complete the analysis with one transferable decision and the context where it applies.
## Notes
Reference analysis is comparative study, not a license to reproduce a game's protected expression or assume its audience wants every feature.
