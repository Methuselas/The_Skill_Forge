# Align TTRPG Rules Voice Difficulty and Character Investment
## Pattern Rule
**IF** setting a TTRPG's rules weight and stakes
**THEN** make its voice, explanation, character-creation burden, and chance of loss support the same player promise.
## Do
- Make complex rules especially clear and easy to navigate.
- Keep replacement fast when characters can die or fail abruptly.
## Don't
- Demand extensive character investment before exposing players to arbitrary early loss.
## Checklist
- The cost of creating a character matches its expected durability.
- The rulebook's voice does not obscure consequential rules.
## Notes
Players accept disappointing outcomes more readily when the game makes its stakes and procedures legible.
