# Separate TTRPG Design Friction from Personal Preference
## Pattern Rule
**IF** a game feels slow, confusing, or unenjoyable during play
**THEN** record the response and identify its observable cause before treating it as a design fault.
## Do
- Track rule lookups, disagreements, and long turns separately from reactions to genre or rules weight.
- Ask whether the friction comes from unclear presentation, table preparation, or a deliberate mechanical tradeoff.
## Don't
- Call a game badly designed merely because its intended style is not yours.
## Checklist
- The observation distinguishes what happened from the explanation.
- A proposed lesson names the player experience it would change.
## Notes
Detailed rules can be an intentional promise; unclear rules and needless searching are not the same thing.
