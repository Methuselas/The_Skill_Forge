# Hybridize TTRPG Genres Around a Single Player Promise
## Pattern Rule
**IF** combining genre elements in a TTRPG
**THEN** make every borrowed element reinforce one coherent kind of play and give its unusual coexistence an internal explanation.
## Do
- Identify which feature from each genre players must actually use during play.
- Preserve the defining pressures of each selected genre instead of treating them as decorative names.
- Establish why extraordinary elements coexist, even when players discover that explanation later.
## Don't
- Add magic, technology, or horror imagery that makes the game’s central genre feature irrelevant.
## Checklist
- The genre blend predicts a distinctive repeatable player situation.
- The setting can explain its exceptional combinations without contradiction.
## Notes
Hybrid games work when their elements create a new playable tension. A label such as fantasy science fiction is not enough unless both parts change the choices players make.
