# Balance Fantasy Magic by Access Risk and Cost
## Pattern Rule
**IF** magic can materially change a fantasy TTRPG’s outcomes
**THEN** control its influence through deliberate access, risk, advancement, or resource costs that fit the game’s tone.
## Do
- Decide who can use magic and what its use lets them change.
- Limit frequency, power, or availability when spells would otherwise erase ordinary challenges.
- Use danger or personal cost when unpredictability and vulnerability are part of the intended experience.
## Don't
- Give magic a dramatic price in the setting but let it operate as a consequence-free solution in play.
## Checklist
- Magical power has an understandable route of access and constraint.
- Its limits preserve meaningful choices for magical and nonmagical characters.
## Notes
The appropriate constraint depends on the promised fantasy: reliable heroic magic can grow through advancement, while grim or hazardous magic can exact a visible toll. The important decision is that the rule treatment matches the fiction.
