# Make TTRPG Genre Promises Legible
## Pattern Rule
**IF** choosing a TTRPG genre or genre blend
**THEN** state the experience players should expect and make its tone, stakes, content, and recurring play situations support that promise.
## Do
- Use genre conventions to set expectations about heroism, danger, morality, and the kinds of choices players will face.
- Tell players in advance when mature content, vulnerability, or sudden character loss materially changes the expected experience.
## Don't
- Use a surprise twist to conceal a sustained genre shift that changes the game players agreed to play.
## Checklist
- A prospective player can describe the game’s emotional and dramatic promise.
- The stated genre predicts the game’s ordinary challenges and stakes.
## Notes
Genre is a player-facing contract as well as an inspiration source. Familiar labels can help a game stand out only when its actual play fulfills or deliberately and clearly reframes their promise.
