# Choose a TTRPG Economy Model by Play Relevance
## Pattern Rule
**IF** deciding how a TTRPG represents money and wealth
**THEN** choose the simplest model that makes the purchases, scarcity, and status relevant to play understandable.
## Do
- Use precise prices when counting resources and trade are central challenges.
- Use broad wealth levels when relative means matter more than accounting.
## Don't
- Invent currencies, exchange rates, and prices that players cannot use in a decision.
## Checklist
- Players can tell whether their characters can obtain a relevant good or service.
- The model reflects the setting’s important economic pressures.
## Notes
Currency can establish a world’s texture, but it remains a game tool. A renamed familiar model is often more playable than a novel system with no useful consequences.
