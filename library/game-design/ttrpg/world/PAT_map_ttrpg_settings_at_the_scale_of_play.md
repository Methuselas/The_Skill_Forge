# Map TTRPG Settings at the Scale of Play
## Pattern Rule
**IF** preparing a map for a TTRPG setting
**THEN** begin with the settlement, route, or region where player characters can act and expand only when wider geography changes play.
## Do
- Mark places that create decisions: authority, trade, worship, lodging, danger, departure, and arrival.
- Let geography explain travel, resources, and the location of population centers.
## Don't
- Delay a playable starting setting until an entire continent is complete.
## Checklist
- The map helps players choose where to go or what to investigate next.
- Important travel routes and local points of interest are legible.
## Notes
Maps are working play aids before they are finished illustrations. A rough local map can support an adventure better than a polished world map with nowhere actionable to begin.
