# Art Pressure Batch 002 Report

Status: complete — **PASS WITH BOUNDARIES CONFIRMED**

Batch 002 tested five high-centrality Patterns governing head and hand structure, surface anatomy, tonal consolidation, and material response. It reused five credited local issues across distinct artists and visual treatments. No comic page or contact sheet was added to the repository.

## Result

All five Patterns survived the required positive, negative, boundary, adversarial, human-evidence, and competing-owner cases. No canonical mutation was justified.

The key boundaries were:

- caricature may radically reshape a face while the facial mass still belongs to one oriented cranium;
- closed fists and low-resolution hands may hide topology, but visible evidence must not contradict the established hand graph;
- anatomical symmetry is valid when the pose/load warrants it; copied symmetry is the failure;
- flat graphic darks can consolidate form without continuous modeling, and line-led work may legitimately omit tone;
- comic material shorthand may simplify secondary reflections, but the retained cues must still describe the host form and material class.
