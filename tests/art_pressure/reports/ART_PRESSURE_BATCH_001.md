# Art Pressure Batch 001 Report

Status: complete — **PASS WITH BOUNDARIES CONFIRMED**

Batch 001 tested six high-centrality Patterns for foundational action readability against five locally held comic fixtures by Frank Miller/Klaus Janson/Lynn Varley, John Byrne/Terry Austin, Erik Larsen, Whilce Portacio/Scott Williams, and Marc Silvestri/Scott Williams. Credits were checked in the issues or collected-edition credit matter. No copyrighted page was added to the repository.

## Result

All six Patterns survived positive, negative, boundary, adversarial, human-evidence, and competing-owner cases. No canonical mutation was justified.

The strongest empirical boundaries were:

- extreme foreshortening may compress visible masses without breaking mass orientation or limb identity;
- crop and overlap may hide a chain segment but do not make its structural ownership optional;
- speed lines and diagonals reinforce action but do not prove causal force or balance;
- airborne, falling, vehicle-supported, and externally supported figures must be judged against their real support or momentum state, not a standing plumb;
- crowded action can form a coherent whole-picture pattern without clean isolated silhouettes;
- a dramatic camera succeeds only when it strengthens a named effect and preserves the key beat.

## Scope limitation

Finished pages validated outcome-facing Pattern behavior only. They were not treated as evidence for a particular blocking process, Drill pedagogy, or AP ordering. The Variants attached to these Patterns remain separately accountable in the master manifest unless directly tested by a later batch.

## Canon action

None. The six cards already contain the exceptions and ownership distinctions exposed by this fixture set.
